## Motion (measured values from the style decode)
- Number card: footage fades to white 0.5 s, the script writes on left to right in about 1.2 s and holds, fades out 0.4 s.
- Panels on caramel: each grows from its top edge in 0.35 s, one per spoken item.
- Between clips: hard cuts and 0.3 s dissolves.
- No zooms, shakes, glitches, emoji or sound effects.
