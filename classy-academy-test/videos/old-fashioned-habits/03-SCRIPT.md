## 1. They press before they dress

`0:55.73` Number one. They press before they dress.  
`0:59.55` A wrinkled shirt looks careless, even when it cost a fortune.  
`1:04.35` A pressed one looks considered, even when it didn't.  
`1:08.26` You don't need to iron everything. A handheld steamer does most of the work in a few minutes.  
`1:14.75` Silk and wool prefer a lower heat, so a quick look at the care label comes first.  
`1:20.83` And on a busy morning, press only what the eye sees first. The collar, the cuffs, and the front of the shirt.  
`1:28.65` Then hang the piece straight away, so the work isn't undone in a drawer.  
`1:33.64` Five minutes with an iron can do more for an outfit than a new purchase.  

## 2. They hang it up the moment they take it off

`1:39.84` Number two. They hang it up the moment they take it off.  
`1:44.45` Clothes left on a chair overnight wake up creased and tired.  
`1:48.95` So the habit is simple. Jackets and coats go on a shaped hanger, so the shoulders keep their line.  
`1:56.27` Knitwear is folded, not hung, because a hanger slowly stretches a sweater out of shape.  
`2:03.00` And thin wire hangers are best avoided. Over time, they leave little bumps in the shoulders.  
`2:10.27` And anything you wore today gets a night of fresh air before it goes back into the wardrobe.  
`2:17.26` A wardrobe treated this way looks after your clothes for you.  

## 3. They mend things right away

`2:22.35` Number three. They mend things right away.  
`2:26.25` A loose button. A fallen hem. A small tear in a lining. On their own, they're tiny.  
`2:32.85` But the eye finds them, and suddenly the whole outfit looks neglected.  
`2:38.20` The fix usually takes less time than choosing a different outfit.  
`2:43.17` The old films showed it plainly. A small sewing box, and the repair done that same evening.  
`2:50.59` Two needles, black, white and beige thread, and a few spare buttons are all you need.  
`2:56.77` And for anything bigger, a good tailor is one of the best-kept secrets of well-dressed women.  

## 4. They brush their coats

`3:04.64` Number four. They brush their coats.  
`3:08.12` Dust and lint make a dark wool coat look dull, faster than you would think.  
`3:13.72` A soft clothes brush, used after wearing, lifts away the surface dirt before it can settle in.  
`3:21.05` Think of a navy coat at the end of winter. Brushed, it still looks deep and dark. Neglected, it looks grey and dusty.  
`3:30.10` Brush gently, along the length of the fabric, and a tired coat looks rich again.  
`3:36.40` Keep a lint roller by the door for the last minute. But let the brush do the real care.  

## 5. They look after their shoes

`3:43.62` Number five. They look after their shoes.  
`3:47.37` Shoes are often the first thing to give an outfit away.  
`3:51.20` Wipe them after wearing, and polish leather regularly.  
`3:55.44` Cedar shoe trees help a pair keep its shape, and dry out between wears.  
`4:00.81` Giving a pair a day off between wears lets the leather rest, and they last far longer.  
`4:07.23` And when you hear a heel tip clicking, take them to a cobbler. A new heel costs far less than a new pair.  
`4:15.13` Cared-for shoes make even simple clothes look intentional.  

## 6. They prepare the night before

`4:20.43` Number six. They prepare the night before.  
`4:24.34` In the old grooming films, the evening was when the real work happened.  
`4:29.65` Tomorrow's clothes were chosen, pressed and laid out. Nails were tidied. Hair was set before bed.  
`4:37.43` Today, it can be just as simple. Choose tomorrow's outfit, check it for a loose thread or a mark, and hang it ready.  
`4:46.17` A loose braid, or a soft scarf at night, and your hair starts the morning with shape.  
`4:52.18` It was never about perfection. It was about not leaving the important things to the last, rushed minute.  
`4:59.85` The morning becomes calm, because every decision was already made.
