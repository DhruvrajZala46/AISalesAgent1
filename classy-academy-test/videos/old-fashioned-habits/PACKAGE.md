Have you ever noticed how some women make a simple coat look expensive? Not because it cost more, but because of everything that happened to it before she put it on.

In this video, we walk through seven habits that were once taught to every young woman: pressing what the eye sees first, hanging and folding with care, mending a loose button the same evening, brushing a wool coat, keeping shoes polished and in shape, preparing tomorrow's outfit the night before, and taking one last look in the mirror before you leave. None of them needs a bigger wardrobe.

Elegance was never about having more. It's about caring for what you already have.
