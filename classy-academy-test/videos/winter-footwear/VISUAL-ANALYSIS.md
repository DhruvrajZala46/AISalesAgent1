## 2. How this video follows the channel's winter format

Every rule in the script has the same four beats, and each beat gets the channel layout that fits it:

| Script beat | On screen |
|---|---|
| "Number one: the heel check." | the white card, the rule's name in black script (`card_at`) |
| the problem ("a heel tip worn down to the metal", "wet pavement and grit") | the literal moment in a row of 2-3 clips, one per thing named (`lrow`), or one clip with the line beside it |
| "Instead, ..." (the fix) | a clip of the fix being done (the cobbler, shoe trees going in, flat laces) with the **product cut out on the caramel** beside it as it is named (`lclip` + `item` / `pieces`) |
| "The test: ..." | a clip of the test itself (looking at the heel from behind in a mirror, tilting a boot to the window) with the test written in white beside it, line by line (`lclip` + `qstack`) |

- **Named items** each get their own picture, appearing on the word:
  - shoe trees, heel liners, flat laces, rubber heel tips, polish, cleaner, insoles;
  - the three forgiving colours (deep brown, burgundy, black);
  - the four trend details (croc, buckles, sock shafts, cowboy edges).
- **The guide:** "Check The Description For / Personal Stylist Guide" over a full clip after rule four and at the end.
  No Classy Closet and no website.
- **Footage:**
  - Pinterest video pins of Instagram and TikTok creators;
  - every clip checked for AI (watermark scan, metadata, eye), captions, ads and duplicates;
  - none repeated from the eight earlier videos (footage hash of every used clip, plus every used pin image);
  - product photos are Pinterest originals cut out onto the caramel.
- **Framing:** vertical clips shown whole, over their own blurred copy or on the caramel; no zoom crops.
- **Sound:** the same soft piano about 12 dB under the voice; no sound effects.
- **The CE logo** in the corner and on the white end card.
