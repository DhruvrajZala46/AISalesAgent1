### A3. What the 38 share (counted)

| Pattern | Count | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Four words or fewer (7 have none) | 24 of 38 | read in under a second, on a phone |
| A warning: cheap, never, stop, wrong, a red X | 16 of 38 | fear of being judged is stronger than the wish to look good; the viewer checks "do I own this?" |
| The creator's own face, close, eyes on the lens | 15 of 38 | a face carries the emotion; it works because subscribers know that face |
| Two states side by side: before / after, wrong / right, cheap / expensive | 10 of 38 | the proof is in the picture; the only open question is "how?" |
| A red X, a tick, or red / green tags | 10 of 38 | a red X or a green tick is understood before any word is read |
| A light, bright background (judged by eye; 10 are dark) | 28 of 38 | the objects stay clear at 168 px wide |

### A4. Which formula travels furthest beyond the channel's own size

Views divided by the channel's subscribers (median of each group):

| Formula | Videos | Median views per subscriber | Best |
|---|---|---|---|
| B before / after | 6 | 8.4x | 97.4x (LS Maker, 24k subscribers, 2.35M views) |

| D hacks collage | 7 | 5.0x | 43.8x (KWSHOPPED, 41k subscribers, 1.78M views) |
| E photos + clean words | 6 | 3.2x | 10.3x (Asia Jackson) |
| A creator's face + items | 15 | 1.0x | 7.4x (Mademoiselle Spends) |

- The face formula is the most common, but it is the big channels' formula: its videos reach about as many views as the
  channel has subscribers. Anna Bey's best is 4.8x.
- The videos that went far beyond a small channel's size are object-led: before / after, wrong / right, hacks.
- The Classy Elegance is small and faceless, so v2 uses the object-led formulas and the lane's proven words.
- All 15 faces are the creator's own. A borrowed celebrity face (v1 A, Olivia Palermo) is not that lever: nobody
  subscribes to her on this channel, and it suggests she presents the video. v2 has no borrowed face.

**Checks**
- Beside the originals (`v2_vs_million.jpg`): the same structure, tags and weights.
- At phone size, 168 x 94, and in black and white (`v2_phone.jpg`): all three read; the tags and the X carry without colour.
- The bottom-right corner is kept clear for YouTube's time stamp.
- The before / after halves got the same colour grade; nothing was retouched to widen the difference.
- Title lint (`yt-package`): no thumbnail word repeats the title.

**Title (the thumbnail and the title are one unit)**
- The working title scores 62/100: 73 characters (search cuts near 60) and no number.
- Suggested, in the script's own words: **"8 Winter Boot Rules That Instantly Elevate Every Outfit"** (55 characters, 98/100).

**Honest limits**
- No thumbnail can promise views. The channel has about 226 subscribers; these raise the chance of the click, they do
  not guarantee it.
- YouTube's Test & compare picks the winner by watch time, so a thumbnail must match the video. Upload all three.
- Thumbnail 1 shows a result of care; the video teaches the care rules, not a step-by-step restoration. Keep the title on
  "rules", not "how to restore".
- "LOOKS CHEAP!" is stronger than the script's own words ("tired", "careless", "busy"). "LOOKS TIRED" is a one-word swap
  if the creator wants the channel's kinder voice; it is not a proven phrase.
- LS Maker's thumbnail looks rendered; only its layout was studied. Ours are real photos.
