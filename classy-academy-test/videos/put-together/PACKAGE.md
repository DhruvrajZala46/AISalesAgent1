## Thumbnails (YouTube Studio -> Thumbnail -> Test & compare)

The channel's measured layout (`tools/thumb_make.py`): 3 real women edge to edge, Cormorant top line,
Montserrat headline, red underline, small italic line, CE logo. Words never repeat the title
(`title.py --thumb`: 100/100 for T1 and T3).

1. **Effortlessly / Polished / \*on any budget\*** (recommended). "Polished" is the word the niche
   winners use ("Always look polished" 16.1x, "Polished in minutes" 7.2x), and the small line adds
   the budget promise that won 75.6x and that our comments ask about most (39 comments, 455 likes).
2. **Elegant Ladies / Do This Daily / \*almost free\***: the channel's own "Elegant Ladies ..." shape.
3. **Effortlessly / Polished / \*at any age\***: women in their 40s, 60s and 70s.

Photos: real women only. They are frames from the video's own clips, so every one is real footage,
already AI-scanned (every one scores 0.00) and credited. A Pinterest photo search was tried first,
but nearly all "outfit" photos were AI (sewmamasew, zentrosy, trevalix, fabbon) and were rejected.
- T1: @gedsadawan, @fashioninspiration21, @luxeofblue
- T2: @itsjazifrizzle, @viktoriyasandyu, @devaronipepperoni
- T3: @marguimz, @jeannecaldarera, @graziaheartsgiorgio

Feed test: the three at 320x180 next to six niche winners on the same topic. The headline reads at
a glance and the faces stay clear. Ours are calmer and more classic than the competitors, which
is the channel's identity.
