## How the channel's teaching is used
- **Every point** follows the 7 steps in `voice.md`:
  1. name it
  2. validate ("We get it", "We've all been there")
  3. why it matters to her
  4. a principle line ("Elegance isn't only worn. It's carried.")
  5. what elegant women do
  6. the how-to ("Try this…") with named pieces and colours
  7. the inner payoff (calm, ease, intention, confidence, self-worth)
- **Nothing is quoted, no one is named, no culture is reported.** The research shaped the tips;
  the words are the channel's own.
- **Red lines respected:**
  - no colour is banned (point 2 keeps every colour she loves as an accent)
  - nothing shames her
  - no statistics
  - no rich-versus-poor framing
- **Comment drivers** (like the "what's in your bag" question that filled the grooming video's
  comments):
  - point 1 asks "What's your go-to outfit?"
  - the close asks "Which one will you try first?" plus the part-two ask
