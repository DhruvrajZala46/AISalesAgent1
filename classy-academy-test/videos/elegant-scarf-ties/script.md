## 1. The Parisian knot
[ON SCREEN: white card, black script: "1. The Parisian knot"]
**Let's start with a knot that has stood the test of time. The Parisian knot.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished knot on a tailored camel coat; then on a casual jacket]
**What's amazing about this knot is its versatility. It works with almost any scarf, and it looks just as right with a tailored coat as it does with a casual jacket.**
[ON SCREEN: one continuous clip from the front, the whole knot start to finish; step words beside it: "fold in half"]
**To create this classic look, start by folding your scarf in half, so you have a loop at one end, and two loose ends at the other.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "ends through the loop"]
**Drape it around your neck, then slip both ends through the loop, and slide it up until it feels comfortable.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: the loop given one twist before the ends go through]
**And here's a little trick for the coldest days. Give the loop one twist before you pull the ends through. It closes the gap, and keeps the cold air out.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished knot, the woman turning toward the camera]
**It looks so elegant because it sits close to your neck, and the ends fall short and tidy. And it takes about five seconds. It's that simple.**

## 2. The woven knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "2. The woven knot"]
**Now, keep that same fold, because the next one begins exactly there. The woven knot.**
**This is the one that looks complicated. But here's the best part. It's really just the Parisian knot, with one extra twist.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "one end through the loop"]
**Fold your scarf in half and drape it around your neck, just as before. This time, pull only one end through the loop.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the hands; step words: "turn the loop, second end through"]
**Then give the loop a half turn, so it crosses over itself, and pass the second end through the new opening.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up of the finished knot, the ends crossing over and under]
**The two ends weave over and under each other, so the knot lies flat against your chest instead of bunching up.**
**It works beautifully with a woven wool scarf, and it instantly gives you that polished, intentional look. So stunning.**

## 3. The once-around
[ON SCREEN: white card: "3. The once-around"]
**Next, we have a style that feels effortlessly chic. The once-around.**
[ON SCREEN: two women side by side: one with a scarf wound tightly round and round, one with it wrapped once and loose]
**We get it. It's tempting to wind your scarf round and round until your neck disappears. But elegance is all about balance.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "one side longer, wrap once"]
**Instead, try wrapping it just once. Drape the scarf with one side much longer, wrap that long side once around your neck, and let both ends fall in front again.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "loosen, even the ends"]
**Then loosen the loop a little, and adjust the ends until they sit where you like.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look with a fine cashmere scarf; then with a chunky knit]
**It works beautifully with a fine cashmere scarf, and just as well with a chunky knit.**
**It creates one soft, relaxed line, and your neck still stays warm. It feels effortless, because it is.**

## 4. The soft knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "4. The soft knot"]
**Next, we have a very simple, yet undeniably classy knot. The soft knot.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "long end over short"]
**Drape your scarf with one side a little longer. Cross the longer end over the shorter one, bring it underneath, and up through the gap at your neck.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "smooth both ends flat"]
**Let it fall over the front, and smooth both ends so they lie flat against your chest.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: a small gold brooch pinned through the knot]
**It suits almost any scarf, whatever its length. And for an extra touch of sophistication, hold it in place with a simple gold pin or a brooch.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished knot under an open wool coat]
**One loose knot, just below your chin. It frames your face, and it looks graceful because nothing is pulled tight. Even the simplest coat feels polished.**

## 5. The fake knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "5. The fake knot"]
**Moving on to one of our favorites. The fake knot.**
**It looks as if you tied something intricate around your neck. You didn't.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "a loose knot in the long side"]
**Keep one side a little longer, and tie one very loose knot in that longer side.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "other end through the knot"]
**Then feed the other end through that knot, and slide it up to where you like it.**
[ON SCREEN: two finished looks side by side: the knot high with a buttoned coat, the knot low over a plain knit]
**With a coat, keep the knot close to your neck. Over a simple knit, let it sit lower, almost like a necklace.**
[ON SCREEN: a woman stepping indoors and slipping the scarf off in one move]
**It looks so neat because the ends fall in one clean line down the front. It's easy to adjust, and quick to take off when you step inside. Clean lines always win. So chic.**

## 6. The hidden knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "6. The hidden knot"]
**Next, we have a style perfect for the coldest days. The hidden knot.**
[ON SCREEN: a woman in a buttoned coat walking through falling snow]
**When the temperature drops, staying warm really is the priority. But winter is also the perfect season to balance comfort with elegance.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "middle at the front, cross behind"]
**Hold the middle of your scarf at the front of your neck, and let both ends fall down your back. Cross them behind your neck, and bring them back to the front.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "one loose knot, tucked under"]
**Tie one loose knot, and tuck it underneath the wrapped part of the scarf.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the hands; step words: "smooth the ends, cover the knot"]
**Then smooth the ends flat, or tuck them into the top of your coat, and pull the top layer down to cover the knot.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look with a buttoned coat]
**It feels like a soft turtleneck. No loose ends, nothing to fix, and your neck is completely covered.**
**So you step outside with ease. Warm, and effortlessly elegant.**

## 7. The drape
[ON SCREEN: white card: "7. The drape"]
**Now, let's talk about the simplest one of all. The drape. No knot at all.**
**True elegance is subtle, never flashy or loud.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "around the neck, under the coat"]
**Place the scarf around the back of your neck, let both sides hang straight down, and wear it under your coat, so it follows the line of your lapels.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: the two ends level; then one end left longer]
**Make sure both ends are even. Or leave one a little longer, on purpose.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look with a tailored camel coat, full length]
**It's the most classic way to wear a scarf with a tailored coat, because those two long lines follow your lapels. That's what makes it so flattering.**
**It looks intentional, even though it takes no effort at all. It's best with a shorter scarf, on a calm day. Save the knots for the windy ones.**

## 8. The shoulder throw
[ON SCREEN: white card: "8. The shoulder throw"]
**Next, we have the shoulder throw. One end in front, one end behind.**
[ON SCREEN: a thick scarf being opened out flat in two hands; then a continuous clip; step words: "one end back over the shoulder"]
**Open the scarf out first, so it covers more of your shoulders. Drape it around your neck, and with a small flick of the hand, pass one end back over your opposite shoulder.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished throw on a thick wool scarf, the woman walking]
**This is the one for a thick, generous scarf. It shows off the texture, and there's an ease to it that feels quietly confident. Relaxed, never fussy.**
[ON SCREEN: two finished looks: a cream scarf on a camel coat; a burgundy scarf on a grey coat]
**And here's a little secret about color. A cream scarf on a camel coat looks calm and seamless. A burgundy scarf on a grey coat becomes the focus. Both are elegant.**
**Just keep your whole outfit to two or three colors, so it always feels cohesive and refined.**

## 9. The blanket scarf triangle
[ON SCREEN: white card: "9. The blanket scarf triangle"]
**Now, if you own a big square blanket scarf, these last two are for you.**
[ON SCREEN: a blanket scarf opened to its full size, held up between two outstretched arms]
**Cozy is beautiful. But too much fabric at once can take away from the polish. The secret is one fold.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip; step words: "corner to corner, point down"]
**Fold it in half, corner to corner, to make a triangle. Hold the two far corners, and let the point face the ground, in front of you.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "cross behind, bring forward"]
**Wrap the corners around your neck, cross them behind you, and bring them back to the front. Let them hang, or tie them underneath and lay the point over the knot.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: the long edge being rolled once before wrapping; then the woman fluffing the front]
**If it feels bulky, roll the long edge a little before you wrap. Then fluff the front.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished triangle over a wool coat]
**A blanket scarf looks best a little soft and undone. It feels cozy, and it still looks put together. And there you have it.**

## 10. The belted wrap
[ON SCREEN: white card: "10. The belted wrap"; a slim leather belt cut out on the caramel]
**And finally, the one that turns a scarf into part of your outfit. The belted wrap.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "open, over the shoulders"]
**Open your blanket scarf fully, and drape it over your shoulders like a shawl, so it covers your shoulders and your upper back. Let both sides hang down the front.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "a slim belt at the waist"]
**Then fasten a slim leather belt over it, at your waist. A belt in a classic color like black or brown will go with almost any outfit.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look over a cream knit and trousers, slow turn]
**The scarf becomes a soft cape. It looks intentional, because the belt gives all that fabric a shape. Your hands are free, and a simple knit suddenly feels like an outfit.**
**It's perfect for everything from a coffee date to an evening out. Absolutely stunning.**
