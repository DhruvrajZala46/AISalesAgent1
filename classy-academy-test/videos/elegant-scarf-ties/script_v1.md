## 1. The Parisian knot
[ON SCREEN: white card, black script: "1. The Parisian knot"]
**Let's start with a knot that has stood the test of time. The Parisian knot.**
[ON SCREEN: one continuous clip from the front, the whole knot start to finish; step words beside it: "fold in half"]
**What makes it so elegant is how neatly it sits. It stays close to your neck, the ends fall short and tidy, and your coat stays in view.**
**Start by folding your scarf in half, so you have a loop at one end, and two loose ends at the other.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "loop on one side"]
**Drape it around your neck, with the loop on one side, and the two ends on the other.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "ends through the loop"]
**Then slip both ends through the loop, and slide it up until it feels comfortable.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: the loop given one twist before the ends go through]
**And here's a quiet little trick for cold days. Give the loop one twist before you pull the ends through. It closes the gap, so the cold air stays out.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished knot on a camel coat; then the same knot on a short fine scarf and on a thick one]
**It works with almost any scarf, long or short, thick or fine. And it takes about five seconds.**
**It's timeless, because it looks just as right with a tailored coat as it does with a casual jacket. It's that simple.**

## 2. The woven knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "2. The woven knot"]
**Now, keep that same fold, because the next one begins exactly there.**
**The woven knot. This is the one that looks complicated. It's really the Parisian knot, with one extra twist.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "one end through the loop"]
**Fold your scarf in half and drape it around your neck, just as before. But this time, pull only one end through the loop.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the hands; step words: "turn the loop"]
**Now give the loop a half turn, so it crosses over itself and makes a new opening.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "second end through"]
**Then pass the second end through that new opening, and gently adjust.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up of the finished knot, the ends crossing over and under]
**The two ends weave over and under each other, so the knot lies flat against your chest instead of bunching up.**
**That's what makes it look so intentional. It's lovely on a woven wool scarf, and it's only two small moves.**

## 3. The once-around
[ON SCREEN: white card: "3. The once-around"]
**Next, we have the softest one. The once-around.**
[ON SCREEN: two women side by side: one with a scarf wound tightly round and round, one with it wrapped once and loose]
**Most of us wind a scarf round and round until our neck disappears. Instead, try wrapping it just once.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "one side longer"]
**Drape the scarf around your neck, and leave one side much longer than the other.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "wrap once"]
**Take the long side, and wrap it once around your neck, until both ends fall in front again.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "loosen, even the ends"]
**Then loosen the loop a little, and adjust the ends until they sit where you like.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look with a fine cashmere scarf; then with a chunky knit]
**It works beautifully with a fine cashmere scarf, and just as well with a chunky knit.**
**It creates one soft, relaxed line, and your neck still stays warm. It feels effortless, because it is.**

## 4. The soft knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "4. The soft knot"]
**Now, keep one side a little longer, because the next two begin that way.**
**The soft knot.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "long end over short"]
**Cross the longer end over the shorter one.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "under, and up through the gap"]
**Bring it underneath, and up through the gap at your neck.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "smooth both ends flat"]
**Let it fall over the front, and smooth both ends so they lie flat against your chest.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: a small gold brooch pinned through the knot]
**For an evening out, hold it in place with a simple gold pin or a brooch.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished knot under an open wool coat]
**One loose knot, just below your chin. It frames your face, and it suits almost any scarf, whatever its length.**
**It gives the simplest coat a polished finish.**

## 5. The fake knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "5. The fake knot"]
**Next, the fake knot. It looks as if you tied something clever around your neck. You didn't.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "a loose knot in the long side"]
**Drape your scarf with one side a little longer. In that longer side, tie one very loose knot.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "other end through the knot"]
**Then feed the other end through that knot, and slide it up to where you like it.**
[ON SCREEN: two finished looks side by side: the knot high with a buttoned coat, the knot low over a plain knit]
**With a coat, keep the knot close to your neck. Over a simple knit, let it sit lower, almost like a necklace.**
[ON SCREEN: a woman stepping indoors and slipping the scarf off in one move]
**The ends hang straight down the front, so it's easy to adjust, and quick to take off when you step inside.**
**It looks neat enough for the office, because everything falls in one clean line. So chic.**

## 6. The hidden knot
[ON SCREEN: white card: "6. The hidden knot"]
**Next, the one for the coldest days. The hidden knot.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "middle at the front"]
**Hold the middle of your scarf at the front of your neck, and let both ends fall over your shoulders, down your back.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "cross behind, bring forward"]
**Cross the ends behind your neck, and bring them back to the front.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "one loose knot, tucked under"]
**Tie one loose knot, and tuck it underneath the wrapped part of the scarf.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the hands; step words: "smooth the ends, cover the knot"]
**Then smooth the ends flat, or tuck them into the top of your coat, and pull the top layer down to cover the knot.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look with a buttoned coat, in falling snow]
**It feels like a soft turtleneck. No loose ends, nothing to fix, and your neck is completely covered.**
**Warm, and still graceful.**

## 7. The drape
[ON SCREEN: white card: "7. The drape"]
**Now, let's talk about the simplest one of all. No knot. The drape.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "around the back of the neck"]
**Place the scarf around the back of your neck, and let both sides hang straight down.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "under the coat, along the lapels"]
**Then wear it under your coat, so it follows the line of your lapels.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: the two ends level; then one end left longer]
**Make sure both ends are even. Or leave one a little longer, on purpose.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look with a tailored camel coat, full length]
**It's the most classic way to wear a scarf with a tailored coat, because those two long lines follow your lapels. That's what makes it so flattering.**
**It's best with a shorter scarf, on a calm day. Save the knots for the windy ones.**

## 8. The shoulder throw
[ON SCREEN: white card: "8. The shoulder throw"]
**Next, we have the shoulder throw. One end in front, one end behind.**
[ON SCREEN: a thick scarf being opened out flat in two hands]
**Before you put it on, open the scarf out, so it covers more of your shoulders and your chest.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "one end back over the shoulder"]
**Drape it around your neck. Then, with a small flick of the hand, pass one end back over your opposite shoulder.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "let it follow the neck"]
**Let it follow the line of your neck, or let it fall softly across your chest.**
[ON SCREEN: two finished looks: a cream scarf on a camel coat; a burgundy scarf on a grey coat]
**And here's a small secret about color. A cream scarf on a camel coat looks calm and seamless. A burgundy scarf on a grey coat becomes the focus. Both are elegant.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished throw on a thick wool scarf, the woman walking]
**This is the one for a thick, generous scarf. It shows off the texture, and there's an ease to it. Relaxed, never fussy.**

## 9. The blanket scarf triangle
[ON SCREEN: white card: "9. The blanket scarf triangle"]
**Now, if you own a big square blanket scarf, these last two are for you.**
[ON SCREEN: a blanket scarf opened to its full size, held up between two outstretched arms]
**A blanket scarf can feel like too much fabric. The secret is one fold.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip; step words: "corner to corner"]
**Fold it in half, corner to corner, to make a triangle.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "point down, in front"]
**Hold the two far corners, and let the point of the triangle face the ground, in front of you.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "cross behind, bring forward"]
**Wrap the corners around your neck, cross them behind you, and bring them back to the front.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "let them hang, or tie underneath"]
**Let them hang. Or tie them together underneath, and lay the point over the knot.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: the long edge being rolled once before wrapping]
**If it feels bulky, roll the long edge a little before you wrap. And keep it low enough that your face stays open.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished triangle over a wool coat, the woman fluffing the front]
**Then fluff the front. A blanket scarf looks best a little soft and undone. It feels cozy, and it still looks put together.**

## 10. The belted wrap
[ON SCREEN: white card: "10. The belted wrap"; a slim leather belt cut out on the caramel]
**And finally, the one that turns a scarf into part of your outfit. The belted wrap.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from the front; step words: "open, over the shoulders"]
**Open your blanket scarf fully, and drape it over your shoulders like a shawl, so it covers your shoulders and your upper back.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "both sides down the front"]
**Let both sides hang down the front.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "a slim belt at the waist"]
**Then fasten a slim leather belt over it, at your waist. Black or brown is always classic.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished look over a cream knit and trousers, slow turn]
**The scarf becomes a soft cape. It looks intentional, because the belt gives all that fabric a shape. Your hands are free, and a simple knit suddenly looks like an outfit.**
**From a coffee date to an evening out.**
