You might think looking elegant in winter requires a whole new wardrobe. But what if you never needed more than one scarf, and five seconds? You don't need a designer scarf, and you don't need any special skill. These work at any age, and on any budget.

In this video, we gently walk through ten ways to tie a scarf: the Parisian knot, with a little trick for the coldest days; the woven knot, the one that looks complicated but only takes two small moves; the once-around; the soft knot; the fake knot; the hidden knot, for when the temperature drops; the drape, worn under a tailored coat; the shoulder throw; and, for a big square blanket scarf, the triangle and the belted wrap.

What you'll need: the scarf you already own. For the last two, a big square blanket scarf and a slim leather belt in black or brown. A simple gold pin or a brooch is a lovely extra touch.

A little secret about color: a cream scarf on a camel coat looks calm and seamless, and a burgundy scarf on a grey coat becomes the focus. Just keep your whole outfit to two or three colors.
