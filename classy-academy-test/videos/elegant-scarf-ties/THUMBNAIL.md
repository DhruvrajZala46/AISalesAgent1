# 10 Elegant Ways to Tie a Scarf - title and Classy Academy thumbnails (13 Nov 2026)

The user asked for the three most proven thumbnails for this video, in the Classy Academy look, with the reason behind
every choice: what makes one scarf video travel and another not, and why women click.

**No thumbnail can guarantee views.** In this research 58 of 832 scarf videos reached 1M (7%) and 2 reached 10M (0.2%).
What follows is what the numbers show and what was built from them.

## 1. What was researched (YouTube public listings, 13 Nov 2026)

- **88 searches** for tying and wearing a scarf, 13 of them in nine other languages. 1,626 videos found; 832 are long-form
  videos about a scarf worn at the neck or shoulders (hijab, hair, knitting, men's and Shorts taken out).
- **Three tiers, every thumbnail read and tagged by eye on 15 points** (`thumbnail-research-tags.txt`):
  - all 58 with 1M+ views (54 after off-topic and men's ones are removed);
  - a random 60 with 100k to 1M (45 kept);
  - a random 96 with 2k to 100k (78 kept).
- **Each winner against its own channel** (`yt-viral`): the full video lists of 48 of the 50 winning channels, 4,574 videos.
- **Titles** of all 832 videos, by tier. **Brightness** of every thumbnail, measured.
- The 34 most-viewed thumbnails read again at full size, one by one.

Skills: `yt-viral`, `yt-package` (title lint), `thumbnail-design` (one promise, phone-size and black-and-white checks,
three different concepts). vidIQ has no credits until 12 Dec 2026 and was not used.

**Limits**
- The listings carry no upload dates. The low-view videos are probably newer, so part of every gap is age.
- The tags are one reading by eye, on small pictures.
- The numbers are small: a 17% against 1% gap is 9 thumbnails against 1.
- Many winners are old and worldwide: half of them show a light scarf or pashmina, only a fifth a winter scarf.

| 1.4M | Laichan's Wonderland, five scarf ties (1,731x) | four camel wool knots on white, no heads, no words | winter scarves, shown like a shop window |
| 1.3M | AliJBoutique, "8 Ways to Tie a Huge Blanket Scarf!" (23,426x) | one woman holding the blanket scarf wide open, no words | the big scarf nobody knows how to wear |

**The psychology, in order of weight**
1. **Proof before the click.** She sees finished ties. A list video that shows its whole list is believed.
2. **A set to choose from.** Several results mean "one of these will suit me and my scarf".
3. **It could be her.** A close crop without eyes makes the scarf the subject, not a stranger's face.
4. **One scarf.** The untied scarf in a woman's hands is an open question; she owns that scarf already.
5. **Low effort to read.** A light ground, few or no words, nothing drawn on top.

## 4. Title

| The title has | 1M+ (58) | 100k-1M (271) | 2k-100k (364) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A number of ways ("10 ways", "12 styles")** | **67%** | 37% | **25%** |
| A number of 10 or more | 52% | 29% | 16% |
| "1 scarf" / one scarf | 10% | 2% | 2% |
| "How to" | 26% | 36% | 46% |
| Winter, fall or cold | 3% | 8% | 11% |
| Elegant, chic, classy, stylish | 14% | 19% | 15% |
| Easy, simple, quick | 19% | 13% | 15% |

- **Recommended: "10 Elegant Ways to Tie a Scarf"** (30 characters, `title.py` 100/100). It is the winners' own form: a
  number of ten, "ways", the scarf. It also fits a phone feed without being cut.
- **The approved title, "10 Elegant Ways to Tie a Scarf This Winter"** (42 characters, 94/100), is cut on a phone to
  "...Tie a Scarf This". Season words are rare among the winners (3% against 11%). Part of that is age: a title without a
  season keeps collecting views all year. The voice still says "this winter"; only the title changes.
- **A second title to test with thumbnail B: "1 Scarf, 10 Elegant Ways to Tie It"** (34 characters, 100/100).
- "Elegant" neither helps nor hurts here. It stays because it is the channel's word.

## 6. Honest limits

- **Every picture is a frame of a clip that is in the video.** Nothing is enlarged: each panel is cut at the frame's own
  size or reduced. The footage is 720 pixels wide and beauty-filtered, so faces in B look soft at full size.
- **B says "1 SCARF".** That is the script's own line ("ten ways to tie one scarf"), but ties 9 and 10 need a big blanket
  scarf. If that feels like too much of a claim, use A or C, or change the band to "ONE SCARF. SO MANY WAYS."
- **B shows one woman's face four times.** Her reel was found on a Pinterest account that posts several different women
  with the same caption style, so it may be a repost. Who she is and who owns the reel are not confirmed. The reel was
  checked at full size (the same ring, nails, earrings, belt buckle and room for 33 seconds, natural motion blur, 0.00
  on the watermark scan) and looks like real footage. A and C show no eyes.
- **A's bottom row has one green scarf** on a teal wall. It is the only clear close frame of the blanket triangle.
- **Rights:** creators' reels; none is licensed. The creator decides.
- **Logo:** these carry the Classy Academy logo. The delivered video carries The Classy Elegance logo. One of the two needs
  changing for the channel it goes to.
