none. But the track itself measures:
- 51% of its energy below 120 Hz: a low drone sits under the piano notes;
- its level swings 10 dB between passages (-30 to -20 dBFS in 5-second windows), so it swells and drops.

Swells and a low drone under a voice can easily be heard as "effects". That is my reading of the complaint; it is a
measurement, not something I heard.

**The new bed.**
- 8.5% of its energy below 120 Hz and 0.08% above 4 kHz: piano notes only, no drone, no percussion or shimmer;
- the level is steady: within 0.9 dB over the whole track (it was 2.9 dB; I evened it with a slow gain curve);
- it sits 1 dB lower under the voice than last time.
- One difference to know: it plays more notes (2.8 per second against 0.9), so it is a fuller piano than the last one.
