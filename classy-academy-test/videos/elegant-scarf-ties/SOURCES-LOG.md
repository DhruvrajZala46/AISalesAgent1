# SOURCES-LOG - 10 Elegant Ways to Tie a Scarf This Winter (Classy Academy, v1, 12 Nov 2026)

One row per clip, in order of first appearance. **54 video clips and 1 photo**.
- **Where the footage comes from:** Pinterest video pins of Instagram / TikTok creators' reels, found in 84 searches over four
  rounds (537 clips downloaded and read). No free-stock site was used.
- **A tutorial reel appears in several lines of its own tie** (one step per line), always with different seconds; the builder
  refuses any window that overlaps footage already shown. No second of footage appears twice.
- **Duplicate check:** every download hashed at 1 frame per second (`tools/dupall.py`); re-pins of the same reel were
  grouped and only one copy kept; 0 clashes among the used clips.
- **No repeats from earlier videos:** the footage check against the used clips of all ten earlier videos (`oldclash.py`) found
  43 downloads repeating earlier footage; none is used.
- **AI checks:**
  - the Gemini watermark scan flagged 1 download (not used); every used clip scored under 0.25;
  - clips with an AI look or mark were dropped by eye: a scarf-folding clip with a "Veo" corner mark, two mirror clips of
    exactly 8.0 s, a "camel coat, 3 ways" slideshow, and the only two video pins of a cream scarf on a camel coat (10.0 s
    and 8.0 s blocks, flawless staged look).
- **Captions, watermarks and stickers:**
  - every candidate was read on time-labelled frame strips, every pick again at large size, every used segment at three
    moments, and the finished edit at 118 snapshots;
  - 20 clips are cropped past a caption, label, watermark or side bars (the `CROP` list in `edit.py`). Most crops are
    1.2x to 1.6x; two are tighter: `x2b13` 2.1x (hands and scarf only, under typed captions) and `t7c21` 2.1x (her
    face and scarf, above an icon on her chest);
  - every prepared clip was scanned for black frames (none).
- **One photo:** no real video of a cream scarf on a camel coat was found, so that one line shows a real street-style
  photo as a still (`pg319`). It passed the photo filter (AI sizes and AI words) and was checked by eye; the same woman
  in the same place appears in a second photo of the set.

**Rights status.** Footage: Instagram / TikTok creators' reels found on Pinterest. **None is licensed**: every row is
**unresolved**, and credit is not permission. A tutorial video is built from other creators' demonstrations, so a
copyright claim or YouTube's reused-content rule is a real risk here. The creator decides before publishing.
Music: "Piano Reflections" by Ahjay Stelino, Mixkit Stock Music Free License (cleared).
