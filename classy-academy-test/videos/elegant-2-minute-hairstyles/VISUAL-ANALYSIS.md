## 2. Sound, measured

| | The channel (two files measured) | This video |
|---|---|---|
| Sound effects | none found (transition gaps at -36 to -42 dBFS, music only) | **none** |
| Music | one soft, sparse solo-piano bed, about 55 bpm | one bed, "Romantic" by Francisco Alvear (Mixkit, free licence) |
| Music under the voice | 11.5 dB and 13 dB | 13 dB |
| Ducking | none; the same level in pauses | none |

- No sound effects were added to any of our videos; the build has none. If the last video felt busy, it was the music.
- **Why the music changed:** the earlier bed (Mixkit "Classical 4/5/9/10") measures 3.4 to 3.9 note onsets per second, and one
  track swings 20 dB between quiet and loud passages. The new track measures 0.9 onsets per second and a 7 dB swing.
- 40 piano-tagged tracks were measured (note density, brightness, level steadiness). **The choice was made by measurement; the
  track was not listened to.**

- **No on-screen timer.** The script suggested one; the tutorial reels are sped up or slowed, so a timer would be invented.
- **Style 8 (the tucked side) uses photos** for the pomade, the barrette and the finished look: no clean video of them
  on a bob was found in three search rounds. Most "tucked bob" photo pins looked AI-made and were dropped; five real
  candid photos are used.
- **Slow motion:** short tutorial windows are slowed up to 2x to last their line. No zoom crops except past captions
  (17 clips, up to 1.64x).
- **The Classy Academy logo** in the corner and on the white end card.
