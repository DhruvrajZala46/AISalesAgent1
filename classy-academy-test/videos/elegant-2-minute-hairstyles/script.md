## 1. The claw-clip French twist
[ON SCREEN: white card, black script: "1. The French twist, in one clip"]
**Let's start with a hairstyle that has stood the test of time. The French twist.**
[ON SCREEN: one continuous clip from behind, the whole style start to finish; a small timer in the corner; step words beside it: "gather low"]
**What makes it so elegant is the line it creates. Everything is lifted away from your face, so your neck and your earrings are what people see.**
**The classic version takes pins and patience. This one takes a single claw clip, in tortoiseshell or black, and about a minute.**
**Start by gathering your hair at the nape of your neck, as if you were making a low ponytail.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "twist upward"]
**Hook your fingers at the base, then twist, so the hair coils upward against the back of your head.**
**If your hair is long, tuck the ends into the twist, or let them fall softly over the top.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "clip beneath the twist"]
**Then secure it with your claw clip, so the teeth grip just beneath the twist.**
[ON SCREEN: two close-ups side by side: fine hair with a small clip, thick wavy hair with a large clip]
**Use two fingers if your hair is thick, and one if it's fine. And for thick or wavy hair, reach for a larger clip.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished twist, the woman turning her head; then the same style at a dinner table and in a grocery aisle]
**It instantly gives you that polished, effortlessly elegant feel, whether you're going to dinner, or just to the grocery store.**

## 2. The wrapped low ponytail
[ON SCREEN: white card: "2. The wrapped low ponytail"]
**Next, we have the simplest upgrade of all. The wrapped low ponytail.**
[ON SCREEN: two low ponytails side by side: one with the elastic showing, one wrapped]
**A ponytail can look rushed, or it can look intentional. The difference is one small strand.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "brush back, tie low"]
**Brush your hair back in the direction of the ponytail, so it sits smooth, and secure it low, at the nape of your neck.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the fingers; step words: "wrap one strand, pin underneath"]
**Now take a small strand from the ponytail, wrap it around the elastic until it disappears, and pin the end underneath.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: hairspray on a clean toothbrush, smoothing flyaways at the hairline]
**And here's a quiet little trick for flyaways. Spray a touch of hairspray onto a clean toothbrush, and smooth them down.**
[ON SCREEN: a ribbon being wound around the elastic of a low ponytail]
**For something softer, wind a ribbon around the elastic instead. Black, ivory or navy always looks classic.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished wrapped ponytail with a simple knit]
**No elastic showing. No loose ends. That's what makes it feel intentional, and a simple outfit suddenly looks finished.**

## 3. The flipped ponytail
[ON SCREEN: white card: "3. The flipped ponytail"]
**Now, keep that same low ponytail, because the next one begins exactly there.**
**The flipped ponytail. It takes less than a minute, and it looks far more intricate than it is.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "open a gap above the elastic"]
**With your fingers, split the hair just above the elastic, to make a small opening.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "flip up, over and through"]
**Flip the ponytail up and over, push it down through that opening, and pull it through.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "slide the elastic up"]
**Then slide the elastic up to tighten it.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished style, slow turn]
**You're left with a soft twist on each side, folding into the center. The flip does the styling for you. So chic.**

## 4. The rolled chignon
[ON SCREEN: white card: "4. The rolled chignon"]
**And if you'd like to carry that ponytail into the evening, this is the rolled chignon.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "loosen, open a pocket"]
**Loosen the elastic by an inch or two, and open a small pocket in the hair just above it.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "roll up, tuck in"]
**Roll the ponytail up on itself, and tuck the roll into that pocket.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the pins; step words: "pin, one flat above the elastic"]
**Secure it against your head with a few bobby pins, and place one pin flat, just above the elastic.**
**If your hair is fine, you may find this one holds even more easily.**
**A low chignon always feels graceful, because it sits softly at the nape, and lets your neckline show.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished chignon with earrings and an evening neckline]
**It looks like you sat in a salon chair. You didn't.**

## 5. The rope twist
[ON SCREEN: white card: "5. The rope twist"]
**Next, the rope twist. This is for the days you'd love a braid, without the braiding.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "two equal sections"]
**Start with a ponytail, high or low, and split it into two equal sections.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the hands; step words: "twist both to the left"]
**Twist both sections in the same direction, to the left.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "cross left over right"]
**Then cross the left section over the right. So you twist one way, and wrap the other way.**
**That opposite direction is what creates the rope.**
**Keep going to the ends, swapping hands as you wrap to keep it firm, and finish with a small clear elastic.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: fingers gently pulling the sides of the twist wider]
**And one more tiny thing. Gently pull at the sides of the twist. It shows off the shape, and makes your hair look fuller.**
[ON SCREEN: the rope twist over one shoulder; then wrapped into a bun]
**Wear it over one shoulder, or wrap it into a bun. It feels polished, because every strand has its place.**

## 6. The one-minute sleek bun
[ON SCREEN: white card: "6. The one-minute sleek bun"]
**Next, we have a very simple, yet undeniably classy hairstyle. The sleek low bun.**
[ON SCREEN: a hair donut in a hand, beside an elastic and three pins]
**It works beautifully on shoulder-length hair, because a small hair donut does the work for you.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "ponytail through the donut"]
**Pull your hair into a low ponytail, and thread it through the donut.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "fan the hair over, second elastic"]
**Fan the hair out over the donut until it's covered, and slip a second elastic over the top.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "pin around the edge"]
**Then press the bun against your head, and slide a few bobby pins around the outer edge.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: damp fingers smoothing a fine ponytail before it is spread]
**If your hair is fine, lightly dampen the ponytail first. It makes the spreading much easier.**
**And if your hair is very long and thick, the twist or the chignon will suit you better.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished bun in profile with a blazer]
**About a minute. And that smooth, round shape makes everything else you're wearing look more deliberate.**

## 7. The flipped half-up
[ON SCREEN: white card: "7. The flipped half-up"]
**Now, let's talk about wearing your hair down, with a little more intention.**
**The flipped half-up.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "top section, small elastic"]
**Take a section of hair from above your ears, across the back of your head, and secure it with a small elastic, close to your hair color.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "flip up and through"]
**Make a small opening just above the elastic, flip that little ponytail up and through, and pull it down to tighten.**
[ON SCREEN: a row of three women with the same style: straight, wavy and curly hair]
**That's all. And it works whatever your texture. Straight, wavy or curly.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished style from the front, then from behind]
**Your face is open, and the back has a quiet twist that looks considered. Effortless, and still put together.**

## 8. The tucked side
[ON SCREEN: white card: "8. The tucked side"]
**If your hair is shorter, a bob or a lob, this one is for you. The tucked side.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip, a woman with a bob at a mirror; step words: "side part, tuck one side"]
**Make a side part. Then tuck one side behind your ear.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: a little pomade warmed between the fingertips, smoothed over the tucked hair]
**Here's the key. Warm a tiny amount of gel or pomade between your fingers, about the size of a small coin, and smooth it over the hair as you tuck. It gives the strands a little grip.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "one barrette above the ear"; the barrette cut out on the caramel]
**Then slide in one barrette, in gold or tortoiseshell, just above the ear, to hold it there.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished bob in three-quarter view, the earring visible]
**One side tucked, one side soft. It shows your cheekbone and your earring, and that's what makes a simple cut feel styled.**

## 9. The headband roll
[ON SCREEN: white card: "9. The headband roll"]
**Next, a style that works beautifully on shoulder-length hair, and looks far more complicated than it is. The headband roll.**
**And it takes about two minutes.**
[ON SCREEN: a soft elastic headband in black and one in brown, cut out on the caramel]
**You'll need a soft elastic headband, in a classic color like black or chocolate brown.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "band on, low at the back"]
**Place it over your hair, and push the back down so it sits low on your head.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "tuck from the ear"]
**Starting near one ear, take a small section, curve it up, and tuck the ends under the band.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "work toward the back"]
**Keep tucking, section by section, working from each ear toward the back, and finish with the last piece in the middle.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: a bobby pin slid across the band behind the ear]
**If your headband tends to slip, slide a bobby pin across the band, just behind each ear, before you begin.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished roll at the nape, slow turn]
**A soft roll at the nape. Timeless.**

## 10. The French pin bun
[ON SCREEN: white card: "10. The French pin bun"; a single U-shaped pin cut out on the caramel]
**And finally, the one I promised. The one that takes a little practice. The French pin bun.**
**One pin. No elastic. And it suits medium to long hair.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "low ponytail in one hand"]
**Gather your hair into a low ponytail, and hold it in one hand.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "loop around the thumb, wrap the ends"]
**Loop it around your thumb to form a small bun, and wrap the ends around the base.**
**Twist whatever is left, tightly, and tuck it underneath.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the pin; step words: "pin in, flip, push through"]
**Now hold the bun against your head. Slide the pin into the edge of the bun, flip it over, and push it along your scalp until it comes out the other side.**
**The tighter the twist, the better it holds.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished bun with the pin, the woman smiling over her shoulder]
**Give it a few tries. It's worth it. There's a quiet confidence in a bun held by a single pin.**
