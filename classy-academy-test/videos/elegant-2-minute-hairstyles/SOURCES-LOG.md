One row per clip, in order of first appearance. **61 clips**, plus **16 photos** (below).
- **A tutorial clip appears in several consecutive lines** (one step per line), always with different seconds; the builder
  refuses any window that overlaps footage already shown. One clip (`cv_ease1`) appears in the hook and again in the
  recap, with different seconds.
- **Duplicate check:** every downloaded clip (367) hashed at 1 frame per second (`tools/dupall.py`): 0 clashes among the
  used clips.
- **No repeats from earlier videos:**
  - pins whose pin, post or pin image had been used in an earlier video were skipped before download;
  - the footage check against the used clips of all nine earlier videos (1,079 clips, `oldclash.py`) found 16 downloads
    repeating earlier footage; none is used.
- **AI checks:**
  - the Gemini watermark scan flagged none of the 367 downloads;
  - clips and photos with an AI look (glossy, generic faces; 8-second "bob" clips; most "tucked bob" photo pins) were
    dropped by eye.
- **Captions, watermarks and stickers:**
  - every candidate read on time-labelled frame strips, every pick again at large size, every used segment at three moments;
  - 17 clips are cropped past a caption, watermark or sticker (the `CROP` list in `edit.py`); the largest crop is 1.64x;
  - one clip (`s5a6`) is mirrored so that it twists the way the voice says;
  - the edit was snapshotted at 74 moments.
- **Free-stock sites:** none used.

**Rights status.** Footage: Instagram/TikTok creators' tutorial reels found on Pinterest. Photos: retailers' product photos
and street-style photos found on Pinterest. **None is licensed**: every row is **unresolved**, and credit is not permission.
A hairstyle video is built from other creators' whole tutorials, so a copyright claim or YouTube's reused-content rule is
a real risk here. The creator decides before publishing. Music: Mixkit Stock Music Free License (cleared).
