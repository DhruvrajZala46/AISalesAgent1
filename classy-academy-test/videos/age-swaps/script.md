## Script

## Hook

**Have you ever seen a woman in her sixties walk into a café and thought, "How does she look so fresh?"**

**She isn't trying to look younger. No trendy outfit, no designer label in sight. And yet she seems lighter and brighter than women half her age.**

**That kind of elegance isn't accidental. More often than not, it comes down to what she has quietly stopped wearing.**

**In today's video, we're uncovering eight things that can add years to any woman's look, at any age, and the simple swaps elegant women choose instead.**

**Most of them are easy to fix, and many need nothing new at all. Some of these may surprise you, but once you see them, you'll never unsee them. Let's get started.**

## 1. Trousers that pool around your shoes

**Number one, trousers that pool around your shoes.**

**We get it. A little extra length feels safe.**

**But when the fabric bunches up at the ankle, it steals length from your legs and makes even good trousers look borrowed.**

**Elegant women keep one clean, unbroken line down to the shoe.**

**Instead, try a straight-leg trouser in a fabric with some weight, like wool crepe or a sturdy cotton twill, in navy, cream, camel or grey.**

**Then take your favourite pair to a tailor, wear the shoes you'll pair them with, and ask for a hem that just meets the top of the shoe.**

**It's one of the simplest alterations there is, and suddenly those trousers fit like they're yours.**

## 2. Stiff blouses with ruffles at the neck

**Number two, stiff blouses with ruffles at the neck.**

**A high frilled collar or a big bow under the chin can look so pretty on the hanger.**

**But once it's on, a stiff fabric holds itself out like a frame, and all those frills crowd the space just below your chin. The eye goes to the fabric instead of to you, and the look can feel a little old-fashioned.**

**Elegant women let softness frame the face.**

**Instead, choose a blouse that drapes, in silk, satin crepe or any fluid fabric that moves when you do, with an open collar or a gentle V-neck.**

**A soft sheen near your face catches the light and lifts a little of it back to your skin. Cream, blush and powder blue are especially kind.**

**When you try one on, notice what you see first in the mirror. If it's your face, keep it. If it's the blouse, let it go.**

## 3. The big chunky roll neck

**Number three, the big chunky roll neck.**

**Let's be honest. Many of us reach for one on days we'd prefer to cover up, and that's absolutely your choice.**

**But a thick, loose collar that bunches into several rolls doesn't really hide anything. It piles extra volume under the chin and shortens the neck.**

**Think of Audrey Hepburn in Funny Face, dressed all in black in a slim turtleneck that simply follows the neck. That's the version that lasts.**

**Instead of a bulky roll neck, try a fine knit in merino, cashmere or soft cotton that fits close without clinging, folded over just once.**

**And if a full turtleneck feels like too much, a mock neck rises just a little way up the neck and gives you the same polish.**

**A small change, but your jawline will thank you.**

## The guide

**And here's a little secret. Many women who always look polished have simply figured out what flatters them. They know the colours that brighten their skin, the necklines that suit their face, and the shapes that work for their body.**

**If you'd like that same clarity, our personal stylist guide is linked in the description. It's sent straight to your email as soon as you have access.**

## 4. The long cardigan that has lost its shape

**Number four, the long cardigan that has lost its shape.**

**We all have one. Soft, comfortable, perfect for an evening at home.**

**But when a cardigan has stretched out, hangs past the hips and sags at the pockets, it pulls your whole outline down and makes even a smart outfit look tired.**

**Elegant women still love their knits. They simply choose ones that hold their shape.**

**Instead, try a fine-knit cardigan that ends around the hip, with neat cuffs and buttons that close without pulling.**

**Button it all the way and wear it as a top, leave it open over a silk blouse, or drape it over your shoulders on a cool evening. Same comfort, with a much fresher line.**

## 5. Words across your chest

**Number five, words across your chest.**

**A slogan, the name of a city, a big brand name or a cheerful little joke. It's fun, we know.**

**But words get read. Everyone's gaze goes straight to the print, and for a moment the slogan does the talking, not you.**

**Instead, try a simple, soft tee in navy, ivory or heather grey, with a scoop or a V-neck that frames your collarbones.**

**Or if you'd like some character, a classic Breton stripe. It was worn by French sailors as far back as the 1850s, and it's still one of the chicest pieces you can own.**

**Save the slogans for weekends at home, and let your face be the first thing people notice.**

## 6. The wide belt with an oversized buckle

**Number six, the wide belt with an oversized buckle.**

**A belt can be a lovely way to define the waist.**

**But a very wide belt with a large, shiny buckle draws a heavy line across your middle and puts a spotlight exactly where you may not want one. It cuts the outfit into two blocks, and each one looks shorter.**

**Elegant women define the waist more softly.**

**Instead, try a slim belt in the same tone as your outfit, cognac with camel, black with charcoal, so it adds shape without stopping the eye.**

**Or let your clothes shape the waist for you, with a wrap dress, a softly shaped jacket, or a half-tuck of your blouse.**

**If your eye goes straight to the buckle when you catch your reflection, the belt is doing too much.**

## 7. Heavy earrings that dangle down to your jawline

**Number seven, heavy earrings that dangle down to your jawline.**

**Big statement earrings can be fabulous for an evening out.**

**But every day, long, heavy drops draw attention downward, toward the jaw and neck, and over time they can pull on the earlobes too. Add a few necklaces on top, and that space starts to feel crowded.**

**Elegant women keep that space calm and bright.**

**Instead, try small gold or silver hoops, a delicate drop or classic pearl studs, sitting close to the face where they catch the light.**

**Gold tends to flatter warm undertones and silver cooler ones. And if you love necklaces, one is enough, resting near the collarbone.**

**When one pair becomes your everyday pair, it starts to feel like part of you.**

## 8. The bag that carries the whole house

**Number eight, the bag that carries the whole house.**

**We've all been there. An enormous tote or a heavy shoulder bag, packed with everything we might possibly need.**

**But an oversized bag can overwhelm your frame, and when it's heavy, it pulls one side of your body down and changes the way you walk.**

**Elegant women carry less, and they carry it well.**

**Instead, try a mid-sized bag with a clear, structured shape, in a colour that goes with most of what you own, one that sits comfortably on your arm or your shoulder.**

**Tonight, tip everything out and put back only what you'll truly use this week. You'll be amazed how much lighter you feel, and how much more graceful you look walking out the door.**

## Close

**Before you buy anything new, look through what you already have. Very often, the better version of each piece is already in your closet, just waiting at the back.**

**Start with one small swap this week, and notice how different you feel the first time you wear it. Because the goal was never to look younger. It's to let your clothes show the woman you are today.**

**Thank you so much for watching. We hope you enjoyed this video and found something valuable to take with you.**

**Let us know in the comments which swap you'll try first. And if you enjoyed it, a simple like or comment would mean the world to us.**

**Be sure to check the description below for links and access to the stylist guide if you'd like to elevate your personal style even further.**

**Until next time, bye everyone.**
