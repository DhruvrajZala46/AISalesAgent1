Other details:
- **Logo:** the white **CA "Classy Academy"** logo sits top-left on every screen except the white cards and the end card.
- **Transitions:** soft dissolves; a fade to white before every card; panels grow from their corner.
- **Never seen:** chat bubbles, crosses or ticks, strike-throughs, a recap list, a website.

## 2. Footage

- **Lifestyle reels:** street-style and try-on reels of women in neutral outfits (white, cream, camel, black, navy).
  They come from Instagram/TikTok via Pinterest, and some keep a small creator caption.
- **Film scenes:** Marilyn Monroe ("Gentlemen Prefer Blondes").
- **Detail close-ups:** jewellery on wrists (6:06), a hand on a fabric (7:30), steaming a T-shirt (7:55-8:00), shoes and
  bags on a shelf (8:05-8:10).
- **Bad first, good second:**
  - the mistake is shown in loud colours and logos;
  - the swap is shown in calm neutrals.
- **Women of every age, size and skin tone** in most rows (0:39, 1:53, 7:50).

## 3. Animation and rhythm

**Measured on the full file** (`../../how-you-edit.md`):
- **Video start:** fade in from black, 1.0 s.
- **Cards:** text wipes in left to right (~1.2 s), holds ~3 s, fades out 0.5 s.
- **Panels:** grow from the top-left corner (55% to 100%) while fading in, 0.6-0.8 s.
- **A centred clip:** grows from the centre, 1.2 s.
- **Before a card:** fade to white, 0.4 s.
- **Never:** zoom punches, shakes or glitches.
- **Rhythm:** a new picture about every 5 s; the layout changes about every 9 s.

## 4. Sound

**Measured** (`../../edit-style-measured.md`, the voice separated from the music):
- one soft solo-piano bed, about 11.5 dB under the voice, the same level in the pauses;
- it starts with the first word and ends about 5 s after the last;
- **no sound effects** (no whooshes, clicks or dings).
