Have you ever seen a woman in her sixties walk into a café and thought, "How does she look so fresh?" She isn't trying to look younger, and there's no designer label in sight. More often than not, it comes down to what she has quietly stopped wearing.

In this video, we gently walk through eight of them: trousers that pool around your shoes, stiff blouses with ruffles at the neck, the big chunky roll neck, the long cardigan that has lost its shape, words across your chest, the wide belt with an oversized buckle, heavy earrings that dangle to your jawline, and the bag that carries the whole house. For each one, you'll see the simple swap elegant women choose instead, and many of them are already in your closet.

Because the goal was never to look younger. It's to let your clothes show the woman you are today. 🤍
