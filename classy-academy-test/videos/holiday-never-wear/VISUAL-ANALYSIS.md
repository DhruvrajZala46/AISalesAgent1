**Motion:**
- soft dissolves; a fade to white before each number card;
- text wipes in left to right;
- clips rise gently into place;
- no zoom punches, shakes, glitches or sound effects;
- a new picture about every 5 s.

**Sound:** the same soft solo piano about 12 dB under the voice.

- **The three outfit formulas** as the winter video's growing check list: each piece appears as it is said, beside a clip of that outfit.
