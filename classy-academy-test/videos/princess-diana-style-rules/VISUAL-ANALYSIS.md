**Why photographs carry the proof.** The script names exact moments (April 1987, Japan 1986, the balcony, the polo
match in 1988, London 1994, Bosnia 1997, June 1997, 29 June 1994). Moving film of most of these moments was not found
on Pinterest: the video pins are mostly fan slideshows with captions over her. A photograph whose caption names the
moment shows the exact thing the line says; a pretty clip of another day would not. The channel uses photographs of a
named woman too (section 1). Each photograph has a slow push-in, so a still never sits dead, and no photograph is used
twice.

**Diana on film** (13 clips, all fan re-uploads of news film): the wedding gown stepping from the glass coach, hands
reaching to her from a crowd, the photographers' stand, stepping out of a car with her clutch, the pale blue gown she
had re-made, the black-dress evening (three different stretches: across the lawn from the car, the close view with the
clutch, laughing), hugging a child, and the close: laughing behind her hands.

**Rule 5 shows the alteration, not just says it:** the pink gown with ruffles (1983) beside the same gown without them
(1986), then the blue gown with long sleeves (1987) beside the same gown without sleeves (1989). Sources read on
17 Nov: Yahoo / Business Insider ("4 times Princess Diana had outfits altered to make them look brand-new", 28 Nov
2020) and Harper's Bazaar Singapore ("All The Times Princess Diana Recycled Her Royal Wardrobe").

## 3. Pace

- 12:06 long, 43 spoken lines, 139 words a minute (Kokoro `af_heart`, speed 0.80, the channel's voice).
- 96 clips in 105 windows and 54 photographs: about 160 pictures, a new one every 4.5 seconds on average. Cuts fall on
  the word that names the thing.
- Hook: full clips, then the question in script, then Diana revealed at 0:40 with her name; the title at 1:33; the
  first rule card at 2:24. (The script's own hook is longer than the channel's 25 seconds: it follows the reference
  channel's format, as asked.)

## 4. Sound

One soft solo-piano bed, "Classical vibes 3" by Grigoriy Nuzhny (Mixkit, free licence), 15 dB under the voice, steady,
no ducking. **No sound effects**: the channel's measured files have none. It is the charm video's bed; the user did not
object to it there.

## 5. What is not perfect (said plainly)

- **Nothing is licensed.** The press photographs and the archive film belong to agencies and broadcasters. This is the
  highest rights risk of any video so far (`SOURCES-LOG.md`).
- Most pins do not name the photographer or agency, so the credits can only say "the original broadcasters, news
  agencies and photographers".
- Several archive clips are low resolution or have been sharpened by the fan who re-posted them. Two are soft on a
  large screen (the red gown at 5:17, the hug at 10:55).
- 37 everyday clips are marked PARTLY in the sources log: true to the idea of the line, not to every word. No clean
  clip was found of a woman being greeted on arrival, of a brooch being pinned on, of the act of sitting down in a
  dress, or of clothes going into a donation box.
- The black dress on a mannequin (9:54) comes from a pin that names no source.
- Diana's "caring dress" is identified by its description (blue with bright flowers); two of its four photographs have
  no caption.
