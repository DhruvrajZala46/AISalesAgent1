How to make your hair look expensive, glossy and polished, at any age and with any hair type. Here's the truth: it's rarely the haircut.
Some women's hair always seems to catch the light. It isn't the boldest colour or the most complicated cut. It's the finish: the way hair reflects light, holds its colour and behaves from the root to the very tips.
