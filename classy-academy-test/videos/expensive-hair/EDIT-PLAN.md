**Rules from your own replay data**, applied in the plan below:
- Number cards are landmarks: 2.67× more often at peaks than chance.
- Demonstration clips beat finished-hair portraits.
- Full-screen clips held 5 s or more sit below trend.
- A segment where the picture doesn't change drops below trend.
- Keep the guide mention to 10 s or less, at the end of a point.

**Avoid:**
- new fonts, colours or caption styles;
- animated word-by-word captions or pop-up words;
- sound effects;
- zooms, whip transitions or glitch effects;
- tick/cross marks, labels, strike-throughs and recap lists (my earlier "demo/stage" edits used these; they are not your format);
- AI images;
- someone else's watermark or burned-in text.
