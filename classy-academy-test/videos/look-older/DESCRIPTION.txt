Have you ever caught your reflection in a store window and thought, I look older than I feel? Often, it isn't your face at all. It's a few small details in what you're wearing: where a hem stops, the color sitting closest to your face, the frames you've worn for years.

In today's video, we start at your feet and work our way up: lighter shoes that are just as comfortable, the most flattering hem lengths, the details your classic pieces may have outgrown, the right size of print, how to wear black, a touch of lip color, the frames that keep your face defined, and why no age rule should decide what you wear.

None of this needs a new wardrobe. Most of it starts with what you already own. Because elegance has no age limit. 🤍
