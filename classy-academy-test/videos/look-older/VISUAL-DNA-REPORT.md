## The rules applied to this video (with where each was seen)

### Opening (0:00 to the first card)

**Hook pictures**
- The voice starts on frame 1 (My7 0:00.1). There is no music intro and no text except the logo.
- One picture per spoken line, about every 3–5 s, joined by soft dissolves. It fades up from black.
- My7: 0:00 hat woman → 0:04 white shirt under an arcade → 0:08 centred street reel (men turn: "she turns heads") → 0:16 straw tote → 0:21 Audrey-style film clip.

**"In today's video" and the title**
- "in today's video..." is written in black script on a white card, lowercase with an ellipsis (My7 0:25, QOE 0:18).
- The title follows in white script over a full-screen clip that dissolves out of the white, held about 6 s (My7 0:28–0:34).
- **Not copied:** the "Episode 5" line above the title. By the owner's rule of 19 Oct, the reference channel's episode count is not used.

**Before point 1**

### Point cards

- White card. Black calligraphic script "N. Title" in sentence case, one line, centred (My7 0:43 "1. Loud oversized logos"; 7:09 "8. Clothing that feels worn down or uncared for").
- The narrator reads the title ("Number one, loud, oversized logos"). The card is blank on "Number" and the text writes on left to right on the title words.
- The card holds into the first words of the point, then fades, then cuts. Cards last 4.4–8.6 s.
- Before the card, the picture fades to white.

### Layout geometry (measured)

- **Full-screen clips:** vertical reels are cropped to fill. There is no blurred filler.
- **Centred clip:**
  - one vertical clip, full height, about 608 px wide at 1080p, on the soft caramel backdrop;
  - it grows from a dot (My7 0:34) or appears.
- **Row of 3:**
  - three vertical panels of about 608 px with **narrow caramel gutters of about 22 px**, and caramel slivers at both edges (My7 0:37.5: gaps at 280–289 of 854 px);
  - My7's rows are narrower-gapped than the 2026 uploads (48 px, MNEo), so this video uses My7's.
- **Rows of 4–6:** touching panels (My7 1:37, neon 4; 1:55, colours 6).
- **Panel entry:**
  - each panel **grows from its top-left corner** while fading in, in about 0.5 s (My7 0:47.7–0:48.3 and 0:48.6–0:49.3, at 10 fps);
  - side panels of the intro row fade in (0:36.7).
- **Pair:** two framed panels side by side on the caramel (My7 4:35 two try-on reels; 7:17 "Cared for / Uncared for").
- **Split:** a framed photo or clip on the left, with the quote in white serif and script on the caramel right (My7 1:43, 2:07).
- **Logo:** small white monogram top-left on every picture, never on white cards (My7 throughout). This video uses the owner's CE / The Classy Elegance logo (owner's rule, 19 Oct). It does not use the reference channel's CA.

### Text that never appears

- No subtitles.
- No names of people or brands.
- No ticks or crosses.
- No statistics or chart graphics.
- No subscribe ask.
- One pair of comparison labels appears in the whole of My7 (7:17). This video does not need one.

### Sound (the 30-video study)

- One calm female narrator.
- A soft piano bed about 20 dB under the voice, from the first word to the last, with no ducking and **no sound effects** (My7: "none detected").
- After the last word, about 0.5 s of quiet, then the music swells on the white end card for about 5 s and fades out.
- Master loudness of the top videos: −16.6 to −19.3 LUFS (My7 −17.5). This video: −18 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP.

### Pace

- My7 changes picture about 13.5 times a minute.
- Points 1–3 change every 2.5–3.4 s; later points every 4–5 s, with the motion inside the reels carrying the change.
- Single-creator holds run 6–12 s; rows are held up to 12 s.
