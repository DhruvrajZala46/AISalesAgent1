## 3. How this video follows it

- **Opening:**
  - full clips of a calm, composed woman;
  - a row of tiny gestures for "a handful of tiny habits";
  - the title in script over a full clip on "seven subtle body language habits".
- **Each habit:**
  1. the cream card with its name, as it is spoken;
  2. the question ("Have you ever…") shown as the moment itself (a smile switched on for a photo, eye contact turning
     awkward, a phone on the café table, a limp handshake, a spill, a racing heart);
  3. the study shown as its real setting:
     - women at a screen;
     - museum visitors;
     - pairs in cafés;
     - handshakes;
     - two people in the same posture;
     - a 1960s quiz and radio;
     - a woman giving a speech;
  4. the habit, step by step, as it is said (eyes first, then the smile; "see, soften, smile"; the phone into the bag
     or the pocket; palm to palm; a hug or a cheek kiss; lean in; a slow breath out; shoulders drop);
  5. the lesson in white lines on the caramel.
- **The evidence lines** ("these were digital faces", "a rough guide, not a rule", "links, not proof", "I'm not
  promising") get the plain caramel text screen, not a dramatic visual, so the honesty reads as calm.
- **Named women and film scenes**, as in the reference:
  - Kate, Princess of Wales (a smile, a handshake, a speech);
  - Audrey Hepburn and Grace Kelly for the slow smile and the gaze;
  - classic film scenes for eye contact and conversation.
- **Comparisons on cream with a small label**, only where the script compares (a quick smile vs a slow one; a phone
  on the table vs in the bag).
- **The guide:** the reference's guide screen after habit four and at the end; no website, no Classy Closet.
- **Logo:** the CE circle logo in the corner and on the white end card (the user's choice, 3 Nov 2026).
- **Sound:** the same soft piano about 12 dB under the voice; no sound effects.
