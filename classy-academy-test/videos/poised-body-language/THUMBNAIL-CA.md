**The behaviour DNA, as seen:**
- Three real women, edge to edge, in **black** or dark neutrals, on darker, warmer backgrounds (restaurants, cars, interiors).
- Hands at the chin or the face (322k, 341k, 159k).
- The **centre woman's eyes sit above the words**; the words cross chins, hands and necks, never eyes or mouths.
- An outcome word in Montserrat Bold, a Cormorant SC top line, and sometimes a small line in Pinyon script
  ("& Eloquently", "Elegantly").
- No red underline on these (that is the "Never" thumbnails' highlight).
- The CA logo top-left.
