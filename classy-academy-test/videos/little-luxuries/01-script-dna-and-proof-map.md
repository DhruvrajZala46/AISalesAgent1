### 1.2 The structure every script follows

1. **Hook (first 3-4 sentences).** Of the 28 hooks:
   - 13 open with "Have you ever [seen / noticed / met] a woman...";
   - 4 open with "You might think... But what if I told you / here's the truth";
   - 3 open with "Did you know...";
   - the other 8 vary (for example "You know that feeling when..." and "What truly makes a woman classy?").

   **Her top 5 by views all open with "Have you ever..." or "You might think..."** The woman is then described with
   2-3 short negatives and a turn: "There's no flashy outfit, no designer overload, and yet she turns heads with ease.
   That kind of elegance isn't accidental."
2. **Promise + go.** "In today's video, we're uncovering N things... and the subtle shifts that instantly...".
   - Often followed by "Some of these may surprise you, but once you see them, you'll never unsee them." (Never Wear,
     Never Do in Public, Avoid in Fall, Outfit Mistakes).
   - Then "Let's get started." / "So, let's begin."
   - No channel intro in her best videos.
3. **Points.** 5-10 per video, most often 7-8. Point 1 starts straight after the promise.
4. **Guide plug.** Once, mid-video, usually after point 3 or 4. It is often framed as a secret: "And here's a
   secret. Many well-dressed women don't share. They know what flatters them..." -> "linked in the description... sent
   instantly to your email."
5. **Close.**
   1. "Thank you so much for watching / spending your time with us today."
   2. A part-two ask, or "which number will you try? I read them".
   3. "A simple like or comment would truly mean the world to us."
   4. The guide link.
   5. "Until next time, bye everyone" (older videos) or "Bye, my loves" (newer).
6. **Length.** 1,040-1,560 spoken words (median ~1,250). The one exception is Old Money Essentials, at 574.

### 1.4 The kind of tips she gives (what makes them not basic)

Her strong tips always carry at least one of these:

1. **An exact swap with named things.**
   - "Instead of a logo-covered tee, try a crisp white blouse. Swap a monogrammed bag for a structured tote in smooth
     leather."
   - "Linen or trouser shorts instead of ripped jean shorts."
2. **An exact number or rule.**
   - "Stick to two to three main colors."
   - "Slow your movements by even 10%."
   - "Pause for two seconds."
   - "Keep your heel between 1½ and 3 inches."
3. **Exact words to say.** "Thank you for waiting" instead of "Sorry I'm late"; "I won't be able to make it, but
   thank you for thinking of me."
4. **A self-test question.**
   - "Would I feel comfortable opening the door in this if someone I admire stopped by?"
   - "Is this enhancing my features or distracting from them?"
5. **A little-known how-to.**
   - Perfume on moisturised skin and don't rub your wrists.
   - Hold a wine glass by the stem.
   - Let wide-leg trousers drape over the ankle boot.
   - Seamless nude underwear, checked in natural light.
   - Keep the robe draped on a chair.
6. **A named place to use it.** "Even just to the grocery store." / "On the phone, ordering a drink."
7. **An exception line.** "If you love color, don't eliminate it. Refine it." / "It's not that puffers are off
   limits."

**Basic** (what her weaker scripts slip into):
- inner states with no action ("take the pressure off yourself", "become the calmest version of yourself");
- generic care ("wear SPF, brush twice a day").

**Honest note.** Surface counts do not separate her big and small videos. Over 300k vs under 100k:

- swaps: 2 vs 2.5;
- questions: 2 vs 2.

The topic and the hook differ; the template doesn't.

## Part 4. The script blueprint (her format, applied to this topic)

- **Hook (her #1 opener):** "Have you ever seen a woman pay for a coffee, check the time, or slip off her coat, and
  thought... [the moment]?" Then 2-3 short negatives ("No designer logo. No big purchase."), then "That kind of
  elegance isn't accidental."
- **Promise:** "In today's video, we're uncovering N small luxuries elegant women quietly choose, and why they change
  how people see you. Most cost very little. Some may surprise you, but once you notice them, you'll never unsee
  them. So, let's begin."
- **Each point (type C):**
  1. a named heading in 3-6 words;
  2. the everyday moment, without blame;
  3. why it changes how she is seen and how she feels;
  4. "Elegant women tend to...";
  5. the exact action, with named materials and colours, and "instead of X, try Y";
  6. a budget or exception line;
  7. a payoff that starts with "Because elegance...".
- **Points:** 8, about 150 words each. That is her strongest range; 12 would leave about 100 words per point.
- **Guide plug:** once, after point 3, in her "secret" framing.
- **Close:**
  1. one recap sentence;
  2. "Comment the number of the luxury you'll try first. I read them.";
  3. the part-two ask;
  4. "A simple like or comment would truly mean the world to us.";
  5. the guide link;
  6. "Until next time. Bye, my loves."
- **Never:**
  - statistics or health claims;
  - brand names or a sponsor;
  - "high society" or "rich women";
  - "Part 2", or any mention of earlier videos.
