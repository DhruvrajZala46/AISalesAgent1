## 1. The claw-clip bun
[ON SCREEN: white card, black script: "1. The claw-clip bun"]
**Let's start with the easiest one of all. The claw-clip bun.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished bun from behind, the woman moving her head; the clip holding softly]
**What makes it so elegant is its ease. It looks relaxed, never stiff, because a clip holds your hair gently, instead of pulling it tight the way an elastic can.**
[ON SCREEN: two short clips side by side from behind: a French twist going up, this bun going down]
**You may already know the French twist, where the hair is twisted upward. This one goes the other way.**
[ON SCREEN: one continuous clip from behind; step words beside it: "ponytail, twist downward"]
**Start by brushing your hair back into a ponytail. Then twist it downward, toward the nape of your neck, and keep twisting until it curls into a bun.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "clip over the end"]
**Now close your claw clip over the end of the bun, so the teeth hold all of the hair securely.**
[ON SCREEN: two clips cut out on caramel: a small one with close teeth, a large one; labels "fine hair", "long or thick hair"]
**If your hair is fine, choose a smaller clip, with teeth set close together, because a large one can slip. If it's long or thick, reach for a larger one, because a small clip may pop open.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up of the inside of a claw clip: a second row of small teeth]
**And here's a detail worth knowing. Look inside the clip before you buy it. A second row of tiny teeth, or a little rubber on the teeth, gives you extra grip.**
[ON SCREEN: an elastic going onto the ponytail before the twist]
**For a hold that lasts all day, tie the ponytail with an elastic first.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished bun in a tortoiseshell clip, with a navy blazer; then with a cream knit]
**In tortoiseshell or black, it goes with everything, from a navy blazer to a cream knit. And it makes even a rushed morning look intentional.**

## 2. The knotted bun
[ON SCREEN: white card: "2. The knotted bun"]
**Next, we have a bun that looks far more intricate than it is. The knotted bun.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished knotted bun, close, from behind]
**What makes it so lovely is its softness. There are no hard lines, and no elastic to hide. Instead, you simply tie your hair, the way you would begin to tie a ribbon.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "two equal parts"]
**Start by dividing your hair at the back into two equal parts. You can leave a few soft pieces loose around your face.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, close on the hands; step words: "tie one knot, low"]
**Cross one over the other, and tie them into a knot, low at the nape of your neck.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "tuck the ends, pin"]
**Then tuck the ends around the knot, so it becomes a small, soft bun, and secure it with a few bobby pins.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: bobby pins beside the hair, the same colour; one sliding in and disappearing]
**Choose pins that match your hair color, so they disappear. And a light mist of hairspray will keep everything in place.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished knotted bun with a soft blouse and small earrings]
**It feels soft and a little romantic, and no one will ever guess it began with one simple knot.**

## 3. The braided bun
[ON SCREEN: white card: "3. The braided bun"]
**Now, let's talk about the bun that lasts all day. The braided bun.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished braided bun, close, the pattern of the braid catching the light]
**A braid creates texture, so this bun looks far more detailed than the effort it takes. And it's wonderful for medium to long hair.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind; step words: "low ponytail first"]
**Start with a low ponytail. That's what gives the bun its hold, because the braid has something to anchor to.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "braid to the ends, small clear elastic"]
**Braid the ponytail all the way to the ends, and secure it with a small clear elastic. If your hair is very long, bring the braid over your shoulder to finish it.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; step words: "roll upward, pin along the edge"]
**Then roll the braid upward on itself, so it forms a bun, and pin along the edge until it feels secure.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: fingers gently pulling at the edges of the bun]
**And here's a tiny detail that makes all the difference. Gently pull at the edges of the bun. It instantly looks fuller.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished braided bun, the woman turning her head; then the same bun at an evening table]
**With a little practice, it takes about a minute and a half. It's the one to choose for a long day, a flight, or a dinner that runs late, because you can forget about it, and carry on with ease.**

## 4. The half-up bun
[ON SCREEN: white card: "4. The half-up bun"]
**Next, we have one for shorter hair. If you have a bob, or your hair sits at your shoulders, this one is yours.**
[ON SCREEN: a woman with a bob from behind, hair down]
**The half-up bun. What makes it so flattering is the balance. It lifts the hair away from your face, while still showing its length. And it takes seconds.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip from behind on shoulder-length hair; step words: "top half, twist, pin"]
**Gather the top half of your hair, twist it into a small bun, and slide in your bobby pins.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip; the short ends falling softly out of the bun]
**Don't worry about the ends. Instead of pinning every one, let them fall out softly. It's part of the charm, and it keeps the look relaxed rather than severe.**
[ON SCREEN: a barrette cut out on caramel, in gold and in tortoiseshell; then sliding in beside the bun]
**For a more polished finish, add a barrette or a small clip, in gold or tortoiseshell.**
[ON SCREEN: close-up: dry shampoo at the roots, fingers lifting the crown]
**And if your hair feels flat on top, a little dry shampoo at the roots creates lift. Then a light veil of hairspray, so it doesn't unravel.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished half-up bun on a bob, with a simple knit]
**Effortless, and still put together.**

## 5. The ballerina bun
[ON SCREEN: white card: "5. The ballerina bun"]
**And finally, the most classic bun of all, and the rule I promised. The ballerina bun.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished bun from behind and from the side, the line of the neck and shoulders]
**There's something quietly powerful about it. Everything is smooth and lifted away, so the line of your neck and shoulders is what people see. It feels graceful, and you may find you stand a little taller.**
[ON SCREEN: a smooth ponytail at the centre of the back of the head, from behind]
**Start with a smooth ponytail, at the centre of the back of your head.**
[ON SCREEN: continuous clip; step words: "twist firmly into a rope"]
**Twist it firmly into a rope. The tighter you twist, the more secure your bun will be.**
[ON SCREEN: same clip, slowed; a small curved arrow shows the direction of the twist, then the same direction for the coil]
**Now, here's the rule. Coil the rope around the elastic in the same direction you twisted. If you twisted clockwise, wrap clockwise.**
[ON SCREEN: the coil lying flat; the ends tucked under]
**Keep it flat against your head as you go, and tuck the ends underneath. That's what makes it look smooth and round.**
[ON SCREEN: a hairpin cut out on caramel; then a close-up of one pin going in]
**And the pins matter just as much. Hold a hairpin with its points facing the centre of the bun. Catch a little of the hair just outside the bun, then turn the pin, and push it in toward the centre.**
[ON SCREEN: the bun from behind with four small dots at 12, 3, 6 and 9]
**Place four of them, like the hours on a clock. Twelve, three, six and nine. If your hair is thick, add a few more.**
[ON SCREEN: a light mist on a fine, silky ponytail before twisting]
**And if your hair is fine and silky, a little texturizing spray first will give it grip.**
[ON SCREEN: the finished bun, smooth and round, the woman turning her head; then with a camel coat, then with a black dress and small earrings]
**The result is a bun that sits smooth and round against your head. It looks timeless with a camel coat or a little black dress, and it carries a quiet confidence.**
