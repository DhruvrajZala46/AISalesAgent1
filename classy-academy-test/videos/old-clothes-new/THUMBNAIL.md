## Why the current thumbnail misses

The current thumbnail shows Kate Middleton three times, with "Same Gown, / **4 Years Later** / *altered, not replaced*".

1. **It sells a different video.**
   - The headline is about Kate's gown.
   - The video is about the viewer's own clothes: pilling, faded black, steaming, fit.
   - The royal gown is one chapter at 7:20.
   - A viewer who wants "make old clothes look new" sees no old clothes and no "new" promise.
2. **The change is not visible.**
   - The three photos look like the same white dress three times.
   - Nothing marks what was altered.
3. **"4 Years Later" is a number with no subject.**
   - At feed size it reads as a timeline, not a benefit.
