Have you ever stood in front of the mirror in an outfit you love, and something just felt off? The colours were right, the fabric was lovely, and yet the whole look seemed a little heavier, a little shorter, a little less polished. More often than not, it isn't the clothes. It's the lines they draw.

In this video, we gently walk through six of them: a layer that peeks out from underneath, a waistline that sits too low, short sleeves that stop at the widest part of your arm, a gap between your skirt and your boots, a line across your ankle, and a jacket buttoned the wrong way. For each one, you'll see the simple fix elegant women use instead, and most of them work with what's already in your wardrobe.

Because elegance was never about adding more. It's about letting every line work with you. 🤍
