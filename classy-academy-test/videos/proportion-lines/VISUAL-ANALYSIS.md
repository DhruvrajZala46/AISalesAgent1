## 3. How the winners are edited (measured)

**Pace and layouts:**
- **Pace:** a new picture about every 5 s; the layout changes about every 9 s; calm, never rushed.
- **Screen time:**
  - the caramel backdrop with quotes, single clips and cut-outs: 34% in the top 5 videos;
  - rows of clips: 23%;
  - full-screen clips: 27%;
  - white number cards: 8%.

**Each point**, as in the #1 video:
1. a white card with the point in black script;
2. the mistake shown literally, in a row (bad first);
3. the principle line on caramel, white serif with one word in script;
4. one centred clip of the fix;
5. a row of good examples, one per spoken item;
6. a full-screen exit.

**Motion and logo:**
- fade in from black;
- soft dissolves; fade to white before each card;
- card text wipes in left to right;
- panels grow from the corner;
- a centred clip grows from the centre;
- **no zoom punches, shakes or glitches**;
- the CE circle logo top-left.

**Sound:** one soft solo-piano bed about 12 dB under the voice, steady; **no sound effects at all** (none found in the measured files).

## 4. What this video does

- **Layouts:** only the ones listed in section 3, with the channel's own full screen: the whole vertical clip over its own blurred copy, never a 16:9 crop that cuts faces.

- **Who is shown:** women of every age, size and skin tone.
