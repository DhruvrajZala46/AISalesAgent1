# How to Look Expensive & Put-Together in Winter (No Matter Your Budget) - Classy Academy thumbnails (8 Nov 2026)

The Classy Academy versions (the Classy Elegance versions are in `THUMBNAIL.md`). Built with the `yt-package` skill,
`workflow/4-THUMBNAIL.md`, the real CA logo (`assets/logo_ca.png`) and `tools/thumb_ca_real.py` (editable layers).
The brief's line "dressing a tummy" was a carry-over from the previous brief; these follow this video's promise.

**The "expensive" DNA** (different from the channel's outfit thumbnails, which are full length):
- **Crop:** waist-up or closer; big faces in the top third, on the centre panel above the words.
- **Women:** polished, glossy hair, soft makeup. Many are mirror or phone selfies (716k, 97k, 310k, 100k).
- **Luxury cues:** gold earrings and watches, structured bags, champagne, fine knits.
- **Palette:** warm neutrals (camel, cream, chocolate, black) in soft indoor or city light, slightly warm and darker.
- **Words:** a huge 1-2 word Montserrat headline, a small Cormorant SC line, centred; no red underline; the CA logo top-left.

**The topic's winners** (the same 12 YouTube searches as `THUMBNAIL.md`): "Expensive" in 8 of the top 20, often with
"on a budget"; women in winter coats.

**Title check** (`title.py`): the title is 70 characters, so a phone feed shows only "How to Look Expensive &
Put-Together in..." and cuts **"Winter"**. Each thumbnail therefore says or shows winter. All three use words the
title does not.

- **Side by side** (`preview_vs_channel.jpg`, next to six CA thumbnails at feed size; `preview_phone_size.jpg` at 168x94):
  - the same panels, grade, fonts, sizes and logo;
  - every headline is at the channel's full size (cap height 108) and reads at phone size;
  - the faces stay clear.
- **Sharpness:** 1.23 to 2.21 source pixels per output pixel; nothing is enlarged.
- **Numbers:** Cormorant SC draws old-style figures ("$6o"), so the builder now sets lining figures (`lnum`). Words
  without digits are unchanged; the tummy thumbnails still rebuild pixel for pixel.

**Which one:** there is no click data for this video, so this is a design call.
- **1** is the closest to the channel: CA's own "Quiet Luxury" words and its warm, glossy, waist-up "expensive" look. It
  is also the most attractive at a glance (three faces, one colour story) and adds a promise the title does not say.
- **2** is the most curious and the only one with a number. It looks most like street style, so it is less "CA".
- **3** is the warmest and most personal (smiles, selfies).

Upload all three to YouTube Studio → Thumbnail → **Test & compare**.
