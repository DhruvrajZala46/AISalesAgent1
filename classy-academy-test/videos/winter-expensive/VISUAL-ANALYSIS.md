## 3. How this video follows it

- **Each point** follows this order:
  1. a white card ("1. Wear a coat that fits you");
  2. the reason in a clip, with a quote beside it;
  3. each step shown as it is said, in rows of clips or a clip with a cut-out;
  4. **"Budget version / Splurge version"** as two product cut-outs on the caramel, labelled, beside a clip, as in the 716k's product rows.
- **Matching, line by line.** For example:
  - the coat fit checks: the shoulder seam, lifting both arms, the sleeve at the wrist bone, the two lengths;
  - the pinch test and the fabric shaver;
  - the three colour families as rows (camel, cream, chocolate; charcoal, grey, black; navy, white, grey);
  - hair oil on the ends, a wooden comb, a low bun under a hat;
  - matching gloves, coat and bag;
  - vinegar on salt marks, polish, a suede brush, the cobbler, a lint roller, a wide hanger;
  - the collar outside the sweater and the cuff at the wrist;
  - perfume on the wrists and the neck.
- **The bonus.** The three-budget outfit is built piece by piece beside a clip of a camel outfit (Budget, Mid-range, Splurge), then the three side by side for "Notice the pattern?".
- **The guide:** "Check The Description For / Personal Stylist Guide" over a full clip after point four and at the end. No Classy Closet and no website.
- **Footage:**
  - Pinterest video pins of Instagram and TikTok creators;
  - every clip checked for AI (watermark scan, metadata, end cards), captions, ads and duplicates;
  - none repeated from our six earlier videos;
  - product photos are Pinterest originals cut out onto the caramel.
- **No heavy zoom:** vertical clips are shown whole, over their own blurred copy, or framed.
- **Sound:** the same soft piano about 12 dB under the voice; no sound effects.
