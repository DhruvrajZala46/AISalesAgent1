You might think looking expensive in winter means buying a designer coat and a closet full of cashmere. But most of what makes a winter outfit look expensive costs very little, and some of it costs nothing at all.

In this video, we gently walk through eight of them: a coat that fits you, knitwear that looks like quality, two or three colours from the same family, smooth hair and soft lips, accessories that look intentional, caring for your shoes and your coat, layering thin instead of bulky, and a warm signature scent. Every tip has a budget and a splurge version, and at the end you'll see one winter outfit at three budgets: the same colours and shapes, only the fabric changes. 🤍
