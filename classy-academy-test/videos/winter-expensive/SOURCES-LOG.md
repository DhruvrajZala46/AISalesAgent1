One row per clip, in order of appearance. **144 clips, each used once**, plus **48 product photos**.
- **Duplicate check:** every downloaded clip hashed at 1 frame per second (`tools/dupall.py`): 0 clashes among the used clips
  (16 re-pins of the same reels, found on the builds, were swapped).
- **No repeats from earlier videos:** checked against the used clips of all six earlier videos (714 clips, `tools/oldclash.py`):
  95 downloaded clips repeated earlier footage; 13 of them were in the first edit and were replaced; none is used.
- **AI checks:**
  - the Gemini watermark scan flagged 2 clips (a_scarf5 0.62, p_camel3 1.00), both replaced; none is used;
  - the metadata check excluded 73 clips (AI-tool lengths, Amazon/affiliate links, AI uploader names);
  - a "couples in scarves" clip with an AI look (a_scarf6) was replaced by eye.
- **Captions, watermarks and ads:** every used segment read by eye at three moments (all-segments sheet, twice); about 50
  clips with captions, brand names, end cards or the wrong subject were trimmed to a clean window or replaced; two kept
  clips are gently cropped past a small caption (p_messy18 1.15x, hr_bun8 1.37x); the whole edit snapshotted every 10 s;
  the render, read every 5 s, showed a thrift-rack clip (k_thrift4) reaching a denim skirt and a blouse under "secondhand
  merino & lambswool": it was replaced by two secondhand-listing photos (thmerino, thlamb) and re-rendered.
- **Free-stock sites:** none used.

Pinterest. **None is licensed**: every row is **unresolved**, credit is not permission; the creator decides before
