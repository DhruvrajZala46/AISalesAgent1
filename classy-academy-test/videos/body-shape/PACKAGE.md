Before you blame your body for one more outfit, stop. There's no right or wrong body, only clothes that are cut for it, or not. If no one ever showed you how, we're showing you today.

We walk through all eight: find your shape in front of a mirror (pear, inverted triangle, hourglass, rectangle or apple, or a little of two); thirds, not halves, with the hand test for your torso; show your narrowest point with a wrap dress, a belt, an empire line or the safety-pin trick; lead the eye to what you love; one colour from top to toe, framed by a long open layer; open the neckline with a soft V, a scoop, a square neck, a wrap or a long pendant; fine knits that skim instead of chunky ones; and shoes that continue the line, no heels needed.

None of these ask you to change your body, and you don't need a new wardrobe. Try just one this week, with what you already own, and notice how you feel.

What's one thing that works on your shape, something you learned yourself or from the women in your family? Tell us in the comments, and if you'd like a part two, let us know. 🤍
