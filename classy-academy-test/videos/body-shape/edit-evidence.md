# Body shape video: why the edit changed (the "demo" edit), Oct 2026

The user, after the Dressing Rules video: the pictures still did not flow with the clips before and
after them, did not match the words closely enough, and did not hold attention; "you are just following
the same format". This page is the evidence behind the new edit. Everything is measured, nothing guessed.

## 1. What the 13 body-shape winners' viewers replay (most-replayed heatmaps)

13 of the 14 study videos have a heatmap (`proof3/*.info.json`). Top 5 peaks and 5 dips per video
(first 15 s and last 5% left out), the words at each moment from the transcript, and the frames at
each moment from the storyboards (`bsev/peaks_sheet.jpg`, `bsev/dips_sheet.jpg`).

**Peaks (median 2.8x the video's median):**
- **The biggest peak of all: Asia Jackson's hand test, 10.7x.** On screen: a real woman doing the test on
  herself, with a label and an arrow drawn on her ("DOUBLE HAND METHOD", "SHORT TORSO"). Her tuck test is
  the second peak (4.3x), her proportion drawing on a real body the third (4.1x).
- **Garment details pointed at on the body:** Marie-Anne's red arrow on the dress detail 5.2x, her try-ons with
  ticks and crosses 8.0x and 6.8x (the monochrome demo).
- **Shape names and definitions:** Lifestyle Cog's list of the five shapes 4.8x; Libby's definitions 3.8x and
  4.9x; Desi Rich's line-up of shapes 2.3x.
- **A checklist building beside the try-on:** Annie Parker "Most Flattering: ✓ High-Waist ✓ Full-Length ✓ Wide-Leg
  ✓ Same Color Shoe" 1.9x and 1.7x.
- **Shoes:** Chic After 60 "your shoes" 8.0x and "Do I really have to wear heels at 60? Absolutely not" 4.2x;
  Marie-Anne "shoes the colour of your trousers" 5.2x.

**Dips (median 0.47x):** abstract talk about confidence and media ("style is not about age...", "clothes are
communication", "media doesn't make our bodies bad"), long holds on a talking head or one frame, promos and
outros, and long looks at the mistake (Annie Parker's "wrong" chunky sweater 0.35x).

**Intros:** from 0:15 to 0:40 the replay sits below the median in 12 of 13 videos (median 0.31x).

## 2. What our own channel's data already said (79-video study, `research-2026-10-12-edit-evidence.md`)

- Number cards 2.67x at our peaks; reach point 1 by 0:30; a new picture every <=5 s.
- Demos and swaps sit at peaks, describing the mistake and abstract "why" talk sit at dips.
- Full-screen mood clips 0.62x at peaks / 1.31x at dips.
- One real woman through a video: median like rate 2.45% vs 1.03% for many different women (thin, 3 videos).
- Static diagram slides: the only layout feature linked to fewer views on our channel once age is taken into
  account (rho -0.38), driven by the body-shape diagrams in "How to Make Jeans Look Instantly Elegant".
- "Pictures don't match the words" 14 comments, 252 likes; "models too young/thin" 39 comments, 1,119 likes.

## 3. The new edit, rule by rule

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **One real woman per point**, from her mistake to her fix to her walking off happy (a small story per point), never 6-8 different women from 6-8 creators | flow (the user); one-woman videos 2.45% vs 1.03% liked; the winners are one presenter demonstrating on herself |
| **The tip is drawn on her** at the word: the thirds line, the three widths, the narrowest point, the V of the neckline, the hard stop at the ankle | the biggest peaks in the topic are labels and arrows on a real body (10.7x, 5.2x); "on-screen graphic showing..." 975 likes |
| **A ✓/✗ list builds beside her**, one line per instruction, timed to the words | Annie Parker's checklist peaks; the screenshot viewers save |
| **Show it**: the test is done on screen (mirror widths, hands under the bust, the safety pins) | demos and tests are the top peaks (hand test 10.7x, tuck test 4.3x) |
| **Real women, never diagram slides**; shapes shown on women who name their own shape | diagram share is the one layout linked to fewer views on our channel |
| **The mistake is brief, the fix is long** | describing the mistake sits at dips (21% vs 1%), swaps at peaks |
| **Kept from the data:** white number card per point, point 1 by 0:30, a new picture every <=5 s, no full-screen mood clips mid-point, guide plug <=10 s at the end of point 4 | 79-video study |
| **The recap brings each point's woman back** as its name is said, the list ticks | closure; the women the viewer followed return |
| **Women 30s-70s+, sizes and skin tones, the same size in DON'T and DO** | 1,119 + 124 likes on model complaints |

Thin spots, said plainly: the one-woman like-rate result rests on 3 videos; the topic peaks show what is
replayed, not full retention curves (no YouTube Studio data in the folder).
