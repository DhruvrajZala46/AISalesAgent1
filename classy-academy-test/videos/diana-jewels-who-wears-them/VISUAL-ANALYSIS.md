## What the jewellery video does (read today)

- **Close-ups of the jewel on the body:** hands with rings, a necklace at the collarbone, an ear with a drop earring,
  fingers fastening a clasp. Almost every jewellery point is shown this close.
- **Film scenes of named women** (Audrey Hepburn) and creators' reels of elegant women, full screen.
- **Labelled comparison rows on the caramel** ("Higher Necklines" / "Open Necklines", "Cool Undertones" / "Warm
  Undertones"): two or three clips with a small title above.
- **A white card per point** in black script; **one sentence alone on cream** ("The most refined jewelry isn't the
  most flashy piece in the room...").
- **A jeweller's workbench** (hands setting stones, tweezers): the channel already shows how jewellery is made.
- The guide over a full clip; the CA logo on the end card.

So this video uses the same: close-ups of the jewel on the body for every "you" line, labelled rows for the
comparisons, real press photographs for each "where is it now?" answer, and a jeweller's bench for point 5.

**Already taught there, so not repeated here:** layering, necklines, small diamonds, a signature metal (gold or silver
for your undertone), knowing when to stop, a signature piece worn every day, earrings for your hairstyle. The new
script's lessons are different: heirlooms on your own big days, comfort before it's admired, one piece worn a second
way, pearls for the day and the night, reworking old pieces, borrowing and lending, choosing what you love over price.

## Motion and sound

Motion as measured: fade in from black, soft dissolves, fade to white before a card, card text written left to right,
panels growing in softly, a slow push-in on every photograph. No zooms, shakes or glitches.

**No sound effects** (none in the channel's measured files). **One soft solo-piano bed, about 13 dB under the voice,
steady, from the first word to just after the last.** The last video's bed ("Gymnopedie No 1") is not used again,
because the user said the last video's music was not good.

## What the build used, and the checks before the render (19 Nov 2026)

- **48 clips** (5 of them real archive film of Diana: her smile, the wedding veil and Spencer tiara, the Lover's Knot,
  two dances) and **41 real press photographs** (Diana, Catherine and Celia McCorquodale in each jewel; the cross at
  auction). Every clip used once. 11:32, 139 words a minute.
- **Music:** "Healing" then "Gymnopedie No 2", both Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0), solo piano, evened by `level.py` to a
  steady bed (Healing: 0.97 note onsets a second, 0.1% of its energy below 120 Hz; Gymnopedie No 2: 0.92 and 1.2%),
  13 dB under the voice. Not the last video's piece. **No sound effects.**
- **Checks:** two screenings of 192 downloads (frames at about 1 a second); every used window sheeted at three
  moments; the last frame of every clip read (5 reels dropped for a company end card); 153 snapshots of the whole edit
  plus re-checks of every changed screen; a dark-frame scan (one guide clip dropped for black flashes between shots).
- **Left as it is, said plainly:** no real clip was found of soft earring backs being changed, of pearl earrings with
  a white shirt and jeans, or of a mother putting her pearls on a daughter except one (a great-grandmother, used for
  "a mother's classic pearls"). Those lines are shown with the nearest real moment and the words on screen; the
  sources log marks each as PARTLY.
