Have you ever looked at a photograph of Princess Diana and noticed the jewel before the dress? Almost thirty years later, nearly every one of those pieces is still out in the world. Some went back to the palace. Some went home to her family. And one is now worn by a woman you would never guess.

In this video, we gently walk through seven of Princess Diana's jewels, where each one is today, and the lesson each one holds:
1. The Spencer tiara: her own family's tiara on her wedding day, 29 July 1981. It went home, and her niece Celia McCorquodale wore it in 2018. Wear family pieces on your own big days, and ask for their story.
2. The Lover's Knot tiara: Queen Mary's tiara, said to have given her headaches. Catherine's favourite tiara since 2015. Comfort before it's admired: try a piece at home before the big night.
3. The emerald headband: a necklace she wore across her forehead in Melbourne in 1985. Catherine wore it as a necklace in Boston in 2022. Give one piece a second life.
4. The Collingwood pearls: worn on her wedding day and again in 1994, and by Catherine since 2017. One pair of pearl earrings for the day and the night.
5. The sapphires made new: a 1981 wedding gift, reworked into drops by Catherine. Have the pieces you never wear changed by a good jeweller.
6. The cross she borrowed: lent to her by a friend in 1987, sold at Sotheby's in 2023. Some of the most beautiful things we wear are simply trusted to us.
7. The sapphire ring: called "the commoner's sapphire" in 1981, and the ring Prince William gave Catherine in 2010. Choose the piece you love, and keep it close.

Because the jewels people remember aren't the most expensive ones. They're the ones that were loved.
