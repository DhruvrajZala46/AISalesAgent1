# 8 Dressing Rules Every Woman Should Know — script

Voice: Kokoro af_heart, speed 0.80. Every spoken line is bold on its own line. Every beat has an
`[ON SCREEN]` line. **DO** = a woman following the rule, **DON'T** = the gentle "before" (never
mocking, never a real person's face held up as a bad example). Women of every age (20s to 70s),
size and skin tone, real footage only.

## Hook

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, a woman in a camel coat, white shirt and dark trousers walking slowly down a street, calm and sure]
**Before you buy one more piece, stop. Which 8 dressing rules make some women look elegant in anything, and why do they cost nothing?**

[ON SCREEN: row of three DO clips: a woman in her 60s in a grey knit and wide trousers, a woman in her 30s in a navy blazer and jeans, a woman in her 40s in a cream sweater and midi skirt]
**Some women know quiet rules that nobody ever wrote down, passed on by a mother or a grandmother.**

[ON SCREEN: close clip, an older woman helping a younger woman get dressed]
**If no one passed them on to you, we're passing them on today.**

[ON SCREEN: white screen, "8 dressing rules"]
**It isn't money, and it isn't a certain body. Just eight timeless rules, for any age. Let's begin.**

## 1. Reveal one, not two

[ON SCREEN: number card: "1. Reveal one, not two"]
**Number one. Reveal one, not two.**

[ON SCREEN: DO clip, a woman in a short skirt with a high neck and long sleeves; then a woman in a V-neck with a midi skirt]
**It's easy to put on a lovely dress and feel that something is a little too much, without knowing why.**

[ON SCREEN: quote side, a woman in her 50s adjusting a soft V-neck knit over a long skirt]
**Usually it's simple. When an outfit shows two things at once, the eye doesn't know where to rest, and the look becomes loud instead of graceful.**

[ON SCREEN: text: "show one. cover the other."]
**Many of us grew up hearing it at home. If it's revealing on top, cover the bottom. If it's revealing on the bottom, cover the top.**

[ON SCREEN: pair: DON'T (short dress, low neckline, from the back, faceless) | DO (same length, high neck, sleeves)]
**So choose one. Instead of a short skirt with a low neckline, try a shorter skirt with a high neck and sleeves. Or a soft V-neck with a midi skirt or wide trousers.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman in a sleeveless top and long linen trousers]
**And if you love showing your arms, that's beautiful. Just keep the neckline and the length calm.**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, a woman walking into a dinner, relaxed]
**It's not about hiding. It's about letting one thing shine, so you feel graceful and completely at ease all evening.**

## 2. Fit first

[ON SCREEN: number card: "2. Fit first"]
**Number two. Fit first, everything else second.**

[ON SCREEN: DON'T clip, a blazer with shoulders hanging past the shoulder, sleeves over the hands]
**It's tempting to buy a piece because the colour is lovely, or because it was on sale.**

[ON SCREEN: DO clip, the same kind of blazer with the seam sitting on the shoulder]
**But fit is what makes clothes look expensive. A simple navy blazer that fits looks more elegant than a beautiful one that doesn't.**

[ON SCREEN: text: "fit is what looks expensive"]
**Fit is what looks expensive, not the label. That's why a classic piece that fits never goes out of style.**

[ON SCREEN: row: shoulder seam on the shoulder | sleeve ending at the wrist bone | trouser hem just touching the shoe]
**Look for three things. The shoulder seam sits on your shoulder. The sleeve ends at your wrist bone. And the trouser hem just touches the top of your shoe.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a tailor pinning a trouser hem]
**If one of them is wrong, take it to a tailor. Shortening a hem or a sleeve often costs less than a new top, and it makes the whole piece look made for you.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman choosing a knit off a rail and checking the shoulders]
**And if a tailor isn't in the budget, buy for your shoulders. Almost everything else can be adjusted, but shoulders can't.**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, a woman in a perfectly fitting coat turning to the mirror, pleased]
**When your clothes fit, you stop pulling and fixing, and you carry yourself with ease.**

## 3. Balance the shape

[ON SCREEN: number card: "3. Balance the shape"]
**Number three. Balance the shape.**

[ON SCREEN: DON'T clip (from the neck down), an oversized jumper over wide trousers]
**Have you ever tried on an outfit you loved on someone else, and on you it just felt wrong?**

[ON SCREEN: text: "it's not your body. it's the balance."]
**It's not your body. It's the balance.**

[ON SCREEN: quote side, a woman in her 40s in a fitted knit with wide-leg trousers]
**That's because when everything is loose, the body disappears in fabric, and when everything is tight, nothing is left to the imagination. The eye looks for one place where the shape is clear.**

[ON SCREEN: pair: DO (wide-leg trousers + fitted knit) | DO (slim trousers + relaxed sweater)]
**Elegant women tend to pair one relaxed piece with one fitted piece. Camel wide trousers with a fitted cream knit. Slim black trousers with a soft, relaxed grey sweater or blazer.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman in an oversized coat pushing up her sleeves to show her wrists]
**And if you love an oversized look, keep it. Just show one narrow point, your wrists, your ankles or your waist.**

[ON SCREEN: text on caramel: "fitted on top, or fitted on the bottom?"]
**Tell us in the comments, which do you reach for more often, fitted on top, or fitted on the bottom?**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, a woman walking, outfit balanced, calm]
**Once the balance is right, the same clothes suddenly look intentional, and you look like yourself.**

## 4. Fabric that holds its shape

[ON SCREEN: number card: "4. Fabric that holds its shape"]
**Number four. Choose fabric that holds its shape.**

[ON SCREEN: DON'T clip, a thin tee clinging and creasing]
**We've all had a top that looked lovely on the hanger and tired after one hour.**

[ON SCREEN: DO clip, a heavier cotton tee that falls straight]
**Thin, shiny fabrics cling, crease and show every line. Fabrics with a little weight fall smoothly and keep the shape of the cut.**

[ON SCREEN: text: "the fabric is half the outfit"]
**The fabric is half the outfit, and it's what makes a classic piece look timeless.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, hands scrunching a fabric in a fist, then letting go]
**Try this in the shop. Squeeze the fabric in your hand for five seconds. If it stays crumpled, it will look crumpled on you. Then hold it up to the light. If you can see through it easily, it will cling.**

[ON SCREEN: row: thick cotton tee | wool trousers | cotton shirt | linen dress]
**Look for thicker cotton, wool, linen and silk, or blends with mostly natural fibres, in ivory, navy, grey or chocolate brown. You may already own a few, and many are easy to find second hand.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman in structured trousers, then in denim, at the same desk]
**Even swapping one pair of soft, stretchy trousers for a pair with a little structure can change how put together you look.**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, a woman in a wool coat on a windy street, the coat holding its line]
**Good fabric quietly takes care of you all day, and you feel polished from morning to evening.**

[ON SCREEN: panel, the stylist guide]
**And if you'd like to know which colours, shapes and fabrics flatter you most, our personal stylist guide is linked in the description.**

## 5. What's underneath stays invisible

[ON SCREEN: number card: "5. What's underneath stays invisible"]
**Number five. What's underneath stays invisible.**

[ON SCREEN: DON'T clip (back view, faceless), a white top with a dark bra showing through]
**It's such a small thing, and it's easy to miss in a rush.**

[ON SCREEN: DO clip, the same white top, nothing showing]
**But a visible strap or line pulls the eye away from everything else you chose so carefully.**

[ON SCREEN: text: "under white, wear nude or red"]
**And here's the one that surprises most women. Under white, wear a shade close to your skin instead of white, or even a soft red. It disappears.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman checking her side profile in a mirror]
**Check your bra from the side. No overflow at the top, no folds under the arms, and the straps stay hidden.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, smooth cotton briefs folded in a drawer]
**For trousers and fitted skirts, choose smooth underwear, ideally in cotton. And if seamless styles feel too warm, a soft cotton one in your skin tone works just as well.**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, a woman in white trousers and a white shirt walking in the sun]
**When nothing underneath shows, everything you wear on top looks cleaner, and you move through the day with complete ease.**

## 6. One star per outfit

[ON SCREEN: number card: "6. One star per outfit"]
**Number six. One star per outfit.**

[ON SCREEN: DO clip, a woman in a bright red coat over all-neutral clothes]
**Colour is wonderful, and a bold print can be beautiful. There's nothing wrong with either.**

[ON SCREEN: DON'T clip, print, sparkle and big jewellery all at once (neck down)]
**But when the print, the colour, the shine and the jewellery all speak at once, they compete, and none of them gets to shine.**

[ON SCREEN: text: "one star. the rest, support."]
**Let one piece be the star, and let everything else support it.**

[ON SCREEN: row: a bright coat with neutrals | a printed skirt with a plain knit | statement earrings with a simple dress]
**A red coat over camel, cream and grey. A printed skirt with a plain navy knit. Bold earrings with a simple black dress, and no necklace.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman with deep skin in a cobalt dress, simple gold studs]
**So if you love colour, wear it. Just let it be the star.**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, the woman in the red coat walking away]
**One clear star makes the whole outfit look intentional, and it lets your personality shine through with confidence.**

## 7. Spend on your shoes first

[ON SCREEN: number card: "7. Spend on your shoes first"]
**Number seven. Spend on your shoes first.**

[ON SCREEN: DON'T clip, scuffed shoes with a worn heel]
**It's easy to spend on the top everyone sees and forget the feet.**

[ON SCREEN: DO clip, clean leather loafers stepping onto a pavement]
**People notice shoes more than we think, because they finish the whole outfit. Worn or shiny plastic shoes can quietly cheapen a lovely look, while one good pair lifts even jeans and a tee.**

[ON SCREEN: text: "good shoes, simple clothes"]
**If your budget is small, put it into your shoes first.**

[ON SCREEN: row: leather loafers | ballet flats | a low block heel, all in one base colour]
**Instead of three cheap pairs, choose one good pair in your base colour, black, camel or chocolate brown, in real leather or suede, with a comfortable sole. Loafers, ballet flats, or a low block heel.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman of 70 walking easily in flat loafers]
**And if your feet hurt in heels, choose comfort, always. Elegant flats are just as graceful, and pain is never elegant.**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, feet in loafers walking over cobbles]
**Good shoes carry you with confidence, wherever the day goes.**

## 8. Hands that look cared for

[ON SCREEN: number card: "8. Hands that look cared for"]
**Number eight. Hands that look cared for.**

[ON SCREEN: DO clip, hands holding a coffee cup, short clean nails]
**Your hands are in every handshake, every cup of coffee, every photo.**

[ON SCREEN: DON'T clip, chipped polish on one nail]
**Chipped polish or very long nails pull the eye, and they can make the most elegant outfit feel a little unfinished.**

[ON SCREEN: text: "short, clean, soft shape"]
**Short, clean and softly shaped is classic, and always elegant.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, hands applying a sheer nude polish]
**Try a soft oval shape, a sheer nude, a pale pink, or simply clean and buffed. Keep hand cream by the sink, and remove polish the moment it chips.**

[ON SCREEN: single clip, a woman with longer, softly shaped nails in a quiet colour]
**And if you love longer nails, keep them. Just choose a soft shape and a quiet colour.**

[ON SCREEN: full-screen clip, hands smoothing a scarf, unhurried]
**Cared-for hands show that you take care of yourself, and that is quietly elegant.**

## How the competitor study shaped the writing

From 49 winners (verified quotes, `proof2/craft_rules.md`):
- **Opening:** the hook removes a barrier ("it isn't money, it isn't a certain body"). It doesn't open on fear.
- **Framing:** rules passed from woman to woman, without inventing anyone's personal story. Comments about mothers and grandmothers appeared in 11 of 16 top videos (12,329 likes).
- **Every point is an upgrade with an exception** ("if you love X, keep it, just..."), never a ban.
- **Every point has a 2-5 word name**, so viewers can list them. Viewers' summary lists were top comments on 12 winners (10,211 likes).
- **One self-identifying question mid-video** ("fitted on top, or fitted on the bottom?"). On one winner, "Comment your body shape below" produced its top 4 comments.
- **A family-rule question at the close**, plus the part-two ask.
- **Clips:** every on-screen beat is marked DO or DON'T, with women of every age and size.
