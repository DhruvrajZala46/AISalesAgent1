## 3. The patterns, and why they work
1. **The three faces the audience already knows: Grace Kelly, Diana and Kate.** Three of the six winners above use
   that exact trio, and Diana or Kate is on five of the six. People click on faces they recognise in a split second, and these three are the most recognised
   elegant women in the world.
2. **Big faces, close, looking out.** On the winners, faces fill the frame and can be read at phone size. On the
   losers (24 read), the women are small, full-length figures, or there are 4-6 tiny photos split by coloured bars.
3. **Sparkle and white.** Tiaras, pearls, veils and lace catch the eye in a dark feed and say "royal" before a word
   is read.
4. **A question, not an answer: "Who is #1?"** In a ranking video, the click comes from wanting to know who wins.
   None of these winners names number one. They show three possible winners and ask.
5. **Few words.** The biggest winner (4,449x) has none. Losers spell the topic out ("Queen Elizabeth's Royal Style
   Secrets", "How To Dress Like A Princess") or add arrows, "REVEALED!" and big logos.

**Left out on purpose:** the AI-painted portraits (gold glitter, waxy skin) used by the 54x and 66x winners. They
work for those channels, but this channel's trust rests on real pictures, and the user has asked for no AI. Every
photo here is a real press or studio photograph. Four AI-redone portraits found on Pinterest (Grace and Kate) were
checked face by face and rejected.

## 5. How they were made, and checks
- Built with `workflow/tools/thumb_ca_real.py` and `thumb/thumb_spec_ca_1080.json`, the same tool and format as the
  channel's thumbnails: three photos edge to edge, a Cormorant small-caps top line, a Montserrat Bold headline, a red
  underline, a small line in \*asterisks\* and the CA logo.
- Every face was checked at full size for AI smoothing. A small "Royal Beauties Kingdom" watermark on Diana's photo
  in A was removed. All three were read at feed size (360 px) and phone size (168 px) beside the topic's winners,
  with every face and the headline readable.
- **Rights:** press and studio photographs, none licensed, as with the earlier thumbnails.
- **The logo is CA**, as on the earlier thumbnails; it can be switched to CE.
