## 2. What it became
1. White number card (kept: our proven card).
2. **One cast per point, filmed in one shoot**, so the clips before and after always match: the café pair (ordering,
   served, thanking), the sofa pair (tired / me too / listening), the café-phone pair (I know), a dinner with friends
   (of course), the friends in yellow (whatever), a tense moment and calm voices (calm down), the restaurant pair
   leaning in (gossip), a woman at her mirror + women 50-70+ (about yourself).
3. Full-screen 16:9 scenes, a new shot every ~5 s (median 4.9 s, 114 shots), soft 0.35 s dissolves inside a scene.
4. **The swap on screen at the exact words**: a cream pill with a red cross for the phrase, a green tick for what to say
   instead (the screenshot moment).
5. Hook: the phrases themselves pop up as they are said; point 1 by 0:38; the guide plug 9 s at the end of point 4;
   the mid-video question with the 4 phrases on screen; the recap is the full 8-swap list on our caramel.
