**What did not hold up.** At first, thumbnails that make a claim about how other people see her ("people notice
you", "signs you're more attractive than you think") looked twice as strong as labels. Compared inside each view tier,
the difference disappears (1M+: 37x against 42x). So in this topic the kind of words matters less than how few there
are and the face.

**The list: what makes one of these videos take off and another not.**
1. **Three words or fewer in the big line.** Readable in under a second on a phone.
2. **A real face showing the feeling the video promises:** a laugh, warmth, delight. Neutral posed faces sit more
   often on the losers.
3. **Not a talking head.** In this topic the creator's own face is more common on the losers.
4. **A promise she can believe about herself:** "it's not your looks" (relief: she does not have to change her face),
   or the payoff in other people (being remembered, loved, admired).
5. **One idea.** The losers name a trait to become ("Become magnetic", "Own any room") or a topic ("Etiquette
   rules"); the winners say something that makes her feel something about herself.
