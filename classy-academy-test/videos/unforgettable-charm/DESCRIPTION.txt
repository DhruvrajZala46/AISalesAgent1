You can be the most beautiful woman in the room and still be forgotten in five minutes. But why are some women never forgotten? She isn't the prettiest. She isn't the loudest. She doesn't even have the best outfit. And yet she's the one everybody talks about in the car on the way home.

That isn't luck, and it isn't looks. It's charm. In this video, we gently walk through five quiet habits, and exactly how to practise each one:
1. Choose where your attention goes: choose one person, turn your shoulders towards her, and stay for one whole story.
2. Let your contrasts show: let one hidden side of you show, softly. "Can I be honest? It wasn't my favourite. But the music was beautiful."
3. Make them feel interesting: ask about the choice behind what she just told you. "What made you choose that?"
4. Be calm on the inside: before you walk in, finish one small sentence. "Tonight, I'd simply like to..."
5. Let them discover you slowly: give a true answer in two sentences, then hand the conversation back.

Charm was never about being the loudest woman in the room, or the most perfect one. It's quiet. It's intentional. And once it's yours, people don't just notice you. They remember you.
