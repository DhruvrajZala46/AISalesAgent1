# Visual script v5: what the viewer must SEE on every line (sound off)

Written before any sourcing, from `workflow/EDIT-RULES.md` (her own videos, second by second). Each section tells one
story: **the mistake** (caramel rows of different women in the exact item) -> **why** (one principle line, or a
labelled cut-out) -> **the swap** (cream: framed reels or cut-outs of the right piece) -> **a calm exit**. A row holds
for its whole sentence and fills left to right on the named words.

**Layout codes**

| Code | What it is |
|---|---|
| R3 / R4 | caramel row of 3 or 4 tall full-length reels, edge to edge, each growing in on its word |
| C1 | one tall reel centred on caramel, growing from the centre; pictures may change inside it |
| W2 / W3 | cream background, 2 or 3 framed reels with gaps (the "instead" look of the fall video) |
| W1 | one framed reel centred on cream/white |
| CUT | product cut-outs on cream with small serif labels, appearing one per word |
| PL | principle line: white serif with one word in script, beside a reel (left reel, text right) |
| FS | full screen: landscape footage only (film scene / landscape clip) |
| CARD | white number card |
