# Music and sound-design plan: 8 Things Elegant Ladies Never Wear in Winter

The plan copies what the channel's own videos measure (CHANNEL-DNA-REPORT.md, section 5). Every level below is checked on
the rendered file in QC-REPORT.md.

## What the channel does (measured)

The voice and music of "7 Things Elegant Ladies Avoid in Fall" were separated with Demucs and measured on their own.
- **Levels:**
  - voice about -18.9 dBFS while speaking;
  - music about 19-20 dB under the voice while she talks, and about 6 dB louder in the pauses.
- **The music:**
  - one soft instrumental bed, with 87% of its energy below 300 Hz;
  - almost nothing above 4 kHz, and no drums;
  - it starts with the first word and ends a few seconds after the last word.
- **Sound effects:** none. At 17 of 18 cuts the high band of the music stem sits at -60 to -73 dBFS, which is
  silence; a whoosh would read about -30.
- **Earlier measurements:** the #1 video "8 Things Elegant Ladies Never Wear" and "Old Money" sit at 11.5-13 dB under the
  voice, steady.

## What this video does

| Element | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Music | Mixkit "Classical 4", "Classical 9", "Classical 5", "Classical 10" (Jonny S.), soft solo piano, chained with 4 s crossfades | slow, warm, low-register piano, like the channel's beds; free licence; the same tracks as the channel's earlier edits in this repo |
| Tone | high-pass 40 Hz, +2 dB at 150 Hz, +2 dB at 3.5 kHz, mono | keeps the bed warm and low, out of the voice's way |
| Level | about 12 dB under the voice (speech RMS -17.7 dBFS, music RMS -29.7 dBFS), steady, no ducking | matches the #1 video (the most viewed) at 11.5-13 dB; the newest video's 19-20 dB is the other end of the channel's range |
| Start / end | fades in over 1.2 s with the first word (0.7 s), fades out over the last 3.5 s under the end card | the channel's own timing |
| Sound effects | none: no whoosh, ding, riser or impact at any cut or card | none found in the channel's measured videos |
| Film clips | *You've Got Mail* and *Little Women* scenes play **silent** under the voice | keeps the narration dominant, and keeps the films' soundtracks out (Content ID) |
| Master | loudness-normalised to -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP, 48 kHz AAC | YouTube's playback target, so nothing is turned down or pumped |

## Pauses (where the music is heard alone)

- 0.55 s between lines.
- 0.75 s after each spoken "Number ...".
- 1.15 s before each new point, while the white number card fades in.
- Longer only where the script asks: 0.7 s after the welcome, 1.0 s before the outro.
