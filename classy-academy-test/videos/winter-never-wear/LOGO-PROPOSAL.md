# Logo proposal for The Classy Elegance (for approval: nothing has been changed on the channel)

## What the channel has now (analysed 28 Oct 2026)

- **YouTube avatar:** a "CA" monogram (a high-contrast serif C overlapping an A) in light taupe on white, inside a thin
  gold ring, with "Classy Academy" in widely spaced small capitals.
- **In-video mark:** the same "CA" monogram, top-left, on every screen that is not white.
- **New name:** the script announces "The Classy Elegance". The repo already has a "CE" mark drawn in the same
  monogram style, in camel and white versions (`workflow/assets/logo_ce.png`, `logo_ce_dark.png`). Earlier edits use it.

## What does not work, measured on the avatar at YouTube's own sizes

- **At 98 px (channel page), 48 px (comments) and 32 px (search, mobile):** the taupe-on-white letters lose contrast
  against YouTube's white page, and the "Classy Academy" line is unreadable below 98 px.
- **The gold ring is barely visible at 48 px.**
- **The letters say "CA", which no longer matches the name.**

## The direction: keep the identity, fix legibility

The monogram stays: the same serif, the same overlapping letterforms and the same calm, old-money feel. It becomes
"CE", and the small "Classy Academy" line is dropped from the avatar (the name moves to the banner lockup). Three colourways were rendered and tested at 98, 48 and 32 px. The board
is `logo/logo_options.jpg` in the session files and was sent in chat.

| Option | Look | Small-size result | Fits the videos |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Espresso + ivory, gold hairline** | ivory CE on a deep espresso disc, one fine gold ring | strongest contrast at 32 px on a white page | matches the chocolate/espresso of the newer "Lookbook" videos |
| **B. Ivory + espresso, fine double ring** | espresso CE on ivory, a thin double ring | readable at 48 px; at 32 px fainter than A on a white page | closest to today's light avatar, so the least change for viewers |
| **C. Camel + ivory** | ivory CE on the channel's caramel/camel | readable at 48 px; at 32 px softer than A | matches the caramel backdrop used in most videos |
| **Wordmark lockup** | CE monogram, a gold rule, "THE CLASSY / ELEGANCE" | for the banner, end card and watermark only, not the avatar | |

**Recommendation: A** for the avatar, with the wordmark lockup for the banner and end card.

- **Why A:** it is the only option that stays crisp at 32 px, which is where most viewers see it (search results and
  mobile comments).
- **Old-money match:** espresso, ivory and a single gold line is the same palette as the channel's newest videos.

   **This winter video keeps the current CE logo until you approve.**
