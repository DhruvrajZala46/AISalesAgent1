## 5. Where the picture is not an exact match (said plainly)

58 of the 117 clip rows in `SOURCES-LOG.md` are marked **PARTLY**, each with what is off. The ones that matter most:
- **Outfit 3 ("dinner")**: a belted blazer over a pleated midi skirt with heels. No boots, coat or gloves in the shot;
  the cut-out grid beside it shows the exact six pieces.
- **Boots under jeans**: the only clip of tall boots going under a leg shows grey trousers, not jeans; the next clip
  shows light jeans over black boots.
- **Jeans**: the hem close-ups are wide or flared jeans, and one walking clip shows heels, not boots. No clean clip of
  dark straight jeans resting on a boot was found.
- **"Belted over a skirt in the evening"**: the belted coat is filmed by day, so the label says only "Or belted".
- **Tights**: the skirt worn with opaque tights ends above the knee.
- **Gloves under a sleeve**: brown fabric gloves with knit cuffs, not leather.
- **Wool skirts**: two of the moving skirts are not wool (a teal mid-calf skirt, a cream pleated one).
- **"A warm cafe" table**: a third person's wrist is in the foreground of the cafe clip.
- **Small clips shown large**: the colour-draping clip in the guide is 360 pixels wide and the outro clip 486, so both
  are soft.

Removed for being AI-made or suspect: three near-still "videos" from one account (the same synthetic-looking face in
three outfits), a clip from an account whose name ends in "ai", a clip with a computer-made closet, a product clip with
a machine-smooth zoom. A real-looking clip can still be AI; these checks lower the risk, they do not prove a clip real.
