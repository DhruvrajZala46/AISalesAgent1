**The list: what makes one of these videos take off and another not.**
1. **A claim, not a label.** "French Women Never Wear", "Only 10 Clothes", "Stop Looking Average" promise something and
   leave a question open. "Winter Wardrobe Essentials" only names the shelf the video sits on. This is the largest gap
   in the niche table (60% against 33%, and the reverse for labels).
2. **Three words or fewer in the big line.** No 1M+ thumbnail has six words; a quarter of the rest do.
3. **For a French topic, a real woman the viewer recognises.** She is the proof that the advice is French. More than
   half of the French 1M+ thumbnails have one (Jeanne Damas, Camille Rowe, Caroline de Maigret); almost none of the
   low-view ones do. They use anonymous street photos or AI women.
4. **One idea in the picture.** Collages of cut-out clothes around a presenter are five times more common in the
   low tier than among the winners.
5. **A number or the word "only" when the video is a list** (the live winner: "The Only 10 Clothes You Need All
   Winter (The French Way)", 658,000 views in 22 days on a channel of 8,750 subscribers).
6. **AI women are not what separates them** in this niche (43% of the over-performers use them). What separates them
   is the claim.

**By phrase (titles on the same elegance channels, each video against its own channel):**

| Phrase | Videos (channels) | Usual result | 3x or more | Under 0.7x |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "never wear" | 43 (16) | 1.3x | 10 | 8 |
| "only" (only wear / the only N you need) | 25 (15) | 1.2x | 5 | 5 |
| "always wear" | 8 (7) | 1.5x | 2 | 1 |
| "stop / don't" | 79 (32) | 1.0x | 11 | 27 |
| "essentials" | 55 (23) | 1.3x | 6 | 15 |

- "Never wear" is the strongest phrase, but this video teaches what to wear, so it would be a false promise here.
- "Only" is the phrase of the two biggest live French videos (658,000 and 565,000 views, both on channels under
  9,000 subscribers), which are outside these 59 channels.
- "Always wear" is slightly above usual on a small sample. "Stop" wins big on a few videos and loses often.

**Limits of these numbers.** 21 winners is a small group. Videos with 1M+ are older and come from larger channels
(Vogue, well-known creators), which is why the second table compares each video with its own channel. The tags are my
reading.

## 7. What you should know before using them

- **Five of the nine pins do not name the woman** (table above). They came up in a search for her name and look like
  her press photos, but the pin itself is not proof. Check those five faces yourself before publishing.
- **Rights.** These are press, agency and street-style photos (one is from Getty Images). None is licensed, and none
  of these women has endorsed the video. Classy Academy's own thumbnails use photos the same way; the decision is yours.
- **"French Women Always Wear" is a generalisation.** The script itself says "not every French woman dresses the same
  way". Your 25,000-view video made the same kind of claim with "Never Wear".
- **B repeats the title's words** ("only", "8", "pieces"; `title.py` 80/100 for that pairing). It is kept because the
  topic's biggest live video does exactly that.
- **C uses "Stop", a word Classy Academy has not put on a thumbnail.** It is the same family as its "Never" headlines.
- **The logo is the Classy Academy "CA"**, as asked ("exact same as Classy Academy"). The video itself carries the CE
  logo and goes up on The Classy Elegance. Say the word and all three are rebuilt with the CE logo.
