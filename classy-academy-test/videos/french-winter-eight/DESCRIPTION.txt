Before you buy one more thing for winter, stop. You might think looking elegant in winter means buying more. But the best-dressed women often wear less: the right pieces, again and again. Look at Parisian street style on a cold day and you'll notice it. A long coat, a fine knit, a good pair of boots.

In this video, we gently walk through eight pieces and exactly what to look for in each one:
1. A long wool coat: read the label inside, choose a length below the knee, and try it on over a thick sweater.
2. A fine turtleneck: thin layers keep you warmer than one bulky knit.
3. A V-neck sweater: it frames a collar, your turtleneck, or one fine necklace.
4. A wool blazer: wear it under your coat, and fit the shoulders first.
5. Dark straight-leg jeans: full length, with boots that match your denim.
6. A wool midi skirt: the piece most of us pack away in winter, and shouldn't.
7. Tall leather boots: the top of the boot disappears under the hem of the skirt.
8. Leather gloves: a slim, snug pair finishes any coat.

Then we turn all eight into four complete outfits, without adding a single extra piece: for the market, for lunch with a friend, for dinner, and one more.

Elegance was never about having more. It's about choosing with intention.
