## 3. Animation and rhythm

**Measured on the two channel files** (same channel, same editor):
- **Video start:** fade in from black.
- **Between clips:** soft dissolves.
- **Before a card:** fade to white.
- **Card text:** wipes in left to right (~1.2 s), holds, fades out.
- **Panels:** grow in softly, one per spoken item.
- **Caramel backdrop:** drifts slowly.
- **Never:** zoom punches, shakes, spins, glitches.

**Part 1's storyboard:**
- a new picture about every 5 s;
- each point opens with its white card;
- the layout changes every ~10 s.

## 4. Sound

**Measured on the two channel files:**
- one soft solo-piano bed, about 12 dB under the voice, steady through the pauses;
- **no sound effects** (no whooshes, clicks or dings).

**Part 1:** its captions mark "[music]" under the voice throughout. Its music and any sound effects are **not
measured** (no file). A viewer wrote: "the background music, it's so soft, not overpowering" (12 likes).

- Only layouts A-O.
- Point cards named as short "They..." phrases, as in Part 1.
- Every quote on screen is a line the voice says at that moment.

- **Footage:** Pinterest (Instagram and TikTok reels), plus film scenes and public figures found there. No free-stock
  sites.

- **Branding:** the CA logo top-left; the video ends on the white CA end card.
- **No website on screen**, and no QR code.
- **Title screen:** the episode number is left out until the creator gives the right one.
