## 3. Animation and rhythm

**Measured on the two channel files** (the same channel and editor):
- **Video start:** fade in from black, 1.0 s.
- **Between clips:** soft dissolves.
- **Before a card:** fade to white, ~0.4 s.
- **Card text:** wipes in left to right with a soft edge (~1.2 s), holds, fades out 0.5 s.
- **Panels:** grow in softly from a corner, 0.6-0.8 s, one per spoken item.
- **A centred clip:** grows from the centre.
- **Caramel backdrop:** drifts slowly.
- **Never:** zoom punches, shakes, spins, glitches.

**Storyboard of this video:**
- a new picture about every 5 s;
- each of the 7 points opens with its white card;
- layout changes every ~9 s.

## 4. Sound

**Measured on the two channel files:**
- one soft solo-piano bed, about 12 dB under the voice, the same level in pauses;
- it starts with the first word and ends after the last;
- **no sound effects** (no whooshes, clicks or dings).

**This video:** its captions mark "[music]" under the voice throughout. Its music and any sound effects are **not
measured** (no file).
