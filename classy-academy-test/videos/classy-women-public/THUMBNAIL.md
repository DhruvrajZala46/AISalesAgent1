## What the channel's thumbnails do (all 31, read by eye at full size)

| Part | What every one of the 31 does |
|---|---|
| Layout | **3 vertical photos edge to edge**, thin light dividers (31 of 31). No other layout is ever used. |
| Logo | **"CA / Classy Academy"** white, top-left (31 of 31). Not the CE logo used in the videos. |
| Top line | thin serif small caps (Cormorant SC), white: "Elegant Ladies", "How to Look", "Become" |
| Headline | heavy rounded sans (Montserrat Bold), white, 1-3 words in Title Case, centred, about 75% of the width |
| Small line | serif small caps or a script word: "\*Avoid These\*", "\*Instantly\*", "for Summer", "Elegantly" |
| Red underline | only under **"Never"**, and on every "Never" thumbnail (Never Wear 1.2M, Never Do In Public 512k, Never Share 76k) |
| Depth | soft dark shadow under the text and a gentle darkening behind it; the photos stay bright at the edges |
| Photos | polished, close (waist up) photos of elegant women; faces in the top third, above the text; sunglasses in about a third; café tables, coffee cups, wine glasses; **Kate Middleton in 6 of 31**; Black, white, Asian and Latina women in most |
| Palette | cream, white, black, camel, brown; warm daylight |
| Ages | 20s to 40s only. No thumbnail shows a woman over 50, though the audience is "women of every age". |

What earned the most views:
- The "Never" family (underline + "\*Avoid These\*") holds the two biggest videos: Never Wear (1.2M) and Never Do In Public (512k, the video this one copies).
- Those headlines repeat the title's key word ("Never"), so the channel's own winners break the generic "don't repeat the title" rule. Option A follows the channel; option B tests different words.
