## 4. Light, shadow and the other tricks used

- **Studio light:** a soft window light from the upper right on the cream wall; the table slightly darker.
- **Shadows:** every object stands on the table with a dark contact shadow where it touches and a long, very soft
  cast shadow thrown away from the light, so the things look photographed together, not pasted.
- **Warm grade:** the objects are warmed 4% to sit in the same light; Diana's photo +6% colour, +5% contrast.
- **Vignette** pulling the eye to her face and the bags; a soft shadow on the seam between photo and table.
- **Contrast of colour:** her red jumper against the cream table, the green boots and black Dior as dark anchors.
- **Her smile looks at the viewer** (eye contact) and her face sits in the upper third.
- **Size:** objects about 50-70% of the height, readable at phone size (168 px), checked beside the winners.
- Brightness 138 (B, C) and 117 (A); the winners: 80-125.
- The cut-outs were cleaned (white left inside the bag handles removed, halos trimmed); nobody is retouched.
