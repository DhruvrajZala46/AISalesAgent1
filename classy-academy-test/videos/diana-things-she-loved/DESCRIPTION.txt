Have you ever saved your favourite things for a special day that never seems to come? The good bag. The perfume you love. The jumper that makes you smile. Princess Diana didn't. The things she truly loved, she wore and carried again and again.

In this video, we gently walk through seven of them, the true story behind each, and how to bring the same feeling into your own life:
1. Her green wellies: Balmoral, May 1981. Choose a simple rubber boot in green, navy or black, and keep the rest quiet.
2. The black sheep jumper: from a market stall in Covent Garden, mended at her request, sold at Sotheby's in 2023. Make room for one playful piece, with everything around it calm.
3. Soft brown, not blue: the change make-up artist Mary Greenwell made in 1991. A soft brown or taupe pencil, smudged close to the lashes, and one good coat of mascara.
4. One shoe, three colours: her Tod's Gommino loafers in beige suede, light blue suede and tan leather. When a shoe works, get it in a second colour.
5. The everyday tote: the Gucci bamboo bag, renamed "the Diana" thirty years later. One good everyday bag in tan, black or cream, at any price.
6. The bag she didn't save: the Dior gift from Bernadette Chirac in 1995, renamed the Lady Dior. Use what you love, on ordinary days.
7. Her perfume: Quelques Fleurs on her wedding morning, and First by Van Cleef & Arpels. Choose one scent you truly love, and wear it often.

Because the things you love, worn often, become the way people remember you.
