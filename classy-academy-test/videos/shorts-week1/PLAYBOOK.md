# Shorts playbook - built from 69 Shorts watched by Gemini (12 Nov 2026)

Supersedes the format sections of `PLAN.md`. Raw notes: `../../research-2026-11-12-shorts*.md`.

## 0. How this was made, and its limits

- **69 Shorts watched end to end** by Google Gemini (frames + audio): 22 royal, 14 etiquette, 4 classic film,
  8 old money, 15 women's style (incl. 3 Classy Academy), 3 makeovers, 1 history, and 2 of ours. 28 were watched by
  `gemini-3.6/3.5-flash`, 41 by the lighter `flash-lite` models (free-tier daily limits); the lite answers are
  shorter. 15 of them were also watched by vidIQ the same day (`../../research-2026-11-12-shorts-watched.md`).
- Views, likes and comments from the YouTube Data API; top comments read on 9 winners.
- Fact checks by web search for every royal/film claim used below.
- **Limits:** these are winners picked because they won, so this shows what winners share, not what causes a win.
  Medians, not proof. Shares and "stayed to watch" are not public for other channels.
- **No one can guarantee 10M views or 10K subscribers in a week.** Small channels did reach 10M+ with exactly this
  content (dawn, 7.7K subscribers: 15.7M; TheRoyalFamily, 16.7K: 29.0M; Theviralscope, 27K: 17.8M), but as one hit
  among their 14-42 Shorts. This plan maximises the number of good shots at that.

## 2. Why each element works (what viewers actually react to)

| Element | What the winners do | Why it works (evidence) |
|---|---|---|
| **First frame** | A famous face + one line of tension: "The queen could never bow to anyone" (17.8M), "When Princess Diana wore her revenge dress after Charles confessed to cheating" (13.2M), "Your rich friend's parents invite you to a fancy dinner" (74.8M), "10 times Diana broke royal rules!" (27.6M), "Define Aura" (31.4M), "Notice Kate's subtle hand movement…" (32.8M) | Recognition happens in under a second; the line opens a question the rest answers. 39/43 big winners do it. |
| **Pictures** | Real archive/film; 1-2 s cuts while building, then **one 5-9 s hold on the moment** | Comments are about details found on rewatch: "The gaze the other woman gave her was like saying 'you go girl!'" (51K likes), "that handshake, the walk" (29.8K). The hold lets viewers find them. |
| **Words** | Silent: 1-3 short lines carry the whole story. Voiced: the first sentence *is* the title, a new fact every 4-6 s, "the strangest of all" last, last line loops to the first | Lists hold people to the end ("save the best for last", 21.2M); silent ones are understood with the sound off. |
| **Music** | Silent: a slowed, emotional cover ("Let Her Go"-style, "Stand by Me" cover, Dido slowed), the swell timed to the reveal. Lists: a light or "mystery" bed. Demos: a trending pop song with the switch on a beat | Six of the seven silent royal mega-hits rely on the song for the emotion. |
| **Effects** | Almost none: hard cuts, static text, sometimes a red cross/circle on the detail | 32/43 no sound effects. The footage is the effect. |
| **Emotion** | Admiration for a woman's grace under pressure | Marilyn's calm comeback: top comment "She's yelling in lowercase" (**925K likes**). Diana: "She gave her children a normal life… She slayed" (86.8K). |
| **Comments** | A small debate the viewer can win: was it really taboo? who wore it better? does elegance need money? | "Not EVERYTHING needs to be ELEGANT" and "2044: how to breathe elegantly" (13.6K) on the 21.3M soup Short: mockery is engagement. |

## 3. Five formats for The Classy Elegance

Every Short: real footage only (never AI, and never actresses playing the real woman, e.g. "The Crown"); the CE
monogram; Playfair serif text; the person named on frame 1 ("Princess Diana", not "she"); one documented fact per
claim; a one-line lesson at the end in the channel's voice.

| Code | Format | Length | Voice | Proof (views) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S** | Silent moment: rule/tension in 1-2 lines -> 4-6 one-second cuts -> the moment held 5-9 s -> lesson line | 12-20 s | none | 32.8M, 31.4M, 29.0M, 17.8M, 15.7M, 13.2M, 11.2M |
| **L** | Voiced list: "N times / N secret …", archive per item, cream number card, best item last, loop line | 35-50 s | Kokoro `af_heart` | 27.6M, 24.5M, 23.4M, 23.2M, 21.2M, 18.9M |
| **D** | Etiquette demo: a situation the viewer fears ("You're invited to…"), wrong (red cross) -> right (tick), hands/POV stock + royal footage, a debate question at the end | 15-30 s | voice or text | 74.8M, 50.3M, 22.7M, 21.3M, 16.4M, 16.0M, 13.9M, 13.8M |
| **F** | Film scene: a famous woman's calm, witty line, the scene as filmed with serif subtitles, one hook line on top | 30-60 s | the film's own | 86.8M, 21.5M (Luciano Films' median Short: 1.35M) |
| **C** | Contrast: the same woman at 25 and at 70; then and now; one line | 10-20 s | none | 29.0M (Queen 55 & Diana 20 in 1981 -> 1997), 16.5M, 10.0M |

Sound for S Shorts: we render a soft Mixkit piano/strings track. When uploading from the phone, a trending emotional
sound can be added from YouTube's own library (the licensed way); we never embed a commercial song.

## 6. Production and posting

- Builder: `tools/build_short.py` (spec -> 1080x1920 Short, CE look); each Short's spec, sources log and package in
  its own folder. About 20-30 minutes per Short once the footage is found.
- Voice for L and D Shorts: Kokoro `af_heart`, the channel voice (synthetic voice-overs are used by 9 of the 43
  Shorts over 5M).
- Posting: automatic upload is blocked until the user allows a temporary file host (option A) or keeps posting from
  the phone with reminders (option B).
