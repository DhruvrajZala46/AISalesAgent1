## 1. The idea in one line

The biggest Shorts next to our niche (13M-87M views) are **royal and classic-film women caught in one moment of
grace**, built from archive or film footage with a serif caption. That is Classy Academy's own visual world. We add
what those channels don't give: **the habit the viewer can steal**, and a series name, which is the reason to
subscribe.

## 2. Two kinds of Short, because the winners split in two

| | **S: the silent moment** (reach) | **V: the voiced lesson** (subscribers) |
|---|---|---|
| Proof | Queen bowed for Diana 17.8M (14 s); Kate 32.8M (8 s); revenge dress 13.2M (17 s); "Define Aura" 31.4M (31 s) | Queen's secret rules 21.2M (53 s); Diana broke 10 rules 27.6M (59 s); funeral gesture 23.2M (47 s); dining etiquette 74.8M (29 s); CA's own 508K (79 s) |
| Length | 8-20 s | 25-55 s |
| Voice | none | from frame 0 |
| Text | 1-2 lines carry the whole story | the number cards + word captions |
| Pictures | 4-6 quick archive cuts (~1 s) building tension, then **one long hold (5-9 s) on the moment** | one picture per fact, ~3-5 cuts per 10 s |
| Sound | an emotional track that swells on the moment | soft bed under the voice |
| Ending | a final serif line with the lesson, then it replays | the best item last, a last line that loops to the first |

## 3. Rules every Short follows

- **Footage:** real only, never AI. Royal press/news archive and photos, classic film scenes, interviews, Pexels for
  hands-and-objects. The user accepts the same film/archive clips as the long videos; each clip still gets a
  `SOURCES-LOG.md` row.
- **The Classy Academy look:** the CA monogram top-centre; white serif text with a soft shadow (as on CA's own Short
  and on the "Define Aura" and Diana-list winners); white title cards in black serif for numbers; hard cuts; no sound
  effects.
- **Sound:** a soft emotional piano/strings track rendered in. If the user prefers a trending song, it is added in
  the YouTube app at upload from the Shorts audio library (the licensed way); we never embed a commercial song.
- **Facts:** only documented ones, named in the source line. No royal gossip or "revenge on Camilla" stories, even
  though they get views: they break "every tip proven", and several viral royal Shorts we watched contain claims we
  could not verify (lipstick as a safety signal, the corgi menu).
- **Series tag** on screen and in the title: "Royal Rules for Real Life" (V) and "Moments of Grace" (S).
