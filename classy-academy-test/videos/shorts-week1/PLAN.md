## 1. The idea in one line

The biggest Shorts next to our niche are **royal women and classic-film women caught in one moment of grace**, told
in under 40 seconds over real archive or film footage with a serif caption. That is already Classy Academy's visual
world. We add the one thing those channels don't have: **the habit the viewer can steal**, which is the reason to
subscribe.

## 3. Rules every Short follows

- **Footage:** real only. Royal press/archive footage and photos, classic film scenes, interviews, Pexels for
  everyday hands-and-objects. Never AI. Rights: the user accepts the same unlogged film/archive clips as the long
  videos; each clip still gets a `SOURCES-LOG.md` row.
- **The look (Classy Academy):** the CA monogram top-centre; one serif hook line on screen from frame 0 (cream box or
  white serif over the clip); serif word-by-word captions low-centre; soft dissolves; one soft piano bed about 12 dB
  under the voice; no sound effects; film audio kept when the woman's own words are the point.
- **Script:** 20-40 s, 55-100 words. Second 0: the twist or the rule on screen. Every line names a picture. One
  habit the viewer can do this week. The last line loops into the first.
- **Facts:** every claim is documented and named in the script's source line. Royal gossip, "betrayal" drama and
  unverified stories are out, even though they get views; they break the "every tip proven" rule.
- **Series:** the description and on-screen tag say "Royal Rules for Real Life" or "Moments of Grace", so a viewer
  who likes one has a reason to subscribe for the next.

- After 48 hours, whichever shape has the best "stayed to watch" and views gets 2 of the 3 daily slots; after
  day 4 we can go to 5 a day in the winning shape, if the clips and fact checks keep up.
- Why not 50 a week from day 1: every Short is tested on its own by YouTube; 21 checked ones beat 50 rushed ones, and
  each needs real clips, a fact check and a render.
- Posting times are a test: winners in this data post at every hour, and our own "when viewers are online" data is
  Studio-only.
- Subscribers: our Shorts converted at 0.27% (30,053 views -> 80 subscribers, Jul-Nov). At that rate 1M views ~= 2,700
  subscribers; 10,000 subscribers needs ~3.7M Shorts views, unless the series tag lifts the rate.
