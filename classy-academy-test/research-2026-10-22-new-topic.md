# Classy Academy: one genuinely new, historically proven topic (22 Oct 2026)

Topic research only. No script, outline or package. The exclusion register it was checked against is
`TOPIC-EXCLUSION-REGISTER.md`.

## A. Final title

**8 Elegant Ways to Wear a Scarf** (30 characters, `title.py` 100/100: it shows in full on the mobile feed)

- **Concept:** how to wear a scarf so it looks elegant, not fussy or bulky. It covers the silk square, the long
  scarf and the winter wrap, and which way suits which neckline, coat and occasion.
- **Promise:** after watching, she can take the scarf already sitting in her drawer and wear it eight elegant
  ways, and she knows which way to choose for the day.

## C. Why this concept repeatedly works

**Verified facts:**
- **Season.** YouTube search interest (Google Trends, worldwide, 5 years) for "how to tie a scarf":
  - by month: December 75, November 65, January 62, October 58; July is the low at 45;
  - by year: 49 in 2023, 53 in 2024, 61.5 in 2025, 61.9 in 2026 so far.

  "how to wear a scarf" peaks in December too (55, against 19 in summer).

  Several of the winners were uploaded in December: UnBlah (7 Dec), ParisianVibe (15 Dec), Life with Elegance
  (25 Dec), Mariana Zelenjuk (29 Dec), Slick and Natty (5 Dec).
- **What viewers wrote under the winners** (public top comments, likes in brackets):
  - "I watched this video, then wore a scarf out. I received compliments on my outfit at the grocery store!" (273, UnBlah)
  - "wonderfully helpful for those of us not raised wearing scarves as accessories as is done in Europe" (264, UnBlah)
  - "I love how scarves are making a comeback! A truly classic article of clothing." (174, Mariana Zelenjuk)
  - "I bought a beautiful silk scarf today and I came here just trying to learn how to wear it" (28, Life with Elegance)
  - "I have some lovely ones people have bought me as gifts, they just sit in the draw, now I know what to do with them" (16, Life with Elegance)
  - viewers wrote out every knot as a numbered list (109) and posted timestamps (100) (Justine Leconte)
- **Your viewers:** one direct request in the 239 stored request comments: "Please make a video on scarf styling please.
  Its my earnest request" (1 like, "How to Dress Casual yet Elegant").

**Interpretation (supported by the above, not proven):**
- The desire is "I own scarves, often gifts, and I don't know how to make them look elegant".
- It is a concrete skill she can try the same day and be complimented on. That matches what your own viewers reward:
  - something to use today;
  - writing the list out;
  - reporting back ("I tried the pause…").
- The scarf is one of the oldest symbols of timeless, French and old-money elegance.

**Not established:** that scarves caused any of these videos' views. Channel size, era (the 2011-2017 "how-to" boom)
and thumbnails also played a part.

## G. Final decision

**Recommended: "8 Elegant Ways to Wear a Scarf".** It passes the historical-evidence threshold and all three novelty
checks.

**Timing.** Publishing in early-to-mid November puts it at the start of the November-January search peak.

**Lens, so it stays yours:**
- the scarf she already owns, any budget;
- women of every age, never "look younger" or "after 60";
- no luxury-brand focus;
- recognisable elegant women (Grace Kelly, Audrey Hepburn, Jackie Kennedy) seen wearing scarves, as visual examples
  only.

This does not promise a million views. The evidence shows the concept has worked repeatedly. It does not show that it
will work again here.
