## 4. The pick

### ★ How to be unforgettable: the small habits of charming women

- **Proven on the reference channel:** it is The Intemporelle's biggest video by far (500,000; 6.7x), and that video
  is this exact topic ("unforgettable", "the science of charm").
- **Proven beyond it:** 13 videos with 1M+ on 11 channels; 25 videos at 4x their own channel or more on 19 channels.
- **Not made yet:** neither of your channels has a video on charm or being unforgettable.
- **Working title:** **7 Tiny Habits That Make a Woman Unforgettable** (45 characters, `title.py` 94/100). It joins
  the reference channel's word ("unforgettable") with the shape of the biggest winners ("5 Tiny Habits That Make You
  Instantly Magnetic", 113x).
  - Others to test: "7 Tiny Habits That Make You Instantly Charming" (94), "8 Habits of Quietly Magnetic Women" (100).
- **The promise:** small things she can do in any conversation so that people remember how she made them feel.
- **Why now:** it has no season, and it goes out just before the weeks of gatherings, dinners and family visits.
- **Your audience on this kind of video** (`fit.py`): your 11 behaviour videos have a usual result of 0.9x, from 3.6x
  down to 0.3x. The winners have a sharp format ("7 Things Elegant Ladies Never Do in Public" 556,000; "10 Signs
  You're an Elegant Woman" 394,000; "7 Body Language Habits That Instantly..." 325,000). The weak ones are the vague
  "presence" videos ("How to Own Any Room You Walk Into" 56,000; "7 Ways to Be More Confident & Elegant in Any Room"
  22,000). So this must be told as concrete habits, never as general "presence".
  - On The Classy Elegance the three behaviour videos did 2.7x, 1.9x and 0.9x of its usual 377 views.
- **The lens:** charm as kindness and attention, at any age. Not attraction, not "make them chase": that half of the
  reference channel is dating advice and does not fit your voice.
- **Footage:** 152 Pinterest video pins from seven searches (two women talking over coffee, dinners with friends,
  cafe scenes, a woman smiling as she walks). Counted, not screened.
- **What the script must do:**
  - take each habit from published research or a written source, read that day, as with every script;
  - give one concrete "instead of X, try Y" per habit;
  - avoid the items already taught in your body-language, speech and "never do in public" videos (to be checked
    against all your scripts before writing).

**Why not the reference channel's second video** ("The 'Her' Effect: Why People Treat Some Women Better Than You",
309,000, 4.1x): Classy Academy already made the respect video in August and it did 0.6x; and "better than you"
compares women, which your viewers push back on.
