## 5. The idea

### ★ The Only 8 Things French Women Wear All Winter

- **Title score:** 46 characters, 94/100 (`title.py`). Titles to put in YouTube's title test:
  1. The Only 8 Things French Women Wear All Winter
  2. 8 Things Elegant French Women Wear All Winter
  3. What French Women Wear All Winter (Only 8 Things)
- **Evidence:** section 4. It crosses the channel's own breakout (French women, 66x) with the wave's biggest live video
  (the winter list, 658k in 22 days on an 8,750-subscriber channel) and with the channel's second-fastest video (winter).
- **Why now:** winter has just begun, the wave is weeks old, and "all winter" stays true until February.
- **Audience fit:**
  - The Classy Elegance: French women is its best topic by 15 times over the next video.
  - Classy Academy: it has never made a French video. `fit.py` puts its what-to-wear videos at a median of 0.7x, with
    its "Never Wear" format at 9.1x. The topic word is new there; the format is its strongest.
  - The lens: timeless pieces you may already own, at any age and on any budget. No red lines in the title.
- **The promise:** eight pieces, and how French women wear them, so a whole winter of outfits comes from a small wardrobe.
- **What must be true in the script:**
  - eight pieces, each named by French stylists or writers in a source read that day;
  - none of the items taught in "7 Things Elegant French Women Never Wear" or "8 Ways to Look Expensive in Winter";
  - each piece told the channel's way: what it replaces, the piece, how to wear it;
  - "the only" softened inside the script to "the eight they come back to".
- **Makeable:** 117 video pins from five Pinterest searches for French and Parisian winter outfits (counted, not yet
  screened by eye).
- **Thumbnail:** two to test against each other.
  - **A, the channel's own winner:** three real Parisian women in winter coats, "FRENCH WOMEN / All Winter", the CE logo.
    This is the layout of the 25,000-view video.
  - **B, the wave's formula:** two halves, a crowded clothes rail with a red cross and one French woman in the eight
    pieces with a green tick, at most two words ("ONLY 8"). Of 24 of the biggest French-style thumbnails, 9 use a tick or
    a cross and 6 are a right-against-wrong pair, including the 658k winter video.

**The series, if this one moves:** each of these has a live winner above.
- 7 Things Elegant French Women Never Wear in Winter
- 8 French Dressing Rules Every Woman Should Know (Mademoiselle Spends, 1.8M, 18x)
- How French Women Get More Elegant With Age (481k and 297k in October)

## 7. The method: what makes an idea win

1. **Your own breakout first.** A video at 10 times your usual or more means YouTube has found an audience for that
   topic on your channel. Make its nearest sibling next.
2. **A live wave on several channels,** small ones included, in the last 30 to 90 days. One channel's hit is that channel.
3. **Multiple, not views.** How far above its own channel's normal did each video go?
4. **Ceiling.** How large has the topic ever gone? It sets the most a video can do.
5. **Season.** Will it still be true in two months?
6. **One viewer, one promise in the title:** a number, who it is about, and "only" or "never".
7. **The thumbnail says the same promise in one second,** readable at phone size.
8. **Makeable:** a real clip for every line.
9. **Repeatable:** a series with a new promise each time.

Why women click this one:
- borrowed authority (French women are the niche's shorthand for effortless elegance);
- relief (only eight things, a whole winter solved);
- fear of getting it wrong, answered kindly;
- "at any age", which lets every viewer see herself.

## 9. Honest limits

- The channel lists give approximate dates ("1 month ago" covers 30 to 59 days), so views per day for older videos are
  approximate.
- YouTube's "this year" and "this month" search filters returned older videos too, so they are not used here; what is
  live comes from dated channel lists.
- Topic counts depend on the search words chosen; every topic got six.
- Small channels that tried the French wave and got nothing are not in the table: it shows the winners.
- The Pinterest pins were counted, not viewed. Screening for AI, captions and rights happens at the video stage.
- No clip is licensed until the creator decides.
