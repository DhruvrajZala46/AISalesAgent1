# Classy Academy: the next video topic (20 Oct 2026)

Topic and title only. No script, hook or outline.

## Recommendation

**7 Things Elegant Women Do to Get More Beautiful With Age**

**The idea.** Some women look more beautiful and more elegant at 50, 60 and 70 than they did at 30. They are not fighting age: they refine how
they present themselves as it changes. The video teaches the specific style and beauty choices these women keep, soften or start as the years
pass, in the channel's calm "instead of this, try this" voice. A viewer of any age then knows what to do now and what to change later.

### Interpretation (not proven)

- **Feed, not search.** The wave titles are curiosity titles ("…Get More Beautiful With Age"), not search phrases: autocomplete does not
  offer the phrase itself. So it should travel through the home feed and suggested videos, as the channel's own winners did.
- **The gap.** Every wave video is framed around France, around "over 60", or around anti-aging. None teaches it as **elegance that grows
  with age, for any decade**, in a calm "instead of X, try Y" voice. That is this channel's lane.
- **Avoid the copycat look.** The two copycat thumbnails ("PRETTIER AT 70?") look like AI images. The channel's version must look and
  sound different: real women, no French report, no "prettier at 70".

## 6. The eight tests

1. **New to the channel?** Yes. No video, Short or transcript addresses age (0.027 mentions per 1,000 words); `fit.py`: "new ground".
2. **In the niche?** Yes: elegant women's style, beauty and presentation.
3. **Real interest?** Yes, from three sources:
   - the live wave (6.5×, 22.5×, 222× in four weeks);
   - 65 age videos at 5× or more from 16 channels;
   - the channel's own most-liked comment (272 likes).
4. **A distinctive angle?** Yes. Elegance that grows with age, for every decade, rather than French culture, an over-60 segment or
   anti-aging products.
5. **Can it carry a full video in the channel's format?** Yes. There is enough material, but the points must avoid the exclusion list
   (no signature scent, posture, quality basics or daily-grooming repeats).
6. **Does a new viewer get it at once?** Yes. Everyone ages, and the promise is concrete.
7. **Is the title accurate and on-brand?** Yes, if the video teaches what women do, never products, procedures or statistics.
8. **Why now?** The wave is four weeks old and still being copied, so it is not saturated yet.

## 7. Limits

- **YouTube Studio was not available,** so there is no CTR, retention, audience age, gender, geography or traffic-source data. The
  channel's real age mix is unknown. The comment sample (top comments on 23 videos) shows women in their 30s to 70s, but it cannot size them.
- **The risk:**
  - The channel's packaging has always shown women in their 20s-40s.
  - An age topic could pull an older, narrower audience.
  - On The Classy Elegance, the thumbnail with only older women (Put Together v1, 3.0% CTR) was the weakest. That is a small sample.
  - So the packaging must stay aspirational and show more than one age.
- **The wave may fade, or fill with copies.** Two channels copied the exact title within two weeks.
- **Multiples measure the idea against each channel's own median, not a promise of views.** Older videos have had more time; publish dates
  in the niche base are month-accurate.
- **Not verified:**
  - search volumes (Trends gives relative interest only; some Trends requests were rate-limited);
  - Reddit and forum discussions (not accessible);
  - medians for four of the search hits (YouTube refused their metadata).
- **Which channel is "yours":** this project has also treated The Classy Elegance as the user's channel. This study follows the request:
