### 3. Why Elegant Women Never Have to Fix Their Clothes

- **Concept.** Many outfits look perfect in the mirror and fail once you sit, reach, walk or wear them for hours: they ride up, gape, dig, cling or crease. The video teaches the cuts and fabrics that stay graceful in motion, so she never tugs or rearranges herself.
- **Overlap check:**
  - "How it moves with you" was said once (Never Wear #3) and never taught.
  - Already covered and kept out: care (steam and polish), undergarments (Cheapen #4) and the daylight check (Cheapen #2).
  - Body Language #4 covers fidgeting hands. This idea covers the clothes that cause it.
- **Demand:**
  - Women search the problems themselves: "how to stop skirt from riding up", "static cling dress", "how to keep shirt tucked in female".
  - The concept is unproven as a video: no competitor video built on this exact idea showed up. It's the least proven of the three and the most original.
- **Caution:** keep it fashion knowledge (fabric behaviour, cut), not "wardrobe hacks". Avoid any "put together" framing.
