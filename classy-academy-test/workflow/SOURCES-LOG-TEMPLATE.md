## Decision rules

- **cleared** - a licence or written permission that covers this exact use (YouTube, monetised, edited), from the
  person or company that owns it, with the evidence saved. Examples: a Pexels/Pixabay clip whose page we read and
  saved; a Mixkit clip under the "Stock Video Free License" (not the "Restricted License"); a creator's written "yes"
  that names the clip and the use; a paid stock licence with its receipt.
- **conditional** - a licence exists but has conditions we must still meet or check at publish time (attribution text,
  editorial-only, territory, no use in ads/thumbnails, model-release limits). List each condition.
- **unresolved** - everything else: "credited only", "found on Pinterest", "it's on YouTube", film or TV excerpts,
  news/interview footage, unknown origin, a re-upload whose original we could not find. Unresolved rows go to the
  creator before delivery, with the risk stated; they are never used silently.

## Facts that do not change the decision

- Being public on Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube is not permission. Pinterest's terms give Pinterest and its
  users a licence to use pins **inside Pinterest**; they do not give a right to download and republish, and they
  restrict automated collection.
- Credit in the description is not permission.
- Cropping, mirroring, slowing down, adding text, a filter or a sound effect does not make a clip ours.
- "Fair use" (US) is a defence a court weighs case by case, not a licence. We do not mark a row "cleared" on a fair-use
  argument; if the creator decides to rely on one, the row stays "conditional" with her decision and reasoning written in.
- Public figures still have publicity/personality rights in many places: no famous face in a thumbnail, ad or
  anything that suggests she endorses us, whatever the footage licence says.
- Public-domain films are public domain only where the law says so (e.g. "Charade", 1963, in the US) and the music or
  story inside them can still be protected. Mark these "conditional" with the territory and the evidence.
