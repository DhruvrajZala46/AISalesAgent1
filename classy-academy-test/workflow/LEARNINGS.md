# Learnings - mistakes made once, never again

Each line is a mistake that reached the user, and the check that now stops it.

| Video | What went wrong | What stops it now |
|---|---|---|
| French, Oct 2026 | The script reported what others say (quotes, named people, "Paris" 16.5 per 1,000 words) instead of teaching in the channel's own voice; too little "why" and inner payoff. | `voice.md` "How I teach" + `tools/dna.py` gate in stage 2. |
| French, Oct 2026 | An AI clip (Gemini ✦ sparkle, "scarf9") got into the final video. | `tools/aiscan.py` on every clip + `build.py` refuses flagged clips. |
| Put together, Oct 2026 | AI scan v1 (one median frame) passed 3 AI clips that scored 0.49-0.58; another came from an uploader named "maheez_gemni". | `aiscan.py` v2 (16 frames, the sparkle must repeat at a known spot; cutoff 0.40 because scarf9 scored 0.56); read every uploader name by eye. |
| French, Oct 2026 | 6 clips had built-in dark flashes that looked like glitches. | `tools/flash.py` after every render. |
| French, Oct 2026 | 7 moments showed the empty caramel backdrop while the voice was talking. | Per-second contact sheet watched end to end + `tools/empty.py` (found 2 more in the put-together video); fix every gap. |
| French, Oct 2026 | Thumbnails v1/v2 did not look like the channel ("worst"). | Measured thumbnail DNA + `tools/thumb_make.py`; real named women only. |
| French, Oct 2026 | The thumbnail underline crossed the "y" of "Style". | Underline sits below the lowest descender (`thumb_make.py`). |
| Winter, Oct 2026 | Too many sound effects and the wrong music. | No sound effects (none in the channel); soft solo piano ~12 dB under, steady. |
| All | Rendering took ~11 minutes per minute of video on this machine. | `tools/ffr.py` fast renderer (~4.3 min per minute), checked against browser frames with `ffr_cmp.py` before every full run. |
| Put together, Oct 2026 | The fast renderer placed the blurred backdrop of full-screen clips 120 px off, so caramel showed at the edges. | Fixed in `ffr.py`; `ffr_cmp.py` must include full-screen moments. |
| Put together, Oct 2026 | The user: the pictures did not flow with the clips before and after; the same format every video. | The "stage" edit (3-VIDEO.md step 5): one calm frame per point, pictures change inside it on the exact word (`words.py` + `at()`), the same woman from mistake to fix, from the 79-video replay study. |
| Dressing rules, Oct 2026 | `dlpicks.py` silently skipped every pick whose tag has a digit inside (`over60chic0`): 11 over-60 clips missing. | Tokens now match any tag + number; check `raw/` against `picks.md` after every download. |
| Dressing rules, Oct 2026 | The fast renderer's capture left each mark's svg visible, so it was baked into later sprites (a tick on the white card 5). | `ffr_capture.js` resets every svg before each capture; `ffr_cmp.py` must include a card and the recap. |
| Dressing rules, Oct 2026 | A clip with no watermark was AI (redcoat0): pin title ending in a leftover "Pinterest Description:", an amzn.to affiliate link, exactly 15.15 s at 24 fps, the handbag changing size between frames. | Check every clip's pin title, source link and length (AI tools give 5/6/8/10/15 s at 24 fps) and look at three full-size frames for objects that change; the watermark scan alone is not enough. |
| Body shape research, Oct 2026 | YouTube refused the study downloads with the cookies ("Sign in to confirm you're not a bot", 403) after a few videos. | Fetch with the embedded player and no cookies: `yt-dlp --skip-download --write-auto-subs --write-comments --write-info-json --extractor-args "youtube:player_client=web_embedded;max_comments=100,100,0,0;comment_sort=top"`; if one still fails, retry later with `tv_embedded` or `mweb`, 20-40 s apart. Stop at once when blocks start. |
| Dressing rules, Oct 2026 | The user: the pictures still did not flow with the clips before and after them, and did not hold attention ("you are just following the same format"). | The "demo" edit (3-VIDEO.md step 5, `videos/body-shape/edit-evidence.md`): one real woman per point from mistake to fix, the tip drawn on her at the word, a tick/cross list building beside her; from the replay peaks of the 13 body-shape winners (labelled hand test 10.7x). |
| Body shape, Oct 2026 | A camel-coat street walk (coatopen47) passed the watermark scan AND the metadata check (30 fps, 14.5 s, Instagram source) but was AI: waxy skin, hair and coat changing between frames. | Look at three full-size frames of EVERY clip that is cinematic, perfect-light, or model-like (`aieye.py` sheet) before it goes in; when in doubt, leave it out. |
| Body shape, Oct 2026 | Pinterest searches without the word "video" returned almost no video pins (0-1 per search); the disk filled up mid-download (167 clips, 2.4 GB). | Put "video" in every query; check `df -h` before sourcing, delete unused raw clips once the picks are final; old renders and clip caches of delivered videos can go. |
| Body shape, Oct 2026 | The soft drop shadow behind each cut-out woman was blurred inside her own box, so it stopped in a hard vertical edge beside her (in the Dressing Rules thumbnails too). | `thumb_xo.shadow()` pads before the blur; `thumb_shape.py` uses it. Zoom to 2x beside each woman before sending. |
| Never say, Oct 2026 | Older Mixkit clips are "Restricted License - 720p for personal use only" (not allowed on a YouTube channel); only the "Stock Video Free License" ones can be used. | Before using a Mixkit clip, check the 1080p file exists (`<id>-1080.mp4` returns 200 only for the free-licence clips) and the page says "commercial or personal use". |
| Never say, Oct 2026 | The disk filled during the final audio mux of a 10-minute render (the scratchpad holds every older video's raw clips). | `df -h` before every full render; delete raw clips and node_modules of delivered videos first. |
| Never say, Oct 2026 | The script taught a different subject (courtesy phrases) in a different unit (phrases) from the creator's own speech video; a one-off line of her #1 video ("We get it") had become a step for every point; filler words were added to pass `dna.py`/`fit.py`, which were built only from wardrobe videos. | `CHANNEL-DNA.md` read first; the teaching architecture (habits + the 8 value answers) approved before any script; checkers are warnings, never a reason to add a phrase (`CHANNEL-DNA-diagnosis.md`). |
| Never say, Oct 2026 | I described competitors' and the creator's visuals without looking at the frames, and asked the user for materials I could find myself. | Every claim about footage names what was inspected; trace sources yourself (`tools/revsearch.py`, `source-trace-2026-10-17.md`); say plainly what stays blocked (YouTube refuses video downloads here). |
| Channel DNA, Oct 2026 | Automatic reverse-image scores on 160x90 frames matched unrelated pages at 0.9+; my storyboard tracer also skipped blank tiles without counting them, shifting every timestamp by one frame. | Confirm every match by eye on a review sheet before labelling it; count every storyboard tile, blank or not, and check one known frame (a white card) against its time. |
| Part 2 (speak), Oct 2026 | 23 of 49 Mixkit clips picked by eye were Restricted License; the browser render needs the raw-free disk (frames streamed, but extraction used ~0.6 GB). | Run the `-1080.mp4` licence check on the whole candidate list BEFORE picking; `df -h` > 4 GB before `hyperframes render`. |
| Never Do in Public copy, Oct 2026 | The same footage came back under two different Pinterest pins (re-pins of one reel), so the "each clip once" check by key missed it: 3 pairs reached the first build. | `tools/dupall.py check used.txt`: every frame hashed (1 per second); re-pinned footage is grouped and any group used twice is listed. Must say "clashes among used clips: 0" before rendering. |
| Never Do in Public copy, Oct 2026 | Full-screen 16:9 crops of vertical reels at a fixed height showed hands or a coat instead of the woman's face. | `tools/facepos.py` (face height per shot) and `_fy()` in the edit: the window keeps the face at 38% of the screen. Montage reels with changing framing go in the rounded vertical frame (layout D) instead. Snapshot every full-screen shot before rendering. |
| Never Do in Public copy, Oct 2026 | Pinterest now returns encrypted pin ids, so `pin_search.py` leaves the pin URL empty and the credits had no links. | Credit the creator's handle and the pin's outbound Instagram/TikTok link (`src`) in `SOURCES-LOG.md` and `CREDITS.txt`. |
| Never Do in Public copy, Oct 2026 | Pinterest searches return many AI and ad clips that pass the watermark scan: fertility, song-gift and perfume ads, "too perfect" café scenes. Two royalty-free stock clips also came through Pinterest (Kmeel, rawpixel). | Read the uploader and pin title of every used clip, look at three full-size frames of every used segment (`eyeall` sheet), and drop stock-site clips when the creator asks for creators' footage only. |
| Never Do in Public copy, Oct 2026 | The renderer could not find Chrome in a fresh container. | `export HYPERFRAMES_BROWSER_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1243/chrome-headless-shell-linux64/chrome-headless-shell` before `npx hyperframes`. |
| Never Do in Public copy, Oct 2026 | The first full render ran at ~1 frame/s (the live 90 px CSS blur of the caramel backdrop, redrawn every frame), would have needed ~4 h and was then stopped by the 2 h job limit; a later run stalled at one frame. | Pre-render the backdrop once as `assets/bg.png` and swap the `.bg` CSS for it (matched the live blur at mean diff 0.5/255); render with `HF_SEGMENTED_CAPTURE=true` and `--resume` so a stall only re-renders the unfinished segments (~45 min for 9 min). Do not detach the render with setsid: it cancels itself ("render_cancelled_parent_exited"). |
| Never Do in Public copy, Oct 2026 | The word-timed QA sheets of the first render showed a stock-ad clip (a ticket kiosk with a brand screen), a hospital waiting room that read as wrong, empty halves of cream screens, one torso-only crop and two black frames inside source clips. | Watch `wordsheet.py` sheets end to end before delivering, plus `flash.py`; cut around a source's black frame by splitting the clip into two segments. |
