## Method (no titles invented)

1. **All-time niche data:** up to 500 latest videos per channel from 59 channels = 13,676 long-form videos (one channel
   failed). Men's, beauty-only, mindset and vlog channels removed, leaving **12,246 lane videos**.
2. Every video is scored as a multiple of its own channel's median.
3. **Topics were found by the data, not by me:** every 1-3 word phrase in the titles was counted. A phrase stays only
   with 8+ videos and 5x+ winners on 3+ channels.
4. Each surviving topic is compared with the lane baseline: **median 1.0x, 27% of videos reach 2x, 10.6% reach 5x.**
5. **Search demand:** the top 25 YouTube search results for the topic's own words (views of long videos).
6. **Our audience:** `fit.py idea` on every title, plus our 29 transcripts.

## The 3 ideas, ranked

### 1. ★ Little luxuries

- **Title options:**
  - "12 Little Luxuries Elegant Women Enjoy Every Day" (94/100).
  - The proven combined form: "10 Small Luxuries That Make You Instantly More Attractive" (94/100). Two channels
    already won with this exact form: Allison Baek 13.2x (76k), Return to Elegance 4.9x (37k).
- **Evidence:**
  - Old Money Femininity, "The Small Luxuries That Change How People See You": 12.5x.
  - Mirella, "How to create a chic lifestyle | little luxuries": 9.6x (210k).
  - Mirella, "Little luxuries to upgrade your life": 6.0x (131k).
  - Madre, "14 Little Luxuries to ELEVATE Your Daily Life": 5.7x.
  - Search: Tim Dessaint 1.85M, Christina Mychas 440k ("that don't cost a lot").
- **Why now / why unique:** 33 of the 35 niche videos are from the last 12 months. It is a new wave, and few elegance
  channels have done it yet.
- **Audience fit:** new ground for us, with no overlap with any video we have made. It matches what our viewers already
  reward:
  - budget / "not about cost" (39 comments, 455 likes);
  - understated, anti-logo (11 comments, 666 likes);
  - timeless (39 comments, 767 likes).
- **The lens:** luxury is care, not price. Small everyday rituals that cost little or nothing, and no brand names (a red
  line). Never "upper class vs middle class".
- **Promise:** small, affordable everyday rituals that make her days feel elegant and make her feel cared for.

### 2. Attractive without changing your looks

- **Audience fit:** new ground, and already in our voice. Our transcripts say "quietly attractive" and "kindness is
  one of the most attractive things you can wear".
- **Flagged plainly:** it sits close to our presence videos, which are weak right now ("How to Own Any Room" 0.38x).
  The content must not repeat body language or confidence. Second for that reason.
