## The one shared structure

A numbered list that sorts things into "what quietly works against you" and "what to do instead", aimed at a woman who
wants to look graceful **as she gets older**. Age-and-style is the biggest shape in the niche: 426 videos in 12 months,
**51 at 5x+ from 14 channels**. We have **never** made a video on it.

## The 3 ideas, ranked

### 1. ★ 8 Things That Quietly Age Your Look (Wear These Instead) - title score 94/100

- **Evidence:** the age shape (51 winners at 5x+, 14 channels). Live right now: Chic After 60 "French Women Get More
  Beautiful With Age" 28.0x (252k, Oct 2026), "Leggings Over 60" 78.1x (703k, Oct 2026). Same season last year: "These
  Clothes After 50 Are Aging You" 25.7x (99k), Marie-Anne Lecoeur "7 Things That Scream 'Old Lady'" 16.0x (1.0M).
- **Why now:** a live wave (several channels at 25x+ this month) and it won in the same season last year.
- **Audience fit:** new ground for us (0 videos). Our wardrobe videos have a median of 0.7x, but this reuses our #1
  format, "Never Wear: What to Wear Instead" (9.1x, 1.2M). Our viewers already talk about age (29 comments, 476 likes:
  "I am 74... elegance should not end with age").
- **The lens (must):** kind, never "old lady" shaming. The blame is on a fixable detail (a colour near the face, a
  fabric, a fit), never on her age or body. Told for **every age**, so younger viewers stay: "elegance doesn't end with
  age, it gets deeper". Every point is a simple swap, often something she already owns.
- **Promise:** she will know the small, everyday choices that quietly add years, and the timeless swap for each.

### 2. 7 Things Elegant Ladies Never Do at the Dinner Table - title score 94/100

- **Evidence:** etiquette / table manners: Mademoiselle Spends "French Table Manners EVERYONE Should Learn" 7.7x (688k);
  Annie Parker "10 NEW Modern Etiquette Rules Every Polished Woman Knows" 7.7x (62k, Oct 2026, live). All-time (16 Oct
  research): 33 winners at 5x+ from 12 channels, up to 7.2M (123x); like rate 4.39%, among the most-loved in the niche.
- **Why now:** the holiday dinners (Thanksgiving, Christmas). Publish in early-mid November.
- **Audience fit:** new ground. Reuses our "Never Do in Public" format (3.6x, 507k). Our viewers asked for it directly:
  "Manners definitely are the most memorable impression..." (32 likes), "Etiquette please", "please post more on
  etiquette, holiday style". The habit is the teaching unit, which is our DNA.
- **The lens:** "consideration for the people at your table", grandmother's manners; never upper class vs middle class.
- **Promise:** she will sit down at her next dinner calm and sure of every small gesture.
- **If timing matters more than size, make this one first:** after December the season is gone.

### 3. 8 Elegant Shoes for Women Who Hate Heels - title score 100/100

- **Evidence:** a live wave of the same title on two channels in Sept 2026: Chic After 60 29.0x (261k), Mademoiselle
  Spends 6.5x (579k); Elvira Hartwell "10 Shoes Elegant Women Over 50 Should Own" 11.6x. Shoes: 7 at 5x+ from 6 channels.
- **Audience fit, flagged plainly:** our only shoe video ("6 Elegant Summer Shoes") did **0.5x**, and the niche's like
  rate on shoe videos is low (0.71-2.10%). Views are proven; love is not. Third for that reason.
- **Promise:** comfortable flats and low heels that still look graceful and expensive.

## Honest limits

- Publish dates are month-accurate.

- The formula label describes the title, not why the video worked.
- "Unique" means new for **our** channel; the topics are proven elsewhere, which is the point.
- Nothing here guarantees a viral video. These are the topics with the most proof.
