## PART TWO: 5 TRENDS THAT ALMOST NEVER LOOK ELEGANT

**Now, the five trends elegant women tend to quietly skip, or soften.**
[ON SCREEN: white card "5 to quietly soften"]

**It's not that they're wrong. It's that, worn all at once, they rarely look elegant in real life.**
[ON SCREEN: quote beside a clip: "worn all at once, they rarely look *elegant*"]

### ✕ 1. Animal print from head to toe
**Number one. Animal print from head to toe.**
[ON SCREEN: ✕ card, then a look in leopard top, skirt and coat]

**Leopard and zebra are back, and it's tempting to wear them all at once.**
[ON SCREEN: row of three print-on-print looks building]

**If you love animal print, you don't have to give it up. Choose one piece, a leopard scarf, a belt or a pair of flats, and keep the rest neutral.**
[ON SCREEN: compare: "All at once" | "One piece" (camel coat + leopard scarf); cut-outs: leopard scarf, belt, flats]

### ✕ 2. Shrunken, skin-tight tailoring
**Number two. Shrunken, skin-tight tailoring.**
[ON SCREEN: ✕ card, then a very cropped, very tight suit look]

**After years of oversized shapes, fashion is swinging back to very tight, cropped silhouettes.**
[ON SCREEN: in-panel swap: an oversized blazer → a shrunken, tight blazer]

**Elegance is fitted, but never tight. Choose tailoring that skims your shape and leaves room to move.**
[ON SCREEN: compare: "Restrictive" | "Refined" (a blazer that skims, straight trousers)]

### ✕ 3. The open bag
**Number three. The open bag.**
[ON SCREEN: ✕ card, then a close-up of an open tote with things spilling out]

**One of the biggest bag trends this season is the open tote, with everything inside on display.**
[ON SCREEN: centred clip of a woman walking with an open bag]

**Wallets, keys, receipts, all visible. A structured bag that closes always looks more put together, and keeps your things safe in busy winter crowds.**
[ON SCREEN: compare: open tote | structured top-handle bag closing with a soft click]

### ✕ 4. Dark romance taken too far
**Number four. Dark romance taken too far.**
[ON SCREEN: ✕ card, then a gothic look: lace corset, long black lace skirt]

**Gothic lace, corsets and dramatic black are everywhere this year.**
[ON SCREEN: row of three dark-romance looks]

**Worn all together, it can feel like a costume. One touch of black lace, like a lace-trim camisole under a blazer, gives the same mood with far more grace.**
[ON SCREEN: compare: full gothic look | blazer over a lace-trim cami; cut-out: lace camisole]

### ✕ 5. Maximalist knits
**Number five. Maximalist knits.**
[ON SCREEN: ✕ card, then a knit with ribbons, holes and stripes]

**Knitwear went bold this season, with ribbons, cut-out holes, giant V-necks and busy stripes, sometimes all in one sweater.**
[ON SCREEN: labels pop on one busy knit: ribbons / holes / giant V-neck / stripes]

**When a knit is doing everything at once, it overwhelms the woman wearing it. Choose one detail instead, a soft cable, a single stripe or a beautiful neckline.**
[ON SCREEN: row of three calm knits labelled cable / stripe / neckline]
