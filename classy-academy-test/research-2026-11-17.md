## 2. The pick: ★ Princess Diana's 8 Timeless Style Rules

**Why it beats the others for the most views:**
1. **The most proven subject on YouTube for a style video about a woman:** 6 Diana style videos with 1M+, more than
   any other elegant woman; 15 of 21 did 3x their channel or more. And the exact kind of video you make, a list of
   tips she can use, has the biggest single result: 2.37M at 121 times its channel.
2. **Your own channels reward lists of rules from a group or a woman she admires** ("Elegant Ladies Never Wear" 1.2M on
   Classy Academy; "7 Things Elegant French Women Never Wear" 73x on The Classy Elegance), and your viewers are women
   40-70+ who grew up with Diana.
3. **Not made by you, and not a repeat of the charm video.**
4. **Real footage exists** (archive film of Diana on Pinterest), which your charm video already proved usable.

**Why not "prettier without changing your face":** it is the stronger topic inside the 59 elegance channels (2.16x), but
it sits very close to the charm video you have just uploaded ("it isn't about looks"). It is the best next one after
this.

**Honest limit:** The Intemporelle has no Diana video, so the proof for this topic comes from YouTube and your own
channels, not from the reference channel. The reference channel gives the script's format.
