**Flag, said plainly:** `fit.py` puts our topic median at **0.7×** across 11 nearby videos. Two of

- "8 Outfit Mistakes That Cheapen Your Look": 0.2×
- "10 Grooming Habits Elegant Women Never Skip": 0.5×

*mistakes* and as *grooming*. This one must be the positive "how to", about outfits and finishing
details, not makeup or nails. It must not repeat those two videos' tips.

**Teaching angle:** she'll know the 8 small, almost-free details that make any simple outfit look
put together:
1. fit before label
2. two colours
3. one good shoe
4. a bag that belongs to the look
5. one finishing detail
6. a long vertical line
7. fabric that holds its shape
8. caring for what she owns

She'll also get a 10-minute evening routine that makes it automatic. It works on any budget and at
any age.

**Why now:** the wave is live this month on three channels, and this same season gave the 75.6×

## Dropped, and why
- **"How to Dress Like Jennifer Aniston" (26.5×), "She's 68 in Paris" (15.9×), "French Women Get
  More Beautiful With Age" (25.2×):** these can only be told as a report on a person or a
  culture. That's not how we teach, and the French video is already in the queue.
- **Fall / autumn trends (51.9×, 14.4×):** told as fashion news. The Winter Trends script is
  already written, and our viewers give seasonal wardrobe topics about 0.7×.
- **"Never Wear" (22.9×, 22.8×, 19.7×, 18.4×, 16.2×):** still the strongest format in the niche
  and our #1 (9.1×). But two Never Wear videos are already in the queue. Come back to it in about
  a month.
- **Ageless "over 50 / 60" (58 winners from 17 channels):** the biggest shape in the niche. But we
  have no video on it, and an "over 50" title narrows an audience aged 20s to 70s. Used as a lens
  instead: idea 1 works at any age.
- **Habits (32 winners):** many are off-lane (makeup, money), and our 11 habit videos have a
  median of 0.9×.
- **"Sleeveless Dresses at 60+" (51.3×):** a summer topic, wrong season.
