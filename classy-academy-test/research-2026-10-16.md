## How it was found

- Fresh lists from 59 niche channels (3,379 long videos, today) + all-time (14,127 videos), every video scored as a
  multiple of its own channel's median (`content-idea`: collect.py, rank.py).

  the top comments (quoted, wrote out, tried, thanked, pushed back), from the 49-winner study
  (`research-2026-10-12-craft.md`, 871 tips tagged) + 9 shoe winners pulled today.
- Our own 29 videos and 2,685 comments (`audience_proof.py`, `fit.py`).
- Left out: everything we already made (Never Wear, French, put together, dressing rules, body shape, winter, colour
  combos, habits, jewelry, jeans, quiet luxury, hairstyles, look expensive).

## How the script's teaching is proven (stage 2, unchanged, with today's additions)

1. The 8-12 winners on the exact topic, chosen by views AND like rate: transcript + top 200 comments each.
2. Every tip listed with its timestamp and exact words; how it is taught (demo, example, test, story).
3. A tip stays only with **two proofs**: taught by 2+ winners, or 1 winner + our viewers' love/requests, or
   written out / tried / thanked by viewers with 10+ likes. Its top pushback becomes its exception line.
4. Each point: name -> validate -> why -> principle -> what elegant women do -> **show it** -> the swap -> payoff;
   one thing she can do today; one quotable line; kind, never shame.
5. `fit.py check` = FITS THE CHANNEL; hook score STRONG; then the user approves before any footage.

Limits: like rates and comment likes are signals, not certainties; older videos had more time; faceless channels get
fewer likes than on-camera ones (our 2.89% median is already 2.5x faceless competitors); the shoe love sample is 9 videos.
