# Editing breakdowns of the creator's top videos (17 Oct 2026)

**Videos:** the five best-liked uploads after the reference (`reference-breakdown-RUDXBR8nyHw.md` has the
second-by-second blueprint of the reference). One table per video, one row per YouTube chapter.

**How each table was made.**
- **Layout sequence:** YouTube storyboard, one frame every ~5 s, labelled by eye. "fullx4" = four consecutive
  frames full-screen.
  - full = full-screen clip
  - framed = one clip on caramel/cream
  - row = 2-4 clips
  - panels = photo panels
  - caramel = backdrop with a sentence or empty
  - white = white card
- **Text frames:** frames with on-screen text out of the chapter's frames.
- **Swaps:** how many times the narration says "instead of" (auto-captions).
- **Replay:** the "most replayed" heatmap, mean and max for the chapter, divided by the video's median.
- **Footage:** the reverse-image trace of 14 frames per video (`source-trace-2026-10-17.md`).

**Not measured: cut points, motion, transitions and sound.** The files could not be downloaded here: YouTube asks
this machine to sign in.
