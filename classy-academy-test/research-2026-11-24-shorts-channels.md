# Shorts channels in our niches that reach 1M+ views (24 Nov 2026)

The user's ask: channels in our niche (elegant woman, fashion tips, old money / quiet luxury, feminine energy and
micro-habits, etiquette and classy behaviour, elegant lifestyle) whose Shorts get millions of views. Goal of the
Shorts now: sell an elegance / fashion-rules ebook, not views alone.

## Method

- 32 YouTube searches sorted by views + 20 channel searches, plus the 60 niche channels already tracked:
  1,121 channels, the last 60-100 Shorts of each read from their own Shorts tab (public listings, no login).
- A Short counts as "in our lane" only if its title is about elegance, classy behaviour, old money, feminine energy,
  etiquette, women's outfits or style rules (men's, gaming, recipes, Indian ethnic hauls taken out).
- A channel is listed only if at least 30% of its Shorts are in the lane, so one lucky video doesn't count.
- Limits: view counts are YouTube's rounded numbers; only the newest 60-100 Shorts per channel; YouTube search shows
  what it chooses, so some channels will be missing.

## What the winners share

- **Etiquette is the strongest repeat performer**: four of the five channels with repeated 1M+ lane Shorts teach one
  etiquette rule per Short ("Never answer with you're welcome", "Never put your handbag on the table").
- **"Never" + one rule**, said in the first second, by a real person showing it.
- **Old money / quiet luxury and feminine energy**: big single hits, few channels that repeat them.
- **Pure fashion tips for women**: many channels, few 1M Shorts; typical lane Short 10k-60k.
