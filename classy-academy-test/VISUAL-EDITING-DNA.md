## 4. Editing DNA (layouts and rhythm)

**The five teaching layouts [O, reference + 10 Microhabits]:**
1. **White card, habit in black script** ("1. Slow Down the Pace", "3 Replying Elegantly", "4 Text like a Lady"):
   one per habit, about one storyboard frame (~3-5 s). Also used for one word ("Like") and for the payoff sentence.
2. **Full sentence on the blurred caramel backdrop** (serif + script): the reason or the payoff, held 5-10 s.
3. **Rounded-corner clip on cream with the phrase in script beside it**: the exact-words layout. The clip changes
   with each phrase while the layout stays (3:03-3:37: six frames, about 35 s, one layout).
4. **Full-screen clip**: the named woman, a film scene, an emotional beat; also the hook.
5. **Rows/collages of 3-4 women**: "most of us", groups, the texting triptych.

**Hook pattern [O, all 30 uploads]:**
1. 0:00-0:25: full-screen film/lifestyle clips of women;
2. then the title card in script over a clip ("How to Speak Elegantly", "Episode 5 / 8 Things elegant women never
   wear");
3. then the first habit card at about 0:25.

**Rhythm [O, storyboard grid]:**

| Measure | Reference |
|---|---|
| New picture per minute | 10.6 (a lower bound: the 5 s grid misses faster cuts) |
| Layout changes per minute | 6.6 |
| Frames with text on screen | 45% (the highest of her speech videos) |
| Layout mix | full-screen 39%; framed single clip 23%; white cards 20%; caramel cards 12%; rows 3%; panels 1% |

**Cut lengths [O, the two measured wardrobe videos]:**
- #1 video: median shot 6.3 s (many long-held layouts);
- Old Money: median 3.8 s;
- cuts fall on sentence boundaries, and a new image lands on the noun being said.

**Replay data on her layouts [O, 24 uploads, `research-2026-10-12-edit-evidence.md`]:**

- Full-screen clips held 5 s or more sit below trend.
- The guide plug and the static end card are dips. The reference's 40 s white end card falls to 0.0x.

## 5. Motion DNA

**Measured on the two wardrobe videos [O]** (`how-you-edit.md`):
- **Video start:** fade in from black, 1.0 s.
- **Between clips:** short dissolves.
- **Before a number card:** fade to white, 0.4 s.
- **Number-card text:** wipes in left to right with a soft edge (about 1-1.5 s), holds about 3 s, fades out 0.5 s.
- **Panels:** grow from their top-left corner (about 55% to 100%) while fading in, 0.6-0.8 s, one per spoken item.
- **A centred vertical clip:** grows from the centre, 1.2 s.
- **Caramel backdrop:** drifts slowly.
- **Never:** zoom punches, shakes, spins, glitch.

**In the reference [O1, storyboard only]:**
- At least six storyboard frames catch a clip half-dissolved into cream: 0:49, 3:32, 4:32, 5:31, 5:36, 5:46. So
  soft dissolves to and from cream are frequent there too.
- The framed clips have rounded corners and a soft shadow.
- Their entrance animation is **not measured**.

## 6. Sound DNA

**Measured on the two wardrobe videos [O]** (Demucs voice/music separation, `edit-style-measured.md`):
- one soft, sparse solo-piano bed about 11.5-13 dB under the voice;
- no ducking: the music is the same level in pauses;
- the music starts with the first word and ends about 5 s after the last;
- **no sound effects** (no whooshes, clicks or dings; transition gaps at -36 to -42 dBFS).

**In the reference [O1, captions]:**
- "[music]" is tagged under the voice from 0:15 to the last line, so a music bed runs throughout.
- Film and interview audio is kept when the woman's voice is the lesson: the "Breakfast at Tiffany's" taxi whistle
  (captioned "[screaming]") and the line "I never could do that" / "It's easy" at 0:36-0:40; Diana's own words
  about a group session at 1:47-1:54.
- Whether there are sound effects: **[?] not measured.**

**Voice [O1]:**
- female, warm, unhurried phrasing;
- 186 words/min in the reference, 146-159 in her other top videos, 132 in the #1 wardrobe video;
- the calm comes from short sentences and pauses, not from a slow voice.
