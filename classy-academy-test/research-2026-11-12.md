# Classy Academy: the next idea after the hairstyles video (12 Nov 2026)

The user asked again for the one idea with the highest odds of a 10M video, and for the method. This run builds on
`research-2026-11-11.md` and fixes its weak point: yesterday hair got 16 dedicated searches and every other topic two or
three. Today every topic got the same depth, and each was checked for what is live this year.

**No idea can guarantee views.** Section 6 says how long the odds are, and what moves them.

## 1. How it was found

- **Fresh niche lists** (`content-idea` skill, 12 Nov 2026): 3,167 long videos from 55 tracked elegance channels (5
  channels failed to load), each scored as a multiple of its own channel's median.
- **Equal depth for every topic:** 16 topics in or next to the lane, 8 YouTube searches each (128 searches, 50 results
  each), counted for long videos from individual creators (content farms, lectures, men's and children's videos removed).
- **What is live now:** 3 more searches per topic with YouTube's own "this year, most viewed" filter (48 searches), and
  8 of the same for hair.
- **Hair in depth:** the 70 hair searches of 11 and 12 Nov (1,320 long hair videos from individual creators), cut by
  sub-topic.
- **Base rates:** how often any video beats its own channel by 8x, 30x and 80x.
- **Makeability:** Pinterest video-pin searches for the three ideas, sheets read by eye.
- **Audience fit:** `fit.py` against Classy Academy's own videos. Title lint: `title.py`.
- vidIQ was not used (no credits until 12 Dec 2026). The channel's comments could not be read here.

## 4. The one shared structure

The 10M videos a small faceless channel can make all have the same shape: **a small, finished result that anyone can
copy in minutes, shown from behind, several versions in one video, followable with the sound off.** That is why they
travel beyond one language and one niche, and why they keep collecting views for years.

This year's version of it is narrow and clear: **the low bun, done several quick ways, often with one claw clip, for work
and busy mornings.** The thumbnails are a set of finished buns from behind on a bright ground, with no words.

## 6. How long the odds are, and what moves them

Classy Academy's median is 121k, so 10M means **83 times its usual video**.

| Where | Videos | 8x+ | 30x+ | 80x+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 elegance channels (latest 60 each) | 3,167 | 130 (4.1%) | 21 (0.7%) | **3 (0.1%)** |
| Hair channels (latest 120 each) | 6,088 | 531 (8.7%) | 162 (2.7%) | **69 (1.1%)** |

- The hair channels were picked because they already had a hit, so their line flatters. Even so: choosing the hair lane
  moves the odds from about 1 in 1,000 to somewhere near 1 in 100 for one video. It is still a long shot.
- The channels that reached 10M made many hair videos. A series is what turns 1 in 100 into a real chance.
- Classy Academy's own best is 9.9x.

**What decides whether an idea can travel that far (the method):**
1. **Ceiling.** Has the topic reached 10M for several different creators, small ones included, and this year?
2. **Multiple, not views.** How far above its own channel's normal did each video go?
3. **Size of the need.** Everyone with hair, or only women who follow elegance?
4. **No language needed.** Can it be followed with the sound off?
5. **A set of quick results.** A number, "easy", a time, in the title; the results themselves in the thumbnail.
6. **Our own viewers.** Have they rewarded the topic above 1x?
7. **Timing.** Is it winning this year, in this season?
8. **Makeable.** A real clip for every step.
9. **Repeatable.** Can it be a series with a new promise each time?

**The plan this points to:** make elegant hair a pillar, every second or third video, each with one new promise (buns,
then ponytails, then shoulder-length hair), alternated with the channel's proven "Elegant Ladies Never" format. After each
one, read its first week in YouTube Studio and keep the sub-topic that beats 4x.

## 7. Honest limits

- Flat search listings carry no upload date; "this year" is YouTube's own filter.
- Older videos have had more years to collect views. A 10M total is usually built over several years.
- Topic counts depend on the search words chosen; every topic got eight.
- Medians come from each channel's latest videos, so multiples on old hits are inflated.
- The channel's own analytics and comments were not available.
- The user called hair off-niche on 11 Nov, then chose to make the hair video. Idea 2 is the pick if the channel must
  stay strictly inside elegance; its 10M odds are lower.
