## 3. The ideas, ranked

### ⭐ 1. 8 Things Elegant Women Never Say
`title.py` 100/100, 32 characters. Alternative: "8 Phrases Elegant Women Use Every Day" (100).

- **Proven by both sides:** the niche (11.2x, 6.5x, 5.5x, 5.0x, 18.9x on speech) and our viewers
  (our best like rate, 4.9%; 46 thank-you comments; the most specific requests: disrespect, manners,
  speaking, writing, texting).
- **Our #1 format:** "Never Wear: What to Wear Instead" (9.1x) becomes "Never Say: What to Say
  Instead". Every point is a swap, which is how we teach (our scripts use "instead / try" more
  than any other marker).
- **New ground:** after four outfit videos, a different kind of value that earns trust and shares.
- **Teaching angle:** she'll know the 8 small phrases that quietly cost a woman her grace (deflecting
  a compliment, over-explaining a "no", "What?", answering rudeness in kind…) and exactly what to say
  instead, in real moments: at work, with family, with friends, in messages.
- **Fit check:** our nearest videos are "Sound More Elegant" 2.4x and "Never Do in Public" 3.6x.
  Fewer views than an outfit topic is possible; more likes, thanks and shares are what the data
  says it buys.

## 4. How the script will be proven (the proof map)

Before a word of the script, every candidate tip gets a row:

| Tip | Niche proof (which winners teach it) | Our viewers (quoted / thanked / asked, likes) | Pushback | Keep? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

A tip is kept only with **two proofs**: taught by 2+ niche winners, or by 1 winner plus our viewers'
love or requests, or quoted/thanked by our viewers with 10+ likes. Tips with pushback are rewritten
to answer it. You see the proof map before the script.

**Proof so far** (our own data):
- Receiving a compliment with a simple "thank you": two comments (14 + 11 likes) - proven by our viewers.
- Not over-explaining a "no": 128-like comment + our own "soft no" section - proven; new angle needed.
- "Pardon?" instead of "What?" / "Say that again": a 10-like pushback chose "Pardon?" - proven, fixes our old tip.
- Answering rudeness calmly: 52-like request - wanted; the exact phrases need niche proof.
