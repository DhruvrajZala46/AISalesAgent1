## 5. Honest limits

- **The exact title has been copied a lot.** In six weeks, 36 copies on 33 channels could be scored. With every copy
  counted: usual result 1.14x; 13 of 36 at 2x or more; 7 at 4x or more; 12 under 0.7x. So the title gave about a
  one-in-three chance of doubling a channel's usual video, against one in five for a normal video. It is an edge, not
  a promise.
- **On very small channels the views were small.** Channels under 2,000 subscribers (yours has 445): 18 copies, usual
  result 0.96x, 5 at 2x or more, usual 270 views, best 3,497. The big numbers came from channels that already had an
  audience. Your own French video (25,000) shows a small channel can break out, but that is one video.
- **"French" alone proves nothing:** 83 French titles in six months have a usual result of 1.06x. The French titles
  about beauty and age together are 21 videos on only 5 channels, usual 0.85x, with a long tail of winners (24% at 4x
  or more) and many losers (48% under 0.7x). The proof is the "more beautiful" promise (section 3), with French as the
  lens your viewers already chose once.
- **The Intemporelle has not made this topic.** Its nearest video is "7 Rules That Give You Pretty Privilege" (1.4x).
  The script copies its format, not a video of its own on this subject.
- **The winners could not be watched or read**, so the seven rules do not come from them. They come from interviews
  with Inès de la Fressange, Mireille Guiliano and Caroline de Maigret, read today (script, section B).
- Publish dates are month-accurate. Older videos have had more time to collect views. Two copies from the last three
  days are too new to judge. Diana (uploaded today) and the charm video (80 views, uploaded today) cannot be judged
  yet either.
