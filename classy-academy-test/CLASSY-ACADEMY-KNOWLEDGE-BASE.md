Reusable for every script. It is built from the creator's own words: 30 long-form transcripts in
`.claude/skills/content-idea/audience/transcripts/`, read in full for the wardrobe videos named below. Our
viewers' words come from 2,076 top comments on all 31 long videos, re-fetched today, public only. It sits on top
of `CHANNEL-DNA.md` (teaching moves, habits and speech videos), `CHANNEL-DNA-diagnosis.md` and `voice.md`.
Where they disagree, CHANNEL-DNA wins for habit and speech videos; this file wins for wardrobe and "what you
own" videos.

**What elegance is NOT, in her words:**
- being fully covered ("elegance doesn't mean being fully covered", Never Wear);
- expensive or designer;
- loud branding, or "too many trends at once";
- being perfect ("The goal isn't perfection", At Home);
- following strict rules ("elegant styling isn't about following strict rules", Jewelry).

**Expensive-looking vs elegant** [I]: she uses "expensive" as the viewer's search word (8 of her titles), but she
teaches it as the effect of intention: fit, fabric, coordination and care. She never teaches it as price. She
never says "rich".

## 2. How she reasons (the thinking behind a tip)

Patterns repeated across the wardrobe videos [E]:
1. **She names the visible result, then the cause.** "it loses its polish" → "rips, frays, or fading that looks
   accidental" (Never Wear #4); "the whole outfit can feel a little off" → the two colours fight (Outfit Mistakes).
2. **The cause is almost always one of five things:**
   - fit ("not too tight, not too loose", Look Expensive);
   - fabric or drape ("weight, structure, or texture", Outfit Mistakes);
   - coordination (2-3 colours; one focal point);
   - finish or care (scuffed, wrinkled, faded, stretched);
   - restraint ("knowing when to stop").
3. **She sizes the change honestly.** "Sometimes it's as simple as hemming a pant leg" (Look Expensive); "Such a
   small change, but it makes a huge difference" (Outfit Mistakes).
4. **She compares on value, not price.** "a $30 tee... fits better, gives you endless outfits, lasts longer, and
   doesn't fade after two washes" (Look Expensive #1).
5. **She adds a "when it applies" line.** "Of course, you can still wear oversized pieces together. Just create
   balance somewhere" (Outfit Mistakes); "Denim can absolutely be part of an elegant wardrobe, but..."
   (Never Wear).
6. **She ends on the inner payoff,** worded differently each time: polished, present, grounded; a quiet way of
   respecting yourself; you matter too.

## 3. Her established wardrobe-video structure [E]

**Opening**
1. **Hook:** a scene. Either "Have you ever seen a woman walk by and thought..." (Never Wear, Beginner's
   Guide) or her own moment, "Have you ever caught a glimpse of yourself at home..." (At Home, 4.12% liked, her
   most-loved wardrobe video). Alternatively a misconception: "You might think... But what if I told you..."
   (Look Expensive).
2. **Barrier removed within the first lines:** "no matter your budget", "without spending a lot".
3. **The turn:** "In today's video, we're revealing / uncovering..." then "Let's get started."

**The points**
- Numbered, with the name spoken: "Number one, loud, oversized logos." / "Invest in basics." / "Two,
  accessories are your best friend." / "Five. Tailoring changes everything."
- In the edit, a white number card carries the heading.

**The guide plug**
- Once, inside an early or middle point: Never Wear in #3; Look Expensive in #4.
- Her wording: "we've linked our personal stylist guide in the description. It will be sent instantly to your
  email after accessing."

**The close**
- A short recap sentence, then a question ("Let us know in the comments which style tip you'll be trying",
  Outfit Mistakes).
- Then: "a simple like or comment would truly mean the world to us".
- Then the guide, then "Until next time, bye everyone" (older) or "Bye, my loves" (recent).

**Length and pace:** 1,150-1,250 spoken words for an 8-point wardrobe video (Never Wear 1,153; Look Expensive
1,235; Beginner's Guide 1,234), about 8.5-9.5 minutes.

## 4. Her language

- **Transitions:**
  - "Here's the truth no one tells you."
  - "And here's a secret."
  - "Now, when it comes to..."
  - "This is one of the most overlooked style details."
  - "And the best part..."
  - "Try this."
  - "So chic." / "So pretty."
- **Words she uses:** elevate, polished, effortless, intentional, timeless, refined, subtle, quiet, grace,
  put together.
- **Colours she names:** cream, ivory, oatmeal, camel, chocolate brown, navy, burgundy.
- **Words she never uses:** slang, hype, shaming, "rich people".
- **Empathy:** once or twice per video, worded differently each time ("We get it", "We've all had those days").

## 6. Already covered: do not repeat without a new angle

**Wardrobe and style**
- logos;
- neon and over-bright colour;
- too tight or revealing;
- distressed denim;
- clashing trends, and too many colours (2-3 rule);
- mismatched accessories;
- **worn-down clothes: steam, polish shoes, check threads** (Never Wear #8);
- basics; accessories and the power trio (shoes, belt, bag); structured bags; pointed toe;
- **tailoring: hem, waist** (Look Expensive #5);
- monochrome; signature scent; **layering** (Look Expensive);
- **proportion, fabric weight, one star piece, underwear lines, logos, tonal colour, styling tricks** (roll
  sleeves, belt, knit over the shoulders, tuck, repeat a colour), signature style (Outfit Mistakes);
- jeans; jewelry; colour combos; quiet luxury basics; old-money essentials; summer shoes; at-home dressing.

**Made in this repo (not on the public list)**
- Dressing Rules: fit first, balance, fabric that holds its shape, underneath invisible, one star, spend on
  shoes, cared-for hands.
- Put Together: decide outfits once, a closet that goes together, finish the outfit, stay ready, hair, the
  ten-second check, carry it well, wear good things on ordinary days.
- Body Shape, French, Winter (incl. pilled knitwear as a mistake), Never Say, Sound Part 2.
