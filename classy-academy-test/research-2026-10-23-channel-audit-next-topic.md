### 3.5 What makes it Classy Academy rather than generic "classy woman" content

**[I] Based on the facts above:**
1. **Clothes and conduct together.** About half the catalogue teaches how she speaks, moves and behaves. Generic style
   channels teach clothes only, and generic "high-value woman" channels teach mindset only.
2. **Elegance as self-respect and quietness, not wealth.** Never rich vs poor; any budget and any age are said
   outright.
3. **Concrete and immediately usable.** Exact phrases, exact swaps, one action per point, everyday places (ordering a
   drink, a café, the grocery store).
4. **Aspirational real women.** Recognisable elegant women and polished creators, a calm voice, slow pacing.
5. **Kind, never shaming.** The "never" lists always give the swap.
