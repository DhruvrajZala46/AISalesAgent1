# Classy Academy - style decode from 5 full videos (27 Nov 2026)

The user uploaded five full Classy Academy videos (360p). Unlike the earlier storyboard-only work, every
number here comes from the actual files: about 2,100 frames at 1 per second, 30 fps filmstrips of each
animation, pixel colour sampling, and fonts rendered side by side with crops of her text.

**Limits:** 360p hides fine detail. Font names are the *closest free match*, not proven. Sound was measured only
roughly here; the full voice/music separation from October (`edit-style-measured.md`) still stands.

## 1. The three "looks" (the theme changes by series)

| Look | Videos | Background share of screen time (measured) | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Caramel studio** (early episodes) | Colours, Beginner, Hair | caramel 50-61%, white 2-12%, footage alone 29-40% | warm, cosy, panels of outfits on a glowing caramel blur |
| **B. White editorial** (Episode 26) | 7 Things | footage alone 54%, white 22%, caramel 17%, cream 7% | quieter, more like a magazine: film scenes, celebrities, quote cards on white |
| **C. Lookbook** (separate series) | Old Money | footage alone 75%, taupe 18%, chocolate 5%, caramel 1% | catalogue / shopping: one model in one room, product names, "Shop the looks" |

The channel moved from A to B over time: as the episodes go on, there's less caramel and more white space and
film footage.

**The caramel backdrop is moving footage, not a flat colour.** Its bright patch drifts across the frame (the
centre moves about a third of the width in 2-3 s). It looks like a looping "soft light / gradient blur" stock clip,
not a gradient she drew.

**The footage palette is chosen too:** cream, white, camel, black, chocolate, burgundy; warm interiors, Paris and
London stone, hotel lobbies, cafés. There's almost no neon or saturated colour. The one bright exception is a
story clip (Kate in royal blue at Wimbledon).

| Role | What it looks like | Closest free font | Confidence |

| Colour-pair labels ("WHITE + CREAM OR BEIGE", "BURGUNDY + GRAY") | thin caps, wide letter-spacing | **Cormorant (Light), tracked** | medium |

| Creator credit ("@handle on TikTok") | tiny script, lower right | same script | medium |

**The signature move: serif line + script line.** A sentence is set in the serif, and the *feeling* half of it
follows on the next line in script:
- "True elegance isn't about seeking permission." / *"It's about making thoughtful decisions."*
- "Protecting your goals until they are ready to bloom" / *"isn't about secrecy."*
- "Elegant women choose" / *"discretion over gossip."*
- "Elegant women keep their finances *private*" (one word in script).

## 4. How the captions work

- **Never full subtitles.** Text appears only for: the number/title of each point; one key sentence per point (the
  "principle"); the exact phrase a woman says; colour or product names; prices.
- **Text is in sync with the voice.** The serif line appears as it's said; the script line appears about 2 s later,
  when the voice reaches it (measured on 7 Things at 1:00-1:03).
- **Placement:**
  - number cards: centred on white, one line, about 2/3 of the width;
  - quotes: centred in the empty side of a split screen (clip on the left third, caramel or white on the right
    two-thirds);
  - product names: lower right, white serif over the clip (Lookbook);
  - creator credits: lower right, tiny.
- **Quoted speech:** in small serif with quote marks, beside a clip of a woman ("No, thank you.", "I won't be able
  to make it.", "That isn't the right fit for me.").
- **Colour combos:** two fabric-texture squares (satin/linen/wool), each with its name in thin spaced caps and a
  "+" between them. Then panels of outfits in that pair.
- **Prices:** "$200" in pale green vs "$4,000" in grey, either side of two framed outfits on textured cream.
- **Logo:** a "CA" monogram with "Classy Academy" in tiny spaced letters underneath, white and semi-transparent,
  top-left on every footage/caramel screen. It's hidden on white cards.

## 5. Effects and animation (measured at 30 fps)

| Effect | How it moves | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Into a number card | footage **fades to white** | ~0.5 s, then white holds ~0.2 s |
| Number-card text | **writes on left to right** (soft-edged wipe, so it looks hand-written) | ~1.2 s for a full line, holds ~3 s |
| Episode title | "Episode 26" fades in first (~0.2 s); then the script title wipes in left to right (~0.6 s); it stays on screen while the clip underneath cuts to a new clip | title on screen ~3-4 s |
| Title card glow | a warm red light-leak in the lower right corner on the early title cards (same strapless-dress clip reused under Episodes 1, 2 and 3) | static |
| Footage under titles/plugs | slightly darkened so white text reads | n/a |
| Panels on caramel | each panel **reveals from the top / grows from its top-left corner**, one per spoken item, ~0.8 s apart | ~0.3 s per panel |
| Quote on caramel | hard cut to the split layout, then the serif line wipes in (~0.5 s) and the script line wipes in later (~0.4 s) | held 4-8 s |
| Between clips | mostly **hard cuts** (38-58% of changes) and short **dissolves** | median soft change 0.30-0.37 s |
| End | the "CA Classy Academy" logo on white | 30 s on 7 Things (end-screen space), 4-7 s on the early videos |
| Never seen | zoom punches, shakes, spins, glitches, emoji, stickers, arrows, whoosh effects | n/a |

**Pace:** across all five videos, something on screen changes about every **1.9-2.7 s** (a cut, a new panel or
a text reveal). Layouts themselves hold longer (see `edit-style-measured.md`: about one layout change every 9 s on
the winners).

## 6. Layout library (what each screen is for)

1. **Full-screen clip**: the hook, film scenes, emotion, celebrities. The logo sits top-left.
2. **White number card**: "1. They Try to Keep Goals Private". It starts each point.
3. **Split: clip (left third) + caramel or white (right)**: holds the principle sentence (serif + script).
4. **Panels on caramel**: 1-4 vertical clips or product cut-outs side by side, added one per spoken item.
5. **Framed photo/clip on cream** (7 Things): a single square-cornered picture centred on `#F7F5F1`, no border.
6. **White quote card**: the principle sentence alone on white (7 Things, mid-point).
7. **Colour-pair swatch** (Colours): fabric textures with thin caps labels and a "+".
8. **Product cut-outs on caramel** (Beginner shoes, Colours trousers): background-removed product shots that line up
   into a row (e.g. 4 shoes = the capsule).
9. **Guide plug over a darkened clip**: "Check the description for / *Stylist & Elegance Guide* /
   www.classyladyacademy.com". Twice in 7 Things (2:04 and 7:12).
10. **Lookbook** (Old Money):
    - a cold open with a script word over the clip ("Fall is Here");
    - a chocolate title card ("Classy Academy Lookbook / *6 Old Money Fall Essentials* / Episode 01");
    - a "SHOP THE LOOKS ↓ / Mentioned pieces linked below" card;
    - a big white script number over a full-screen clip ("1. Sweaters");
    - product names lower right;
    - taupe or chocolate flat backdrops with framed clips.

## 7. Themes (what the footage is of)

- **Women only**, mostly 20s-50s, with older women and several skin tones in most panel rows. Men appear only as
  the other half of a scene (dinner, a couple).
- **Old money / quiet luxury:** tailored cream trousers, knitwear, trench coats, structured bags, ballet flats,
  sunglasses, wide-brim hats, pearls.
- **Settings:** European stone streets and colonnades, hotel lobbies, cafés with white tablecloths, bright minimal
  apartments, wardrobes.
- **Film and celebrity moments**, used for "this is what it looks like". Identified by eye, not traced:
  - Scent of a Woman: the tango in Hair, the restaurant in 7 Things;
  - the Princess of Wales at Wimbledon (7 Things);
  - what look like Audrey Hepburn, Marilyn Monroe (in black and white) and TV interview clips: unconfirmed.
- **Creator reels** (TikTok/Instagram/Pinterest try-ons and hair tutorials), sometimes with a visible
  "@handle on TikTok" credit.
- Rights status of film, interview and creator footage: unresolved, as recorded in `VISUAL-EDITING-DNA.md` section 7.

## 8. Sound (rough check here; full measurement in `edit-style-measured.md`)

- Voice-over starts at 0:00 in all five videos.
- The quietest moments sit about 24-30 dB under the loudest speech. That fits the very soft piano bed measured in
  October (~12 dB under the voice, no ducking, no sound effects).

## 9. Corrections to earlier notes

- `VISUAL-EDITING-DNA.md` section 5 says framed clips have "rounded corners and a soft shadow". In these five
  videos the framed pictures on cream have **square corners and no visible shadow** (at 360p). Treat the rounded
  corners as unconfirmed.
- The caramel backdrop is a **moving** blur clip, not a still gradient.
- 7 Things (Episode 26) runs a much whiter, more editorial layout mix than the "caramel-heavy winners" rule in
  `edit-style-measured.md`. That rule fits episodes 1-3 better than the newer style.
