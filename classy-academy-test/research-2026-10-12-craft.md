## The rules, ranked by evidence

Ranking: first by how many videos show the pattern, then by the likes on the comments that back it. Each rule ends with **Ours:**, which says one of three things:
- ALREADY: our voice.md or 2-SCRIPT.md already does it (quoted);
- ADD: something to add;
- CHANGE: something to change.

### 1. Show it, don't only tell it: every point needs one concrete thing she can SEE or recognise
Every point needs at least one of these:
- a real example (a real outfit or a real woman),
- the same outfit before and after the one change,
- a 1-2 sentence scene she recognises,
- a self-test she can do while watching,
- a gentle funny image.

A rule plus a swap on its own is the shape of the least-loved videos.

Evidence (tags on 871 tips, 46 videos):
- **"Show" moves** (personal story, demo, humour, before-after) are in **36% of top-tier tips** (median per video 36%) against **15% of bottom-tier tips** (median 11%).
- **Personal story:** 17.6% of top tips (13/15 top videos with a transcript) against 3.9% of bottom tips (3/16).
- **Humour:** 5.9% (7/15) against 1.1% (3/16).
- **Demo:** 12.9% against 6.2%.
- **WHY does NOT separate the tiers:** 43.0% of top tips against 46.6% of bottom tips. Explaining more is not what the loved videos do differently.
- **Bottom videos are lists of rules and items:** rule-of-thumb 53.4% against 38.2%; named item 26.1% against 14.3%; 22.2 tips per video against 18.1.
- **Faceless only** (our case): the 5 faceless videos at ≥1.8% teach with a real example in a median **24%** of tips (`3mMT-9A-DAE` 54%, `HG2OJ0O3lxg` 69%, both built on photos of a real, named woman). The 9 faceless videos at ≤1.03% do so in **10%**. Instead, they lean on warning + swap (swap in 25% of tips against 12%; warning in 20% against 12%).

### 3. Frame every point as an upgrade she can choose. Never as a ban, and never as an age-gated ban
Evidence:
- **Inside the 50+ group** (the same audience):
  - Age-gated titles with a **positive or permission** frame ("Always Look Elegant", "Rules She Never Breaks", "And Still Look Stunning"): 4 videos, **median 2.58%**.
  - Age-gated **prohibition/mistake** titles ("NEVER Wear", "STOP Wearing", "REMOVE", "Don't Flatter", "Mistakes", "WORST"): 9 videos, **median 1.03%**.
- **Same channel, same voice (Chic After 60):**
  - "How To Wear Sleeveless Dresses At 60+ And Still Look Stunning": **1.92%**. Promise: "I want to show you that you can wear that sleeveless dress you love".
  - "12 Fashion Prints That Don't Flatter Women Over 60": **0.51%**.
  - "10 Elegant Shoes For Women Who HATE Heels": **0.71%**.
- **A disclaimer does not rescue a ban.**
  - `IRbxFHwtwmQ` says "These are recommendations, not laws" and still gets 0.51%.
  - `sxOqZspbaEI` pre-empts: "many women defend right away, and I understand why". It gets 0.78%, and its top comment is 74: "Wear what ever you like at any age in any color & style".
- **The top comments on age-gated bans are defiance:**
  - 133: "Leave it alone. Women should wear whatever they like"
  - 76: "I'm 72. Never tell me what I can't wear."
  - 76: "I'm 70 and breaking lots of your 'rules'!"
  - 56: "I'm tired being told what we are doing wrong! Dress how you want !" (`A3rY2G2mYMQ`)
  - 27: "So in other words, you want anyone over 50 to look like a pensioner?" (`sxOqZspbaEI`)
  - 41: "you don't give a damn what other people think" (`TFhmgp8frbA`)
  - 21: "she keeps referring to women over 50 like we are dinosaurs" (`0gX_ib7TZu4`)
  - 57: "style shouldn't be about avoiding 'mistakes'" (`mmICqziDXtA`)
- **On-camera prohibition can still be liked, but the top comment is pushback:**
  - `HICszZ5xQdc` (3.99%): 8,600 "elegance is not about what you wear, but how you treat others"; 2,800 "Elegant doesn't always equal french..."
  - `hNL6z168Eh0` (3.85%): 1,000 "Wouldn't it have been more classy to approach from a positive perspective..."
- **Our own:**
  - The 5 prohibition titles (Never Wear / Never Do / Avoid / Mistakes / Never Share) have a median like rate of **2.42%** against **2.97%** for the other 24. Their median views are higher: 148,948 against 127,221. They get the click, not the love.
  - Our biggest video, `My7qteRU9k0` "8 Things Elegant Ladies Never Wear" (2.24%), drew 667: "Bright colors on Black women and women of color are always classy, elegant and absolutely stunning" and 424: "I disagree with your second point. Bright color can be elegant."

**Ours: ALREADY in part.** voice.md: "Never say a colour or culture 'is not elegant' - refine, never ban." 2-SCRIPT test 4: "Kind: validates her first, never shames". **ADD:**
- (a) Never age-gate a "don't" ("women over 50 should never ..." is banned). Age appears only as permission ("at 60 you can still ...").
- (b) Validation is not enough: the whole point must read as "here is an upgrade", not "stop doing this". A "We get it" line inside a ban did not lift any bottom video.
