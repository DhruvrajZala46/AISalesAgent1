## D. Why people click

**Read from titles, thumbnails and 2 transcripts. This is interpretation.**
- **The desire is near-universal and fear-based:** "Am I making myself look older than I am, without knowing it?" Then the promise of an easy fix.
- **It is not limited to one age group.** 9 of the 13 clothing videos with 1M+ name no age group in the title. Justine Leconte opens with: "this video works at any age, for everyone… I'm saying older than you are."
- **The title pattern** = [a number] + things/mistakes + "make you look older" (or "look 10 years younger") + often the fix ("and how to fix them", "what clothes work"). Questions are rare.
- **Thumbnails** (25 women's winners and 6 failures inspected):
  - About 22 of the 25 winners put the age verdict in the biggest or most prominent text: "LOOK OLDER", "YOU LOOK OLD", "10 YEARS YOUNGER", "ROUPAS QUE ENVELHECEM".
  - 8 add ✗/✓ outfit comparisons.
  - Most show the creator's face.
  - The failures used the same devices, so the thumbnail does not explain success on its own.
- **Content** (transcripts of MM Personal Styling 1.53M and Justine Leconte 1.46M):
  - The promise comes in the first 30 seconds, with reassurance: "no finger-pointing… a safe space"; "it isn't about making you look trendy".
  - Point 1 starts at 0:47–1:00.
  - Each mistake gets a demonstrated fix. MM restyles the same outfit "styled vs unstyled".
  - MM's video is itself a remake of one of their most popular videos. The creator repeated the concept because it worked.
  - Captions for the other winners were refused by YouTube's rate limit and are not claimed.

## E. Fit with Classy Academy

**Format:**
- The channel's #1 video is the same mechanism: "8 Things Elegant Ladies Never Wear: What to Wear Instead" (9.3×), a list to avoid plus the fix.
- `fit.py` places the new title with that video.

**Audience:**
- Age 40–70+ is a real comment theme (29 comments, 476 likes).
- The channel's most-liked comment (272 likes): "Even though I am 74… elegance should not end with age."
- Most proven versions are not age-targeted, so the channel's every-age stance holds.

**Never covered here:**
- The channel has no "look older" video.
- It is not the rejected "Beautiful With Age" idea. That was an aspirational phrase which 45 channels copied within weeks (median 321 views). This one is the fear-plus-fix concept, with seven-plus years of million-view history.

**Rules for the script** (so it does not repeat or contradict the channel):

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Avoid points the channel already teaches | logos, neon, too tight, distressed denim, proportions, fabric, over-accessorising, tailoring and fit, layering, colour pairs; and the "No Longer Wear" items |
| Do not say monochrome ages you | The channel teaches monochrome as elegant |
| Do not say bright colours age you | Viewers pushed back on this (435 likes) |
| Keep the points broad | Not one single item (see the failures) |
| Prove every point | Use a stylist or other source |

**Thumbnail in the channel's template:**
- Top line: "Things That Make You". Headline: **"Look Older"**, with the red underline under "Older". Small line: *what to wear instead*.
- Photos: three elegant women only, as on every channel winner.
