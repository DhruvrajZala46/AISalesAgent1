# Next idea: which open topics are proven at millions of views AND big likes (26 Oct 2026)

The user asked for winners that reach millions of views and hundreds of thousands of likes, topics we have not
made yet, and one idea with the highest chance. Nothing from memory: every number below comes from today's run.

## How it was found

- **Skill run.** `content-idea`, all steps, on 26 Oct 2026:
  - fresh public lists from 59 of the 60 tracked niche channels (one failed), giving 3,383 long videos;
  - every video scored as a multiple of its own channel's median (`collect.py`, `rank.py`, `shapes.py`).
- **Love, measured.** Public views, likes, comments and upload dates of 49 top winners, plus the table-manners
  leader, with `yt-dlp --skip-download` (embedded player, no cookies, no login). Like rate = likes / views.
- **What each winner teaches.** The captions of the two biggest winners were read, to see what they teach and
  whether we have already made it.
- **Audience fit.** `fit.py idea`, `title.py`, and our viewers' requests from the 12 Oct and 16 Oct runs
  (`audience_proof.py`, 2,685 comments).
- **Left out:**
  - off-lane rows (men's style, beauty-only, hygiene, nutrition, mindset), even when huge;
  - everything already made in `videos/` or in Cut C (see "Taken" below).

## Copies do not break out (today's data)

When a title is copied, the copies rarely repeat the original's result:

- **"10 Things Elegant Ladies NEVER Wear":** 16.1x for the original (1.48M). Mademoiselle Spends' own
  remakes got:
  - At Work 2.43x;
  - Summer 1.86x;
  - Fall 1.71x;
  - At a Party 0.92x;
  - "NEVER Do" 0.78x.
- **"Elegant Shoes For Women Who HATE Heels":** one title, five channels in four weeks (Sep-Oct 2026):
  - Chic After 60 28.5x;
  - Mademoiselle Spends 6.2x;
  - Madre 1.37x;
  - Classy Woman 1.3x;
  - Rock Your Fashion 0.35x.
- **"French Women Get More Beautiful With Age":** 27.0x, then 4.99x, then 1.08x.

So the pick has to be a topic that is proven but that we have not made, told in our own way. It should not be
another variant of our own title.

## The one structural thing

The million-view, high-like winners teach **a practical skill she can use the next time she gets dressed or
sits down**. They are told as a numbered set of rules or fixes: "how to master", "rules everyone should learn",
"mistakes to avoid". The viewer leaves able to do something, not with an opinion.

## The ideas, ranked (views AND love AND our audience)

### ★ 1. 8 Table Manners Every Woman Should Know (title.py 100/100, 39 characters)

- **Niche proof, views and love:**
  - Jamila Musayeva: 7,248,463 views, 265,958 likes, 10,000 comments;
  - Mademoiselle Spends: 687,194 views, 30,056 likes (4.37%, the 2nd-highest like rate of all the million-class
    winners);
  - Madre: 752k at 209x (12 Oct run).
  - In the 12 Oct all-time run: 33 videos at 5x+ from 12 channels.
- **Why now:** the holiday dinners. US Thanksgiving is 26 Nov, then Christmas and New Year. Publish in early
  November. This is a timing reason, not a measured trend.
- **Our viewers asked for it** (12 Oct run):
  - "Part 2 please. Manners definitely are the most memorable impression..." (32 likes);
  - "Etiquette please";
  - "please post more on etiquette, holiday style" (5).
- **Our closest format:** "7 Things Elegant Ladies Never Do in Public", 4.2x, 501,942 views, 11,817 likes.
  The title mirrors the user's favourite video, "8 Dressing Rules Every Woman Should Know".
- **Audience fit:**
  - `fit.py` matched only "6 Elegant Summer Shoes Every Woman Should Own" (0.5x), on the words "Every Woman
    Should". That is a word match, not the topic. We have no manners video yet, so this is new ground.
- **Lens (channel stance):** consideration for the people at your table, not status. Kind, never fear. An
  exception line for real life, because rigid rules drew pushback in the 16 Oct study ("don't fret", 1,000
  likes).
- **Red lines:** none in the title. Never say "upper class" or rich-vs-poor; Manners Matters' "Upper Class"
  wording is a red line for us.
- **Teaching angle:** she will know the table moments where grace shows and exactly what to do in each, ready
  for her holiday dinners.
  - **The 8 manners are not chosen yet.** They come from the stage 2 proof map: the winners' transcripts and
    top comments, two proofs per tip.
- **Flags, plainly:**
  - etiquette is evergreen, not a live wave: today's lists have 73 etiquette/manners videos at a 0.91x median,
    with 5x+ from only 4 channels;
  - the 7.2M video is from 2020;
  - nothing here guarantees millions. This is the open topic with the most proof of views AND likes.

## Honest limits

- Multiples come from each channel's latest 60 uploads; publish dates in the lists are month-accurate.
- Likes and views were read today; older videos have had more time.
- The 7.2M table-manners multiple (123x) is from the 12 Oct all-time run. Today's views and likes were
  re-checked live.
- A like rate is a signal of love, not proof of cause. Faceless channels usually get fewer likes than
  on-camera ones.
- The formula label describes a title, not why a video worked.
