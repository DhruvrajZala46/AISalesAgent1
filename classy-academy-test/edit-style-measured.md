# How The Classy Elegance videos are edited (measured, Oct 2026)

Measured on all 37 uploads (30 long videos + 7 Shorts) from their public storyboard frames (one frame
every ~5 s), plus the full audio of the #1 video ("8 Things Elegant Ladies Never Wear", 1.2M) and the
newest video ("6 Old Money Wardrobe Essentials"), with the voice separated from the music (Demucs).

## Screen time by layout (share of frames)

| Layout | Top 5 videos by views | All other long videos |
|---|---|---|
| Caramel blurred backdrop (quotes, single clips, cut-outs) | **34%** | 19% |
| Row of clips edge to edge (one per spoken item) | 23% | 22% |
| Full-screen clip (blurred copy behind a centred vertical clip) | 27% | 36% |
| White number / chapter card (black script) | 8% | 10% |
| Layout changes per minute | **6.6** | 7.9 |

The more a video is watched, the more time it spends on the caramel backdrop and the calmer its
pace. The winners change layout about every 9 seconds.

## Sound

| | #1 video | Newest video |
|---|---|---|
| Voice loudness | -17.6 LUFS | -22.2 LUFS |
| Music loudness | -27.9 LUFS | -35.1 LUFS |
| Music under the voice | **~11.5 dB** | **~13 dB** |
| Music in pauses vs under speech | same level (no ducking) | same level |
| Music starts / ends | with the first word / ~5 s after the last word | same |
| Music character | soft solo piano, sparse, very little percussion | soft piano with light pulse |
| Sound effects (whooshes, clicks, dings) | **none found** | **none found** |

## Rules used for the French video
- One steady soft solo-piano bed, ~12 dB under the voice, no lifts in the pauses, fades in with the
  first word and out after the end card. Tracks: Mixkit "Classical 4/5/9/10" by Jonny S. (free license).
- **No sound effects.**
- Layout mix matched to the winners: backdrop with quotes and panels, a row of clips that grows one
  panel per spoken item, full-screen clips for emotion, white script number cards, the stylist-guide
  haze card, an X checklist recap and the white CE logo end card.
- Real, credited Pinterest / TikTok / Instagram clips only (no AI), each clip used once, every clip
  checked frame by frame against the words it sits under.
