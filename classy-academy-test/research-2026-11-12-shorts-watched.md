## Hook formulas seen in these 15

1. Rule -> exception: "The queen could never bow to anyone" -> "but at princess diana's funeral…"
2. A situation the viewer fears: "Your rich friend's parents invite you to a fancy dinner."
3. "POV: you think you know…"
4. "Notice …" + a red cross or circle on the detail.
5. Meme words + an icon: "Define Aura" -> "Princess Diana >>>".
6. "N times [icon] broke [the rules]", best item last.
7. The uncomfortable truth: "Here's the uncomfortable truth about why you don't look as good as other women in
   clothes."
8. The rare type: "There's one that's rarely discussed, even though it's actually pretty common."
9. Contrast in frame 1: an elegant woman doing something inelegant.
10. No hook text at all: a famous film scene's own conflict.
