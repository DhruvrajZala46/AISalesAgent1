## 2. How the channel reasons (the thinking behind a tip)

1. **The visual mechanism.** A rule is never left as taste; the channel says what the eye sees.
   - "cut the leg at an awkward spot, making your frame look shorter" (Fall #2);
   - "you lose your waist, your proportions" (Fall #1);
   - "it can overwhelm your features" (NW #2);
   - "the contrast can feel a little harsh" (Cheapen, pink with black);
   - "creates visual noise" (NW #6).
2. **The cause is often how a piece is styled, not the piece.**
   - "the boots aren't the issue. It's the way they're styled" (Fall #2);
   - "It's not just the hoodie itself, it's how it's usually worn" (Fall #5);
   - "Sometimes it's not about the piece being cheap, but about how it's styled" (NW #7). [O]
3. **Honest exceptions.**
   - "if you're super tall with long legs, you might still pull it off" (Fall #2);
   - "Trust me, I love mine for lounging" (Fall #5);
   - "Of course, you can still wear oversized pieces together. Just create balance somewhere" (Cheapen). [O]
4. **A test she can run.**
   - "until you step into bright or natural lighting" (Cheapen, fabric and underwear);
   - "ask yourself, 'How can I style this?'" (Cheapen);
   - "before heading out, take one extra moment" (NW #8). [O]
5. **The swap is concrete.** Named pieces, colours and fabrics:
   - "Instead of a logo covered tee, try a crisp white blouse. Swap a monogrammed bag for a structured tote in smooth leather" (NW #1);
   - "a dark wash straight leg jean" (NW #4, Fall #6);
   - "Think chocolate brown, navy, burgundy" (Fall #3). [O]
6. **The payoff is inner.** Confidence, ease, self-respect, intention. It is said once per point, in different words each time. [O]

## 3. How a wardrobe-list point is built

This is the shape of the 1.2M video (NW), confirmed by Fall. The moves are flexible. Never use the same move as a fixed formula at every point.

1. **Name it, spoken:** "Number one, loud, oversized logos."
2. **Acknowledge her once or twice per video, never at every point:** "We get it." (NW #1 only), "Now, listen." "Let's be honest, we've all done it." (Fall #7).
3. **The mechanism:** what it does to how she looks.
4. **Where needed, the principle in one line:** "True elegance whispers."
5. **What elegant women tend to do.** A few times per video, not at every point.
6. **The swap:** "Instead of… try…" with named pieces and colours, and often two or three options.
7. **An exception or a test**, where it is true.
8. **The payoff**, varied.

Word budget per point:
- NW: about 110–150 words.
- Fall: about 150–200.
- Points run 40–55 seconds.

## 4. Script structure (wardrobe list)

**Hook (about 100 words). Point 1 starts around 0:41–0:43.**
1. A scene question: "Have you ever seen a woman walk by and thought…" (NW, Fall; BG uses "Have you ever noticed a woman…").
2. The reframe: "That kind of elegance isn't accidental… it's often not about what she's wearing, it's about what she knows not to."
3. The promise: "In today's video, we're uncovering eight things… and the subtle swaps…"
4. The signature line: "Some of these may surprise you, but once you see them, you'll never unsee them. Let's get started." (NW, Fall, Cheapen)

**Guide CTA:**
- Once, folded into an early point (NW: inside #3, "And here's a secret. Many well-dressed women don't share. They figured out what flatters them… we've linked our personal stylist guide in the description. It will be sent instantly to your email after accessing.").
- It is repeated in the outro.

**Close:**
- "Thank you so much for watching. We hope you enjoyed this video and found something valuable to take with you. If you did, a simple like or comment would mean the world to us. Be sure to check the description below for … the stylist guide… Until next time, bye everyone." (NW)
- Variants: "Bye, my loves" (Cheapen); "Let us know in the comments which style tip you'll be trying" (Cheapen); "comment the number of the habit" (Microhabits).

**Length:**
- Wardrobe winners: 1,168 words (NW, 8 points), 1,252 (LE), 1,368 (Fall, 7 points).
- Channel median: about 1,257 words, 8:41.

**No "Part 2" framing unless the user asks for it.** (User rule, 21 Oct 2026.)

## 5. Language

- **Words the channel uses:** elevate, effortless, quiet, polished, timeless, refined, intentional, put together, subtle, grace, "so chic", "instantly".
- **Colours it names:** cream, ivory, camel, chocolate brown, navy, burgundy, soft beige, sage.
- **Transitions are short and change each time:** "Let's start with…", "Now, listen.", "This one is so common.", "I love this one.", "Here's something subtle."
- **Avoid:**
  - repeating "the secret is", "here's the thing" or "it's not about X, it's about Y" at every point;
  - hype words;
  - shaming;
  - "rich people";
  - statistics;
  - quoting other people;
  - culture reports.
