# Shorts research - 12 Nov 2026

Question: who is winning on Shorts in the elegance niche (women 40+, US), with what formats, and what is a
realistic plan for The Classy Elegance (@theclassyelegance, 236 subscribers)?

## How this was collected

- Public Shorts listings (`yt-dlp --flat-playlist` on each channel's `/shorts` tab, up to 80 latest per channel)
  for the 60 channels in `.claude/skills/content-idea/channels.json` plus 31 Shorts-first channels found by
  searching the niche. 3,718 Shorts from 65 channels with a usable list; 2,913 Shorts from 53 channels are in our
  lane (off-lane influencers, men's style and sponsored-lifestyle channels removed).
- Every Short scored as a multiple of its own channel's median (`yt-viral`).
- Publish date, length, likes and comments for the top 150 in-lane Shorts from the YouTube Data API.
- Our own audience from YouTube Analytics, 1 Jul - 10 Nov 2026.
- Visual read: the public thumbnail image of 18 winning Shorts, checked by eye.

**Not inspected.** The spoken scripts and the full video of every competitor Short. YouTube refuses the video
pages from this machine ("confirm you're not a bot") and vidIQ had 0 credits (resets 26 Nov). So hooks are read
from titles, on-screen text in the thumbnail frame and lengths; retention inside the videos is not measured.
"When your viewers are online" is Studio-only and was not available.

**The one thing they share:** the verdict is on screen in the first second. A number or a cross/tick, the item
named in a few words, over a real woman doing or wearing it. It is the same shape as our long-form finding of
11 Oct ("a numbered list that sorts things into elegant vs not").

Formats 1, 2, 3 and 5 can be made faceless from footage like our long videos use. Formats 6 and 7 need a person
on camera. Format 4 is proven only with AI images, which this channel does not use; a real-photo version would be
a test.

**Paid views, removed from the proof:** Midlife Posh Closet "4 Fall Styling Mistakes" 421K views with 9 comments
and 0.3% liked; Alice in Paris sponsor Shorts with 86 likes on 223K views; Jamila Musayeva "Fanka Activewear"
415 likes on 234K. These are ad-boosted, not ideas that spread.

## 5. Posting time

The winners publish at every hour (Classy After 50 at 11:45-13:05 UTC, Desi Rich 16:00-23:55 UTC, Classy Academy
23:55 UTC, Beth Djalali 16:16 UTC). No hour separates winners from the rest in this data, and our own
viewer-online data was not available. Any posting time is a starting assumption to test.
