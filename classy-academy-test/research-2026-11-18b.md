The user said the topics suggested so far have not brought views, and asked for a deeper, channel-specific study:
the audience, the competitors, the gaps, and only the strongest ideas, each with three titles, the evidence and an angle.

**Nothing here can promise views.** Each idea shows its numbers with the losers counted, and section 6 lists the limits.

## 5. The four ideas, strongest first

### 1 ★ Things Princess Diana loved that you can still buy today

- **Titles:** "15 Things Princess Diana Loved That You Can Still Buy Today" (94/100) / "15 Everyday Things Princess Diana
  Could Not Live Without" (94) / "Princess Diana's 15 Favourite Things You Can Still Buy".
- **Why:** the best angle in the fair test (3.76x; 58% of 33 videos doubled their channel), crossed with the one woman
  this channel's audience already clicks.
- **Evidence:** "20 Items Princess Diana Loved You Can Still Buy Today" 426,000 (45.6x; 71,400 subscribers);
  "5 Perfumes Princess Diana Wore in Secret That You Can Still Buy Today" 234,000 (167x; 13,500 subscribers) and 88,000
  on a second channel; "15 Weird Things Princess Diana Loved to Buy" 112,000 (November 2026; 1,200 subscribers). The
  same format for other women: 452,000, 272,000, 211,000, 160,000.
- **Losers:** two Diana copies got 4,000 and 1,000 views; six of the 33 are under their channel's usual.
- **Angle:** only things with a named source (her perfumer, her bag maker, her butler, a museum, an auction record),
  the story of each (when she wore it, who gave it), and today's price, with the affordable ones marked. This channel
  can tell it as taste, not shopping: what each choice says about her.
- **To decide:** this format speaks brand names, which the channel's style videos never do.

### 2 Princess Diana's jewels: where every piece is today

- **Titles:** "12 Diana Jewels Worth Millions: Who Wears Them Now?" (98/100) / "What Happened to Princess Diana's Jewels
  After 1997?" (98) / "Princess Diana's Jewels: Where Is Every Piece Today?" (80).
- **Why:** jewels "never worn / locked away" is the second-best angle (2.41x; 51% doubled), and the Diana version has won
  on very small channels.
- **Evidence:** "Princess Diana's Most Legendary Jewels - Worth Millions Today" 787,000 (254x; 26,800 subscribers);
  "The Jewelery Catherine Inherited from Diana That She Will Never Wear" 324,000 (87.6x; 6,500 subscribers); "The Diana
  Jewels Catherine Inherited But REFUSES To Wear!" 107,000. All time: "Top 10 Most Beautiful Treasures of Princess
  Diana" 3.8M; "Her Life Through Jewels" 5.1M. This channel's Short "Diana's Engagement Ring Came From a Catalogue": 8,100.
- **Losers:** Diana's jewels as a whole are only 1.30x on 93 videos, usual 3,500 views. The winners are the ones that
  promise where the pieces are now and what they are worth.
- **Angle:** one question per jewel, answered with a dated photograph: who gave it, when she last wore it, who has worn
  it since and when. No guessing about why someone does or does not wear a piece.

### 3 The 10 most elegant royal women, ranked

- **Titles:** "The 10 Most Elegant Royal Women Ever, Ranked" (94/100) / "10 Most Elegant Royals Ever: Only One Changed
  Royal Style" (94) / "Ranking the 10 Most Elegant Royal Women of All Time" (94).
- **Why:** it is the channel's own word, elegance, in a format that makes people click to check the order and comment
  to disagree.
- **Evidence:** "The 10 Most Elegant Royals Ever - But Only One Redefined Royal Style" 377,000 (53.9x; 15,000
  subscribers); "The 10 Most Beautiful Royals Ever Ranked" 461,000 (65.9x); "Discover the SECRETS of the most elegant
  ROYALS!" 243,000; "The World's Most Beautiful Royals Ranked" 151,000 (48.7x). The angle: 1.44x on 42 videos from 20
  channels, 43% doubled, 11 at 100,000+. All time: "Ranking The Royal Wedding Dresses From Worst To First" 4.3M.
- **Losers:** the usual video of this kind has 5,700 views; more than half did not double.
- **Angle:** rank by elegance, not beauty, and give each woman one lesson the viewer can use (the channel's teaching
  inside a story video). Diana is in it, so the Shorts audience has a reason to click. No Meghan Markle (channel rule).

### 4 The story of Princess Diana's wedding dress

- **Titles:** "Princess Diana's Wedding Dress: 9 Things That Went Wrong" (94/100) / "9 Secrets Sewn Into Princess Diana's
  Wedding Dress" (94) / "Princess Diana's Wedding Dress Had a Secret Twin".
- **Why:** this is the channel's own strongest signal. Four of its six best Shorts are wedding moments (75,000; 39,000;
  28,000; 20,000).
- **Evidence:** "Princess Diana's WEDDING DRESS Was Much Nastier Than You Thought" 314,000 (October 2026; a channel of
  1,200 subscribers); "Princess Diana's WEDDING Was Much MORE DISTURBING Than You Think" 48,000 (4,200 subscribers).
  All time: "What Lady Di's wedding dress looked like" 2.2M; the 1981 wedding film 69.5M.
- **Losers, plainly:** Diana's wedding as a whole is below average for small channels (0.72x on 20 videos; one above
  100,000). This idea rests on one breakout and on this channel's own Shorts.
- **Angle:** the long version of the Shorts that already won, told in order through the day (the crushed dress, the
  wrong name, the missing word), in the channel's calm voice instead of "nastier".

**Left out on purpose:** royal rules (ordinary now for small channels); icon makeup (0.74x); candid "forgot the cameras"
compilations (2.23x, but they are other people's footage end to end); Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy and Jackie Kennedy
(strong elsewhere, unproven on this channel: test each with a Short first).

## 6. Limits

- The largest results in this space are 300,000 to 950,000 views on small channels, not millions. No small channel in
  the fair test reached 10M.
- A long video of 17 views after one day (Diana's style rules) cannot be judged yet; it is the only long Diana video.
- The Shorts audience may not follow to long videos. That is untested on this channel.
- Publish dates are month-accurate. Subscriber counts are today's, not the count when a video went up.
- Rights: these videos use press photographs and archive film, as the Diana video did. Nothing is licensed; the
  creator decides.
- Every fact in a script will need a source read that day. The titles above promise only what is documented; any
  that cannot be sourced at the script stage will be changed.
