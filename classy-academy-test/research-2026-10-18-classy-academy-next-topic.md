## C. The single strongest recommendation

**Recommended title: How to Always Look Put Together, Even With No Time**

`title.py` gives it 76/100 because it has no number. Your own number-free "How to" titles (5.40×, 4.96×) show that isn't a problem on your channel. A numbered alternative: "8 Habits of Women Who Always Look Put Together".

**Topic and teaching objective:** by the end, a busy woman has a simple daily system that makes her look polished in minutes, with fewer decisions:
- a personal outfit formula in her best colours;
- what to prepare the night before and once a week;
- a quick elegant hair default and a 5-minute face;
- a final check before she leaves.

It's practical, every age, any budget. It's about time and decisions, not a grooming list.

**The strongest evidence:**
1. **Demand now, from many channels:** 30.8× (308k, ~28 Sep), 11.8× ("…When You Have No Time", ~5 Oct), 18.2× (1.3M), 73.9× (1.7M, the "everyday, even on a budget" version), 4.0× (421k), 4.4× (324k), and fresh copies at 5.6× and 4.6×.
2. **Real search phrasing:** autocomplete for "how to look put together" lists *everyday, all the time, without makeup, as a mom, on a budget, at work, at home*.
3. **Your audience:** it uses your "How to look ___, no matter your ___" promise (5.40×), and the appearance family that still performs for you in 2026 (hair/grooming median 1.22× vs wardrobe 0.38×). Your at-home video earns your second-highest like rate (4.13%), with part-two and "natural look" requests.

**Closest existing videos, and how it avoids repeating them:**
- **"10 Grooming Habits Elegant Women Never Skip":** that video is about *what* to care for (skin, hands, nails, scent). This one is about *how to make it fast and automatic*. Avoid re-listing grooming items.
- **"How to Look Elegant & Expensive at Home":** that is at-home loungewear and rituals. This one is about leaving the house polished on a busy day.
- **"How to Dress Casual yet Elegant":** that is outfit details. This one uses a formula so dressing takes no thought.

**Main uncertainty:**
- **It's crowded right now.** Julianna Lee's two videos and several copies in the last 3 weeks could make yours one of many.
- **No search volume to measure.** The phrase is too small for Google Trends, so demand rests on video outliers and autocomplete.
- **Your channel's 2026 trend.** Most 2026 uploads sit under your median.

If timing matters more than crowding, **candidate 2 (dinner-table etiquette)** is the alternative: it's new ground for the channel, in your best 2026 format, with a direct manners request, and it's seasonal from November.
