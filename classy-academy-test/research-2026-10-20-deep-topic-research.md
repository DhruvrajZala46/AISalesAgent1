### Formulas

- **Multiple** = views ÷ median views of the same channel's latest ≤60 long uploads.
  - The `content-idea` method: a channel needs ≥4 videos.
- **Hit** = multiple ≥3, **and** views ≥20,000, **and** channel median ≥3,000.
  - The floors keep tiny channels from manufacturing 100× "hits".
- **Hit rate** = hits ÷ videos in the window.
- **Index** = a topic's hit rate ÷ the hit rate of all videos in the same window.
  - Window baselines: 0–120 days 8.0%; 121–365 days 11.1%.
  - This corrects for young videos not having finished growing.
- **Views/day** = views ÷ days since publish (a rough lifetime average).
- **Views/subscriber** = views ÷ channel subscribers.

### Caveats

- **Publish dates** come from YouTube's "x weeks/months ago" listings, so they are accurate to about a month. Exact dates were blocked by the CAPTCHA.
- **Topics are tagged from titles with patterns**, spot-checked by hand.

## 9. Risks, missing metrics, confidence

1. **Season.** The dated lane peaks in September and October. Last November to January it ran at median 0.95×. Publish as early as possible. The "classics" framing is less tied to a season than "fall trends that are out".
2. **The channel's slump.** The last 9 uploads have a median of 0.5×. Without Studio data I can't tell whether the cause is the topics, impressions, CTR or the upload gaps. A strong topic can't fix a distribution problem.
3. **A thin direct precedent for the paradox:** 4 videos, 1.3–2.7×. The upside rests on the wider lane, not on proven copies of this exact idea.
4. **Visuals.** Showing the "dated" way must not mock real people. Show the modern "after" with real creator footage. Name the dated detail in the words and with neutral close-ups, or skip showing it.
5. **Data limits:**
   - dates accurate to the month;
   - rounded view counts;
   - likes from a mirror API;
   - comment counts unavailable for most competitors;
   - no CTR, retention or impressions for anyone;
   - YouTube's CAPTCHA blocked most captions and exact metadata;
   - search results come from a US network location;
   - 250 channels is a large sample, not the whole niche;
   - topic tags come from title patterns.
6. **Confidence:**
   - **moderate** that this beats the channel's recent median;
   - **low** for 5× or more;
   - it is the best-supported choice among the candidates measured, **not a proven viral topic**.
