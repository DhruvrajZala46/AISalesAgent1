## What this video does (observed)

1. **The teaching unit is a habit** (pace, volume, replies, texting, the soft no, tiny habits). Exact phrases sit
   **inside** a habit, as its practice ("instead of X, you can simply say Y").
2. **The replay peaks are the phrase moments:**
   - replies, 3:03-3:37, up to 2.4x;
   - the nervous vs short no, 5:16-5:30, 2.3x;
   - the soft-no opening, 3.13x.

   They come after the habit has been explained.
3. **Named women demonstrate with their own voices.** Audrey Hepburn (film audio) for pace; Princess Diana
   (archive audio) for softness.
4. **The layouts carry the teaching:**
   - the habit on a white card;
   - the reason/payoff as a sentence on caramel;
   - the exact phrase in script beside a framed conversation;
   - the exact message in a chat bubble;
   - one word alone ("Like").
5. **Footage sources are mixed by job:**
   - famous film scenes and celebrity interviews for the icons;
   - Pexels stock for everyday actions;
   - creators'/brands' pinned clips for conversations.
6. **Layout mix** (frames):
   - full-screen 39%;
   - framed single clip 23%;
   - white cards 20%;
   - caramel cards 12%;
   - rows 3%;
   - text on screen in 45% of frames.
7. **Rhythm:** at least 10.6 new pictures/min and 6.6 layout changes/min.
8. **Voice:** about 186 words/min. The calm comes from the writing, not a slow voice.
9. **Weak spots, by the data:** the intro 0:05-0:20 (0.5x) and the 40 s static end card (to 0.0x).
