## What this video does (observed, not guessed)

1. **The teaching unit is a habit** (pace, volume, replies, texting, the soft no, micro-habits). Exact phrases appear
   **inside** a habit as its practice ("instead of X, you can simply say Y"); they are not the structure of the video.
2. **The two biggest replay peaks are the phrase moments** (3:03-3:37 replies 2.4x, 5:16-5:30 soft no 2.3x), plus the
   soft-no opening 3.1x. So the swaps are where the value is felt, but they work because the habit was explained first.
3. **Named, recognisable elegant women are the teachers' examples**, shown and **heard** in their own film/interview
   audio (Audrey Hepburn for pace, Princess Diana for a soft voice); other recognisable women (Grace Kelly, Angelina
   Jolie, Beyoncé, Zendaya, Tracee Ellis Ross...) carry the other lines.
4. **Three screen layouts carry the teaching:** a white number card with a script title; a **full sentence on blurred
   caramel** (the reason or the payoff, written); a **rounded-corner clip on cream with the phrase written beside it**.
   Plus full-screen clips, collages of 3-4 women, a single-word card ("Like"), chat bubbles on texting footage.
5. On-screen text in 45% of frames (the highest of our videos with similar topics); quote/caramel cards 12%;
   white cards 20%; full-screen clips 39%; framed single clips 23% (`edit_ev/labels_own`, by eye).
6. The guide plug is ~10 s inside point 3, not at a fixed place.
7. Weak spots, by the data: the intro 0:05-0:20 (0.5x) and the 40 s static end card (falls to 0.0x).
