## Why it works
1. **The hook is a question people already ask**, in 1.2 seconds: "which water in the morning?", then "nobody
   understands", which tells the viewer the confusion is normal. The pill shows the topic even with the sound off.
2. **One sentence shape, five times:** "if you drink X in the morning, you get Y". The viewer learns the pattern
   after the first line, so 4.3 words a second is still easy to follow. A new payoff lands every 5 seconds.
3. **Each benefit is for a different person** (vitamin C, skin, gas, heart, weight gain), so nearly every viewer
   finds "mine" and forwards it to the family member with that problem.
4. **Everything is already in the kitchen.** It costs nothing and can be done tomorrow morning, so people save it.
5. **A plain desi face, not a studio expert.** He stands outdoors and talks to a friend off camera, so it reads as
   advice, not an advertisement. The face gives trust; the footage below shows exactly what to do.
6. **26 seconds and no ending.** It loops, and people rewatch to catch all five, which pushes average watch time
   past 100%.

## What not to copy
Some of his claims are weak (clove water for clear skin). Our lines must be ones we can defend.
