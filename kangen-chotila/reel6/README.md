# Reel 6 (Gujarati, 40 s): "એક મશીન, પાંચ પાણી"

The owner sent another Kangen distributor's 39 s Hindi reel as a reference ("celebrities choose Kangen") and asked
for the same style on a different topic, in Gujarati.

Reference style kept: a beige opening board with round photo cutouts popping in, quick cuts every 2-3 s, big white
captions 1-2 words at a time in the middle of the screen, a "how does it work?" curiosity beat, a free home water
test and live demo, and an end card.

What's different:
- Topic: the five waters of one Kangen machine (drinking; tea, dal and rice; medicine with neutral water; washing
  fruit and vegetables with strong water; cleaning), then "not just a filter: made in Japan for 50 years, recognised
  there as a household medical device".
- A "1/5 … 5/5" counter badge, so viewers stay for all five, and yellow keyword captions.
- The call to action fits WhatsApp: "reply DEMO to this status" (a green reply bubble), with no phone number needed.

Files: `beats.txt` (script), `align_r6.py` (ElevenLabs take timed with edge-tts word proportions and line breaks
matched by dynamic programming, pauses capped), `build.py`, `style.css`, `mix.py` (Mixkit "Discover" at 0:30,
ducked under the voice), and `CREDITS.txt`. The facts come from Enagic's own guidance on the five waters and the Japan
facts used in earlier reels; there are no cure claims.
