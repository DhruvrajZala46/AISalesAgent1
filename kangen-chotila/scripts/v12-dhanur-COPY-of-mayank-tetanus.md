## Facts (checked)
Tetanus comes from Clostridium tetani spores in soil, not from rust; the spores resist boiling; the bacterium
cannot live in oxygen; the toxin's lethal dose is about 2.5 nanograms per kilo of body weight; spasms can break
bones; about 1 patient in 10 dies even with treatment; having tetanus gives no immunity; the vaccine is the
inactivated toxin (CDC, WHO).
