# Reel 5 (version 2): Hindi, 88 s, fits one WhatsApp status (90 s limit)

For the small-town man who sits at his shop all day. It is built on Mayank's tetanus Short, shot by shot
(`../research/mayank-tetanus-shot-by-shot.md`). Voice: Microsoft hi-IN-MadhurNeural at +36% (3.1 words a
second; Mayank speaks 3.2). Gaps between lines are 0.22-0.4 s; the whole voice runs 88.4 s.

## Persuasion techniques used (all borrowed, none invented)
- **Call-out hook + "enter the conversation already in their mind"** (Robert Collier): the belief that
  grandfathers were stronger is the hook, not a fight with it.
- **Pattern interrupt + open loop** (Zeigarnik): "एक चीज़ और बदली है…" holds the answer back for 4 s.
- **Problem, Agitate, Solution** (Mayank's order and the classic copywriting formula): Kangen appears only after
  the loss is felt.
- **Loss aversion** (Kahneman): "जो दिल को चाहिए, वही रोज़ नाली में"; "पूरा घर रुक जाता है".
- **Authority** (Cialdini): WHO, Japan's health ministry and the scientists' finding are pinned on screen as cards.
- **Dream outcome** (Hormozi's value equation): grandfather's mineral-rich water with today's cleanliness, for the whole family.
- **Callback + question** (Mayank): back to the shopkeeper and the RO, then "which water comes to your home?".
- **Reciprocity + risk reversal + demonstration**: we come to your home, test your own water, free.

## Facts and sources
- RO removes dissolved minerals with the dirt: NGT expert committee (2019); WHO, Kozisek, "Health risks from
  drinking demineralised water" (2005).
- More magnesium in drinking water, fewer heart-disease deaths (ecological studies across many countries): WHO,
  "Calcium and magnesium in drinking-water: public health significance" (2009).
- The reject stream of an RO carries the removed minerals down the drain (how RO works).
- Hydrogen selectively reduces the most harmful radicals: Ohsawa et al., Nature Medicine 2007 (cells and animals).
- Japan's MHLW lists alkaline ionizers as household medical devices; Enagic was founded in Japan in 1974.
- No disease or cure claims for Kangen: it is named for clean water that keeps its minerals and carries hydrogen.
