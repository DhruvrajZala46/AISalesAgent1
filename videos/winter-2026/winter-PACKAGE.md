## How to run the test

1. Upload the video with the title above.
2. In YouTube Studio on desktop, open the video → **Thumbnail → Test & compare** → upload A, B
   and C.
3. Leave it running. YouTube splits impressions between the three and picks the winner by
   **watch time share**, not just clicks. That usually takes a few days to two weeks.
4. Don't change the title while the test runs, or you won't know what made the difference.

## Round 2 — three new thumbnails built on the niche's proven outlier patterns

I ranked 1,371 videos from 36 niche channels by how far each beat **its own channel's usual views**
(the `yt-viral` method) and studied the 48 biggest long-form outliers (2.5× to 22×). What they share:

1. **A face looking at the viewer, cut out with a white outline**, over a soft or collage background
   (Julianna Lee "elegant / classy" 13×, "effortlessly feminine" 12×).
2. **The actual products floating around a person** ("girls STOP buying this." 9×).
3. **Wrong vs right with ✕ / ✓** ("Avoid These COMBINATIONS" 6×, hairstyle ✕/✓ 9×, "NO!" 7×).
4. **A short spoken statement, not a title** ("do less. look better." 8×,
   "this makes you prettier" 9×).
5. **Mood-board collages** ("Autumn Trends" 15×, "15 Habits That Make You Look Expensive" 8×).
