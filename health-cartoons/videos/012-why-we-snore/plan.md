## Facts (Mayo Clinic, "Snoring")
- Snoring is relaxed throat tissue vibrating as air passes. A narrower airway makes the airflow more forceful and the snoring louder.
- It is worse when sleeping on the back, because gravity narrows the airway.
- It is also worse with alcohol (it relaxes the throat muscles), a long soft palate, big tonsils, a cold or allergies, and extra weight.
- Not every snorer has obstructive sleep apnea. See a doctor for snoring with:
  - breathing pauses someone has seen;
  - gasping or choking at night;
  - daytime sleepiness or morning headaches.
