**Medical facts (checked):** the large intestine absorbs water from food waste; when stool moves slowly or the colon absorbs too much
water, stool becomes hard and dry (NIDDK). Too little fiber and not drinking enough fluids are listed causes (Mayo Clinic, NIDDK); fiber
holds water and keeps stool soft. Soda does count as fluid, but its sugar makes it a poor swap for water. We do NOT repeat the reference's
claim that "soda makes digestion much harder" (no good source).
Sources: NIDDK "Symptoms & Causes of Constipation"; Mayo Clinic "Constipation - Symptoms and causes".
