**Medical facts (checked):**
- A pill swallowed with little or no water, or right before lying down, can stick in the food pipe, dissolve there and injure the
  lining (pill-induced esophagitis).
- Symptoms: pain on swallowing, burning behind the breastbone.
- Prevention: take pills upright with a full glass of water and stay upright afterwards. Sources give 10-30 minutes; we say 30.
- Most cases heal within days once the pill is stopped.
- Sources: Banner Health, Poison Control (poison.org), Pharmacy Times, PMC3099275.
