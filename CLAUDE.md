# The Classy Elegance - YouTube videos

This repo makes videos for the YouTube channel The Classy Elegance (calm, kind, timeless elegance for
women of every age). Everything lives in `classy-academy-test/`.

Before any work on a video, read:
1. `classy-academy-test/workflow/GOLD-STANDARD.md` - how the user's favourite video
   ("8 Dressing Rules Every Woman Should Know") was made: the recipe and the quality bar every
   new video must meet or beat.
2. `classy-academy-test/workflow/README.md` - the five stages, the rules that never change, the tools.
3. `classy-academy-test/voice.md` - how the channel talks and teaches.
4. `classy-academy-test/workflow/LEARNINGS.md` - every mistake that reached the user and the check that stops it.

The user's way of thinking: copy what is proven (numbers from the winners and from our own viewers),
never guess; real footage only, never AI; every tip proven, told in the channel's own voice; the user
approves each stage ("ship it, or change it?").

Never commit media, keys or cookies. Text goes under `classy-academy-test/videos/<slug>/`.
