# Two projects live in this repo

## A. Health-cartoon Shorts (`health-cartoons/`) - the CURRENT project (Nov 2026)

English "talking organs + doctor" cartoon Shorts, drawn and animated entirely in code.
**New chat about these videos? Read `health-cartoons/README.md`, then `health-cartoons/PLAYBOOK.md` (section 0 first) and `health-cartoons/LEARNINGS.md` FIRST.**
The standard is `health-cartoons/videos/003-food-up-nose/` (built on the 001 engine; the user called 001 "mind blowing").
After 003 the user said: **"Save this standard. Every new video: SAME effort or MORE, in editing and scripting. Never forget it."**
So every video runs PLAYBOOK section 0 in full:
- data-picked topic;
- re-study the references;
- a script measured against their transcripts and approved before spending credits;
- 2 voice takes picked by measurement;
- an acting timeline, frame checks, full QA;
- an upload block, then save everything.

003's measured numbers are the floor. Start every new video by copying the 003 folder.
The user writes in Hinglish; reply in simple Hinglish. Be honest about quality (measured, never "99%" claims).
Never commit media, keys or cookies; `health-cartoons/tools/fetch_assets.sh` and each `script.md` let you re-create the media.

## B. The Classy Elegance - YouTube videos

This repo also makes videos for the YouTube channel The Classy Elegance (calm, kind, timeless elegance for
women of every age). Everything lives in `classy-academy-test/`.

**New chat? Read `classy-academy-test/HANDOFF.md` FIRST** - the state of the work, the user's last (rejected) video and the open task.

Before any work on a video, read:
0. `classy-academy-test/CHANNEL-DNA.md` - the creator's own thinking, teaching, scripting and content DNA,
   built from her videos (and `CHANNEL-DNA-diagnosis.md`: why the last video missed), with
   `VISUAL-EDITING-DNA.md` (how her videos look, cut and sound, and where her footage comes from) and
   `workflow/ASSET-RESEARCH-GUIDE.md` + `workflow/SOURCES-LOG-TEMPLATE.md` (every clip researched and its rights
   logged). They override the files below.
1. `classy-academy-test/workflow/GOLD-STANDARD.md` - how the user's favourite video
   ("8 Dressing Rules Every Woman Should Know") was made: the recipe and the quality bar every
   new video must meet or beat.
2. `classy-academy-test/workflow/README.md` - the five stages, the rules that never change, the tools.
3. `classy-academy-test/voice.md` - how the channel talks and teaches.
4. `classy-academy-test/workflow/LEARNINGS.md` - every mistake that reached the user and the check that stops it.

The user's way of thinking: copy what is proven (numbers from the winners and from our own viewers),
never guess; real footage only, never AI; every tip proven, told in the channel's own voice; the user
approves each stage ("ship it, or change it?").

Never commit media, keys or cookies. Text goes under `classy-academy-test/videos/<slug>/`.
