# Classy Academy visual storytelling — rules learned from 6 videos (2 full, 4 script+storyboard)

## The core idea
Every sentence answers: "with the sound off, what does she SEE that teaches the tip?"
The visual is the literal noun/claim being spoken, shown on a real elegant woman, and it stays
long enough to be understood (median hold 4-5 s, almost never < 1.5 s).

## Openings (what my versions got wrong)
- Hook = CINEMATIC, HORIZONTAL, FULL-BLEED footage: films / celebrities / editorial campaigns
  (Audrey Hepburn, Grace Kelly, Kate Middleton, Marilyn, old Hollywood, a woman in a hat walking a
  European street). Not vertical TikToks side by side. It feels like a film, not a feed.
- Title: "Episode N" small serif + title in big white script, laid over a full-bleed cinematic shot.
- Authority: when the script mentions elegant women / history, show REAL iconic elegant women.

## Teaching devices (per point)
1. White number card (script wipe) -> full-screen or centred clip.
2. Problem shown literally (the actual bad item, on several women, one panel per beat).
3. Principle line as serif text with one script word, on the caramel backdrop.
4. "Instead / Try this / the secret..." small script hint on backdrop BEFORE the fix.
5. PRODUCT CUTOUTS: when an item is named (loafers, belt, bag, trousers, boots, sandal, tee),
   a clean product photo with no background floats on the caramel backdrop — alone, or centred
   between two women wearing it — and items ACCUMULATE into a lineup (shoe lineup, trouser
   lineup, capsule-wardrobe grid built item by item). Optional price tag ($40 vs $1,400).
6. Recipes: "~ Classic blazer ~ Modern sneakers ~" written between two panels.
7. Before/after headers above two clips: "Cared for / Uncared for".
8. Step-by-step: a real TikTok tutorial in one centred panel, kept for the whole step sequence.
9. Detail close-ups (texture, earring, ring, fabric) for the exact detail named.
10. Diversity: different ages, sizes, skin tones in panel groups.

## Rhythm
- Voice ~125-130 words/min overall (mine was 158 -> too fast).
- One layout lives for a whole sentence; panels BUILD inside it, then everything dissolves.
- Holds < 1.5 s: theirs 7-16%, mine 37% -> must get under ~12%.
- No clip used twice in the whole video. New footage keeps arriving.

## Sound
- Warm, full, continuous music bed; never drops to silence. Soft accents on transitions.

## Ending
- "Part two?" + like/comment -> dreamy faded clip + "Check the description for / Elegant women
  style guide" -> white screen + CA logo.
