# Lessons from the user (The Classy Elegance), winter video, Oct 2026

Every point below came from the user's own feedback on a draft. Treat each one as a hard rule.

## Visuals
- **"Each clip has to be there intentionally and earn its place."** Clips picked by keyword
  ("boots", "pilled") felt random. Pick by asking what the viewer must see to learn this line with
  the sound off.
- **Real before → after** wherever a tip claims a result (pilled knit → shaved; fleece → coat;
  flat black → textured black).
- **Never AI clips.** Pinterest, TikTok and Instagram only, credited. The user has never used AI
  footage and never will.
- **No clip repeats** anywhere in the video. New clips keep coming.
- Real, elegant, old-money women, and a mix of ages and skin tones.
- The opening matters most: cinematic full-screen walks of elegant women, real icons (the Princess
  of Wales, Diana) when the script talks about elegant women.
- Product cut-outs floating on the caramel backdrop as each piece is named. The user responded
  strongly to these.

## Pace, motion, feel
- v3 felt "too fast and cheap": 158 wpm and 37% of shots under 1.5 s. Fixed with about 126 wpm
  (Kokoro af_heart at speed 0.80) and one layout per sentence that builds inside itself.
- Smooth: expo.out grows (1.0-1.3 s), 0.55 s fades, slow drift on every clip.

## Sound
- "Background music not hearable": the music ran out at 5:00 (a single 5-minute track) and sat too
  low. Now the tracks are crossfaded through the full length, about 11 dB under the voice with
  lifts in the pauses, and mastered to -14 LUFS.
- "Sound effects should be more engaging": soft but clearly audible (+7 dB): clicks, sparkles,
  whooshes, pops, chime.

## Brand
- The channel is now **The Classy Elegance**. Use the **CE logo** (white top-left during the
  video, dark on the white end card). Never the old CA logo.

## Process
- Study the channel's own top videos line by line before editing a new style. Don't guess the
  style.
- The user wants **finished, full videos** and a **full-quality 1080p download link** (Gofile),
  plus credits.
- Give live time estimates on request: frame N of M and frames per minute → minutes left.
- Ideas must come from the yt-viral data method, not from guesses (theirs or ours).
