# How Classy Academy videos are edited — measured breakdown

Source: "8 Things Elegant Ladies Never Wear" (1.2M views), first 2:00 frame-by-frame (1 fps + 10 fps on
every transition), audio measured; cross-checked against "6 Old Money Wardrobe Essentials" (newest).
Canvas 1280x720 (published 720p/1080p), 30 fps. Average shot ~6 s.

## 1. The opening (0:00 - 0:46)

| Time | What you hear | What you see | How it moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:08 | "Have you ever seen a woman walk by…" | Full-screen **video** (not photo) of a woman walking; 2nd clip on "powerful" | **Fade in from black, 1.0 s**. Cut on sentence boundary |
| 0:08-0:19 | "no flashy outfit, no designer overload…" | **Vertical** street-style video, centred on the blurred backdrop | Short dissolve |

## 2. The loop you repeat for every point (≈35-45 s each)

1. **Number card** — white screen, black script "1. Loud oversized logos". Soft left→right wipe
   ~1.2 s, holds ~3 s, **fades out 0.5 s**, hard cut to the blurred backdrop.
2. **The problem, shown literally** — full-height photos packed edge-to-edge, **one panel per beat of
   the sentence**. Logos → Gucci-logo outfits. Neon → neon outfits. Each panel **grows from its
   top-left corner (≈55% → 100%) while fading in, 0.6-0.8 s**.
3. **The principle as text** — your key line on the blurred backdrop: white serif with ONE word in
   script ("True elegance *whispers*" / "It doesn't shout"), soft wipe 1.5 s. Or as a quote beside a
   vertical clip ("Elegant style *works with you* / not against you").
4. **The swap ("Instead…")** — one centred vertical try-on video **grows from the centre**, then 3
   panels of the *good* examples. Problem = loud/bright images, swap = cream/white/neutral images.
5. **Lists** — one panel appears **on each spoken item** (beige, sage, navy, ivory, rose, chocolate →
   6 panels, each in that colour).
6. **Exit** — dissolve into one full-screen cinematic clip, then **fade to white** → next number card.

## 3. Rules that make it easy to follow

- **Audio = visual, word by word.** A new image lands on the noun being said.
- **Bad first, good second.** The viewer *sees* the mistake, then the fix.
- **White = new chapter. Backdrop = explanation. Full-screen video = emotion.**
- **Text only for the line you want remembered** — never full captions.
- **Women of different ages, sizes and skin tones in almost every group of panels** (viewers thank you for it).
- **CA logo top-left (x45-130, y44-113) on every non-white screen.**

## 4. Look

- Backdrop: warm caramel blur (base #C38D62, lighter #DBA778, deeper #A8673E), drifting slowly.
- Script font: closest free match **Pinyon Script**. Serif: closest free match **Playfair Display**.
- Card text ≈ 640 px wide, centred. Title script ≈ 1,130 px wide.

## 5. Sound

- **One** soft, slow instrumental bed (≈55 bpm, mostly low/mid frequencies — piano/ambient feel).
- Music sits **≈18 dB under the voice**, steady (no pumping). Starts with the voice, ends in silence.
- **No whooshes, dings or risers** — the gaps at transitions measure −36 to −42 dBFS (music only).
- Voice: calm, slow, soft female; ~150 words/min.

## 6. Where the visuals come from

Pinterest / Instagram street-style photos, TikTok / Instagram try-on videos (vertical; some still show
the creator's on-screen text), and AI-animated vintage portraits. **These are other people's
copyrighted images.** For new videos I'll use licensed Pexels / Pixabay media in exactly this layout.

## 7. Your newer "Lookbook" style (newest video)

Flat taupe (#C4B0A3) or chocolate (#2E241F) background instead of the blur; panels with gaps around
them; number titles in white script over a full-screen clip ("1. Sweaters"); product name lower
right; "SHOP THE LOOKS ↓" card; cold open with a script word over a clip ("Fall is Here").
